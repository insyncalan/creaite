creaite X.Y.Z · first day

Claude drafts. You approve. creaite keeps the record.

creaite reads your meetings, mail and Teams, proposes the actions, decisions, risks and issues it finds, and keeps them in one project record once you approve. Everything stays in one folder on your laptop.

The loop

Every piece of work follows the same five steps. Only step 3 needs you, and nothing reaches your project record without it.

1 · IN

Material arrives

  • Meetings in your calendar, on Intake (Process)
  • Transcripts and minutes you add to Intake (Add files, or drop them on the page)
  • Notes from a call or your own thinking (a note, ⌘⇧N), turned into items with Find updates
  • The daily mail and Teams check
2 · PROPOSE

Claude drafts

Draft minutes, plus suggested new items and updates to open ones, each citing its source. It matches every item to a project and owner.

3 · YOU

Review

On Intake, approve, edit or reject each suggestion. Routine items can be approved together (Approve routine); minutes, new decisions and new risks are approved one at a time.

The only gate
4 · RECORD

Save

Save writes what you approved to your project record and files the minutes in the project folder. Every save can be undone.

5 · OUT

See and report

The dashboard updates at once. Weekly and client reports are drafted from your project record as creaite pages, and you approve them before they're filed.

The next meeting starts the loop again. When a meeting discusses an item that's still open, Claude proposes an update to it, never a duplicate.

Once

Your first hour

About an hour, most of it telling creaite about your projects and the people on them.

1

Install

Download creaite-X.Y.Z.zip from the release, not "Source code". Run python3 ~/Downloads/creaite-X.Y.Z.zip and choose a folder outside OneDrive and iCloud.

2

Say "setup"

Open the folder in Claude Code, connect Microsoft 365, and say setup. It asks before each step: git, your name and role, projects, people, a memory file for each project, and the creaite launcher (say yes).

3

Open creaite

double-click the creaite app in your Applications folder (or creaite.command in your creaite folder). It starts creaite and opens creaite in its own Chrome window. If you have no launcher yet: in Terminal, system/scripts/run live_server.py, then open the address it prints in Chrome or Edge, not Safari.

4

First check

Let setup run the first check over the last 14 days, or press Run the first check on Today. Then open Intake and approve what's right. Until set-up is done, Today shows a short checklist of these steps, each with its button. Settings › Health › Set-up runs the health check and rebuilds the views from the page, and says how to do the two things it can't (connect Microsoft 365, make the launcher), with Copy and Check again.

Never bends

Four rules

Local only All project data stays in your creaite folder on your laptop. Never cloud folders, never shared.
You approve every write Claude proposes; nothing reaches your project record until you approve and save. Scheduled runs only propose.
A source on every row Every item names the meeting, email or chat it came from, so you can always check it.
Client-safe by default Client reports show only what you've marked client-visible. Confidential material stays out.
Dates are shown DD-MM-YYYY. Keep creaite running while you use the dashboard. If it stops, a red alert appears across the top with the command to start it and Check again, and your edits wait until it's back; while it runs there is nothing to see.

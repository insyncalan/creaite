creaite X.Y.Z · first day

Claude drafts. You approve. creaite keeps the record.

creaite reads your meetings, mail and Teams, proposes the actions, decisions, risks and issues it finds, and keeps them in one tracker once you approve. Everything stays in one folder on your laptop.

The loop

Every piece of work follows the same five steps. Only step 3 needs you, and nothing reaches the tracker without it.

1 · IN

Material arrives

  • Meetings picked from your calendar (Intake › Calendar import)
  • Transcripts and minutes you drop in (Upload files)
  • Notes from a call or your own thinking (a note, ⌘⇧N), turned into items with Find updates
  • The daily mail and Teams sweep
2 · PROPOSE

Claude drafts

Draft minutes, plus proposed new items and updates to open ones, each citing its source. It matches every item to a project and owner.

3 · YOU

Review

In Review, approve, edit or reject each proposal. Routine items can be approved in bulk; minutes, new decisions and new risks are approved one at a time.

The only gate
4 · RECORD

Apply

Apply writes what you approved to the tracker and files the minutes in the project folder. Every apply can be undone.

5 · OUT

See and report

The dashboard updates at once. Weekly and client reports are drafted from the tracker as creaite pages, and you approve them before they're filed.

The next meeting starts the loop again. When a meeting discusses an item that's still open, Claude proposes an update to it, never a duplicate.

Once

Your first hour

About an hour, most of it telling creaite about your projects and the people on them.

1

Install

Download creaite-X.Y.Z.zip from the release, not "Source code". Run python3 ~/Downloads/creaite-X.Y.Z.zip and choose a folder outside OneDrive and iCloud.

2

Say "setup"

Open the folder in Claude Code, connect Microsoft 365, and say setup. It asks before each step: git, your name and role, projects, people, a memory file for each project, and the creaite launcher (say yes).

3

Open creaite

double-click the creaite app in your Applications folder (or creaite.command in the vault). It starts the live server and opens creaite in its own Chrome window. If you have no launcher yet: in Terminal, system/scripts/run live_server.py, then open the address it prints in Chrome or Edge, not Safari.

4

First sweep

Let setup run the first sweep over the last 14 days, or press Run the first sweep on Today. Then open Review and approve what's right. Until set-up is done, Today shows a short checklist of these steps, each with its button. Settings › Health › Set-up runs the health check and rebuilds the views from the page, and says how to do the two things it can't (connect Microsoft 365, make the launcher), with Copy and Check again.

Never bends

Four rules

Local only All project data stays in the vault folder on your laptop. Never cloud folders, never shared.
You approve every write Claude proposes; nothing reaches the tracker until you approve and apply. Scheduled runs only propose.
A source on every row Every item names the meeting, email or chat it came from, so you can always check it.
Client-safe by default Client reports show only what you've marked client-visible. Confidential material stays out.
Dates are shown DD-MM-YYYY. Keep the live server running while you use the dashboard. If it stops, a red alert appears across the top with the command to start it and Check again, and your edits wait until it's back; while it runs there is nothing to see.

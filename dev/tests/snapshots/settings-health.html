Restore from a backup

Puts creaite back as it was in a daily backup: your project record, settings, items waiting in Review and history. The current database and every file it replaces are kept first. Project files are never touched.

After every update, creaite does this for you

  • Backs up first, and rolls back if a step fails
  • Makes creaite's launcher again
  • Asks Chrome to refresh its copy of the app's icon
  • Restarts creaite on the new version, and runs the health check

Health check

Dismissed health problems Problems you dismissed in Settings › Health: they stop counting until their detail changes. Default: none. None: every problem counts until you dismiss it. Reset brings them all back.

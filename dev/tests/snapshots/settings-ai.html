AI drafts

A draft is a suggestion, never a save: it fills a field, a proposal in Review or a box for you to edit. creaite picks the facts; Claude only words them. Each needs the live server.

Draft the latest update An action's latest update from what happened since the last one, and Draft updates for actions with no recent update
Draft client wording Plain client messaging from the item's own text, with what was taken out
Suggest the thinking fields A decision's options and reasoning; a risk's response, impact and likelihood
Draft the reason for a health or forecast change One sentence from the evidence creaite already holds
Spot repeats of existing items Marks a proposal that looks like an item already in the tracker; Merge, Keep or Reject in Review
Spot items that are probably done The sweep and the minutes propose closing an open item when a sent mail, reply or meeting shows it finished
Sharpen a vague action A clear, verb-first title for a vague action, beside the original; you pick one
Draft the agenda and invite text From the Prep page: the invite text (POAD or a lighter write-up) and a timed agenda, to copy
Shape the meeting Prep: reads your focus and aim, then drafts the objective, talking points, ask and what's carried over, each line cited
Write the status in other forms Generate's Form: a steering note, a three-line update, an email paragraph or a Teams message
Redraft a report section The weekly or client report's ✦, and Polish, Shorter, Executive or your own words on one section, from the week's facts (a client report: client-visible facts only)
Redraft a minutes section The minutes' ✦, and Polish, Shorter, Executive or your own words on one section (the distributed version: nothing marked Internal only or withheld)
Polish the email wording Draft email's opening paragraph reworded in a warm, neutral or firm tone; the table, subject and addresses are untouched

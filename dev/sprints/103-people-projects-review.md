## The panel today: three sections

| Section | What you can do | Where it is also used |
|---|---|---|
| **People** (the register) | Add a person; edit email, aliases, organisation and role; rename or merge to fix spellings across every item (undoable); hide a name from the owner list; the VIP and remind switches; filters (organisation, project, has an open item, removed) | Every owner picker and filter; the People page and a person's page (their work, chase, Draft email); Essentials › People I follow (the VIP alerts); the right-click menu on a name |
| **Workstreams** | Per project: add, edit lead and keywords, rename or merge (undoable), retire | The Work page (workstream field, filter, group), Review's workstream choice, the peek's Details |
| **Projects and register** | Show archived projects; archive and restore a project (asks about open items); add or close a project and edit matching signals through ask creaite in plain English | The Projects page; each project's own page and its coming full-page settings (Archive, Close this project, People and partners: approved for 2.5, `project-settings-brief.md`) |

## Options

**A. Minimal: People moves out, the rest stays.** The register is edited on the People page and a person's page; Settings keeps Workstreams and Projects.

**B. Recommended: dissolve the category, put each thing next to where it is used.**
- **People register to the People page** (rail) and each person's page: add a person, edit email, aliases, organisation, role, VIP and remind switches, hide, and rename or merge there, where you already see their work. A "Fix spellings" action on the People page holds merge. Settings keeps nothing for it but a link.
- **Projects and register to the Projects page and each project's settings page.** Archive, restore and Close this project already live in the project's full-page settings; the Projects page gets a Show archived switch and a restore on archived rows. Adding a project and matching signals stay plain-English in ask creaite (as today), with an Add project button on the Projects page that opens the same.
- **Workstreams stay in Settings** (Alan, 18-10-2026), as one section; with the category gone it moves under Advanced (or keeps a one-item "Workstreams" category if you prefer). The project's settings page links to it.
- **The category "People and projects" is retired**; Settings becomes Essentials, Advanced and Health. The settings search still finds each item and jumps to its new home.

**C. Everything to the project and person pages.** As B, but Workstreams also move into the project's settings page. This reverses Alan's 18-10 decision to keep Workstreams in Settings, so it is not recommended unless he changes his mind.

## Why B
Each thing sits where you use it (people with their work, projects with their archive), the Settings sidebar loses a category with three unrelated sections, and nothing is removed: every action keeps its undo, and the approved project-settings page already carries Archive and Close. It also answers the open point in the project-settings brief ("whether Settings › People and projects stays or is retired").

## Checks

### Intake and Review (#232)
1. One side-bar entry "Intake and Review" and one badge (meetings ready to process plus proposals to approve); the old Intake and Review addresses open the page.
2. The three stages: Arrived (transcript ready, waiting for the transcript, or being drafted), Drafted (proposals waiting), Approved (all decided this week); Mail and Teams proposals are a separate group, not a stage.
3. The List | Board | Two panes switch in the toolbar; each view works; the choice is remembered per person (browser, not shared); List is the default.
4. A meeting row shows its name, when, project, state, age and Stale marker; actions Process, Later and Not needed (✕, with a reason: Informal chat, No decisions, Duplicate of another meeting, Other; undoable).
5. Process runs in the background with progress on the row; ask creaite does not open and is not blocked.
6. Later takes the meeting out of the ready list into a Later group; it comes back when a transcript arrives, and nothing else brings it back.
7. A single click anywhere on a drafted meeting's row opens it and a second click closes it (Check them and Close do the same).
8. An opened meeting's items hang under it on a coloured rail; each group heading starts with the meeting's name; the draft minutes are the first row of "Needs your decision".
9. Two groups: "Needs your decision" (approved one by one: minutes, reports, new decisions, new risks, health changes, closing updates and the rest of the one-at-a-time list) and "Routine" (has a project, no conflict, not on that list); "Approve routine (n)" is on the Routine heading and never covers the first group; a guessed field does not make an item non-routine; the heading words are "Each has a project and no conflict, and none is a change you approve one by one."
10. Every proposal has its kind pill (Minutes, Decision, Risk, Issue, Dependency, Action, Update), the emphasised line (owner, date or priority and "approve individually" or "Routine"), the evidence quote with its rail and a source line naming the meeting; Open source opens the whole file (no time jump).
11. Mail and Teams proposals are split the same way and say what changes (an update shows "status Open → Closed"); an update that closes an item is under "Needs your decision".
12. Clicking a Mail or Teams proposal opens it in the peek: editable title; Status (for an update), Owner, Due date, Priority, Project (Impact and Likelihood for a risk); a "What changes" box; the Source quote with Open source; a description; Reject, Cancel, Save and Save and approve; previous and next arrows. Nothing reaches the tracker until approved; Esc closes the peek.
13. An "invalid" proposal is held back in the list (reviewed in production).
14. Upload files stays on the page: dropped files become rows with the same actions.
15. Pill, line and quote sizes: the line is 16 px semibold, the quote 15 px (measured).

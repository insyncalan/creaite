## Checks

### #239 Client output as one checked snapshot
1. Approve a client report → creaite freezes a checked copy of it; the approval is recorded; the Word, HTML and PDF client files are all made from that one copy.
2. A client export made from a page posted with the request: the posted page is never used (the saved, checked copy is).
3. A client report whose file says it is internal (or has no kind) is still treated as a client report.
4. The header, the logos and the audience on a client export come from the project at the time of export, not from the report file.
5. Only the project's own logo files and the report's own pictures can be drawn; a logo or picture path from another project, or a hard-linked file, is not drawn; extra fields in the report are dropped.
6. Words hidden in look-alike letters or escapes (for example a Cyrillic letter inside a hidden item's word) are caught: the export is refused or the item is left out, as for a plain hidden word.
7. Another project's words and owners in a client report are caught in the same way.
8. The minutes export refuses a client report.
9. A typed export name that matches another report's filing name does not overwrite or claim it (it gets a number).
10. A client report built by the older Word route goes through the same checked snapshot.
11. **Live only, with Alan:** export a real client report as Word, HTML and PDF with the real logo → it looks as before and nothing hidden or internal is in it.

### #238 Usage tab on Activity
12. Activity has a Usage tab beside Changes and Quality.
13. With no runs recorded it says "No runs yet", not an empty chart or zeros.
14. From a fixture record of runs: the summary cards, the by-kind table, the 14-day chart (two aligned panels: cost per day, and what each run loads at the start) and the skills list show the right numbers; no text of any message is anywhere.
15. The live server's daily job adds one line a day per always-loaded file to the context-size record (file, bytes, estimated tokens, date; never text); the page only reads it.
16. **Struck unless it lands in 2.5.2:** "Your reviews" (add a dated note; Download as CSV). Planned for 2.5.3 as a small delta (Stark, 20-10-2026).
17. The kind that used to say VIP check says "Alert check" (nothing on the page says VIP).
18. The Usage tab against its locked mockup, light and dark, 1400 and 900 px (judgement, on the screenshot sheet).

### #245 Intake as a quiet line on Today
19. With meetings ready and proposals waiting, Today shows one small line at the foot of Your commitments with the right counts; the old Intake and Review section and its fold are gone.
20. Its links open Intake (the meetings) and Review (the proposals).
21. The x hides the line; it stays hidden across a reload and in a second tab of the same browser, and comes back when a count rises above what it was (something new arrives) or the next morning. The choice is kept per browser; with browser storage blocked it still hides for that page load.
22. With nothing waiting there is no line.
23. The quick Approve and Reject on Today's rows are gone; approving works in Intake and Review.
24. The Messages section sits above Week ahead only when there are messages from people you follow: each card has Make a work item and Dismiss; nothing on the card says who is followed.
25. The line and the Messages cards look right, light and dark, 1400 and 900 px (judgement, against the locked mockup).

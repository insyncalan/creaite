This sheet holds only what a person must see. Setup: a throwaway vault (`dev/tools/handcheck.py`) with fictional data unless a check says "live". Chrome at 1400 px and 900 px (desktop only), light and dark. Items follow `dev/release-notes/2.6.5-plain.md`. 2.6.4b has no guarded change and no chain of its own.

1. **Intake first look.** Intake feels work-first in the list, Board and Two panes views, and the decisions to approve can be reached without scrolling at 900 px.
2. **The waiting tail.** Press Show on "Waiting for the transcript": it is clear what you are looking at, and Hide folds it with no ticks left behind.
3. **A clear Intake.** Clear the day: the next-job button is the job you would do, and the Week ahead offering tomorrow reads right.
4. **People Display menu.** Group by, sub-group, order by with reverse and properties work, and D opens it. Judgement: as good as Work's; hiding Email at 1100 px and below is acceptable.
5. **Bulk save.** Save a bulk change: "Saved N changes" reads calmly and its one Undo puts everything back.
6. **Ask box.** Focus ask creaite in light and dark: the border is plain and nothing turns.
7. **Reading pass.** Walk Today, Intake, Review, Work, People, Files and the cockpit once, looking for crowded or odd wording, or anything too small or too quiet.
8. **Esc and Group by.** Tick meetings, then press Esc in the search box, and change Group by (including None): the ticks clear. Then the expected-failure marks for these come off in Cap's sheet.
9. **Live (a copy of the real vault).** Run one real mail check from Check now and one real ask; Usage still counts both.
10. **A confidential meeting's Prep.** Use a calendar meeting marked confidential whose title carries a project code or name (for example "[DEF] Redundancy talks"), with no project chosen for it yet. Open its Prep: the header may show a suggested project, but **Open prep page** puts the page in the portfolio Agendas folder (not in that project's folder), no one-pager button names a project, and Draft agenda and Shape this meeting work on "No project". Now pick the project yourself in the page's project chip: the page goes to that project's folder (and the one-pager buttons return if it is a steering meeting), and after a reload the choice is still there. Then open "Note for this meeting" for the same meeting: it does **not** open with the guessed project already selected. Look at one normal (not confidential) meeting with the same title: it still gets its project on its own. Judgement: the Prep popup may still show the suggested project's name and its items on screen, but nothing is filed or sent under it; nothing about the confidential meeting, its title or its project appears in the Intake list rows, the hub or Close the day beyond what the day view already shows.

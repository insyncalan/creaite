Everything scriptable is automated in `dev/tests/handchecks/test_v264.py` (27 checks: the design measures, the plain-words walk, the figure check, the fingerprint on every deciding route, the run engine's set-up, the weekly budget gate and the profile card) and in the release gate. This sheet holds only what a person must see or do. DRAFT: written from the issue texts and Team Cap's findings of 24-10-2026 while `dev/release-notes/2.6.4-plain.md` was not posted; check each item against the plain notes when they are. Setup: a throwaway vault (`dev/tools/handcheck.py`) with fictional data unless a check says "live". Chrome at 1400 px and 900 px (desktop only), light and dark. Items refer to `dev/release-notes/2.6.4-plain.md`.

## Design (#337)

1. **Settings at 900 px.** Open Settings › Essentials and Work. The Weekend, "What the check reads" and "Week ahead shows" rows keep a label you can read (more than one word wide), with the tick boxes beside or below it. Same at 1400 px.
2. **People at 900 px and 1400 px.** The table fits its box with no sideways scroll; Overdue, the column it sorts by, is on screen; press Overdue and the order changes. Words are not cut in the middle ("Sponsor" is never "Spons / or"). Judgement for Alan: at 1100 px and below the Email column is hidden (the address is in the person's record): acceptable?
3. **Cockpit.** The edit pencils are easy to see (not faint) in light and dark; the small text on the charts is readable; hovering or tabbing to a pencil still shows it.
4. **Client view with nothing shown.** On a project whose milestones are all internal, switch to Client view on Milestones and on the cockpit card: the card says how many milestones are not shown and that Internal view shows them, and offers Add milestone; it never says "0 achieved, 0 to come". With ONE such milestone the wording must say it is not shown (Team Cap's H1 found the opposite).
5. **Activity at 900 px.** "Rejected in review" stays on one line in the By column and the rows are not taller than they need to be.
6. **Goldens (Alan).** The snapshot goldens for the cockpit and People changed: sign them off before the cut.

## Plain words (#292, #293): Alan's own walk

7. **Walk Today, Intake, Review, Work, Notes, Settings (every tab), Health, Reports and the milestone and project pages, in light and dark.** No file path on screen (nothing like `projects/…`, `system/…`, `.py`, `.json`, `.db`); no script name and no "sweep"; none of proposal, staged, apply (as the app's own word), tracker, vault, live server in any label, button, toast, empty state or message. Known on the head: a hover title on the portfolio folder in Files, the folder path on a project's Settings page and the detail lines of two Health checks still show a path or a command (check 26 of the automated checks).
8. **Your own change reads "Saved" with Undo; suggestions "wait for you in Review".** Edit an item: the header does not say "Saved for Review" while the bar says "Nothing is saved until you press Save" (Team Cap's H2). Approve a suggestion and undo it.
9. **Health.** Every title and every Fix it button reads in plain words, including "Your creaite app opens a different copy of creaite". "Paste into Terminal" appears only in the stopped alert and Settings › Diagnostics.
10. **Ask creaite.** Ask for a mail and Teams check ("check my mail"), a status and a change: the replies use the same plain words (no staged, proposal, tracker, vault, live server). Type the old words (`sweep`, `apply`, `process new minutes`): they still work.
11. **Help.** Read Help from the start. The rewritten lines and the chat command wording use the same words as the screens; the command table teaches the plain commands as well as the old ones. Judgement: does it read plainly in British English?
12. **Nothing else changed.** One example each: approve an item and undo it, open Health and run a Fix it, press Check now on Today, open Search everything on Files.

## Weekly budget for creaite's AI runs (#334)

19. **Settings.** Find the weekly budget in Settings › Advanced (creaite and ask creaite): dollars or tokens, a plain explanation, a sensible default, and "confirm" or "stop" at the limit. Change it and see Usage change.
20. **Usage.** The Usage tab shows used of the budget and the date the week ends (a Sunday; the week starts again on Monday), with the bar in the design system's colours. Counts and dates only.
21. **Live: 80% and 100%.** With a small budget and a real run: after a run past 80% a heads-up line appears in the chat; past 100% a typed message shows the Weekly AI budget card with Run anyway and Cancel (confirm), or says plainly to raise the budget (stop). The buttons in the dashboard (Review, Save, Undo) keep working.
22. **Live: the real model.** Run a real ask and check that its cost lands in Usage and in the week's total.

## Your profile (#348)

23. **A colleague's name.** In Settings › Essentials › Your profile set the name to a colleague in the people register: the card does not show their email, organisation or role as yours and offers Add my details. Press it: it asks "Is that row you?" before it edits their row; answer yes and the card then shows it.

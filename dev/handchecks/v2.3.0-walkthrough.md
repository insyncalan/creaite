# v2.3.0: your walk-through

About 16 steps in one sitting, in plain words, on sample data (a throwaway vault, never your real one, never Microsoft 365), in Chrome, **creaite and Cursor themes**, light and dark. Each step says what to do, what you should see, and the matching check in `v2.3.0.md`.

Mockups you approved are in `dev/mockups/`: `topbar-hide-mockup.html`, `rail-icon-size-mockup.html`, `files-navigation-v2-mockup.html`.

1. **Top bar.** On Today, scroll down. *Expect:* the top bar goes; back at the very top it returns. Try Work, Files and a report. (Check 1)
2. **Weekly report.** Open a weekly report, scroll down, press a button on the slim bar. *Expect:* the full header does not come back. (Check 1)
3. **Icon size.** Open Customise sidebar, pick Large, then Small. *Expect:* icons grow and shrink, stay sharp, the rail widens a little; your order and hidden pages are as they were; a reload keeps the choice. (Check 2)
4. **Files.** Open Files. *Expect:* four pills with the Filter button beside them; the Filter opens the bar; the date opens a popup calendar. (Check 3)
5. **Today's line.** With things waiting in Intake and Review, look at Today. *Expect:* one quiet line, no list. (Check 4)
6. **Late sweep.** After 07:00 with no sweep yet today, open Today. *Expect:* "Sweep due since 07:00" and a Run sweep button; press it and the banner clears. (Check 5)
7. **Note menu.** Right-click a note in the Notes list. *Expect:* the same menu as an open note, without opening it. (Check 6)
8. **Choose a project.** Open Cockpit and Milestones with no project chosen. *Expect:* "Choose a project", not a blank page. (Check 7)
9. **People.** *Expect:* one kind of zero, counts right-aligned. (Check 8)
10. **Activity.** *Expect:* "You, by hand" on your own changes, even rows, a tick box on every row. (Check 9)
11. **Draft email.** In Review, find both items. *Expect:* each reads "Draft email". Make a draft, press "I sent it". *Expect:* every sent item updates and the email stays internal. (Checks 10, 11)
12. **A person's name.** Right-click one. *Expect:* Draft email for that person. (Check 12)
13. **Sizes.** Look across Work, Settings and Review in light and dark. *Expect:* fields, chips and headings feel the same everywhere. (Check 13)

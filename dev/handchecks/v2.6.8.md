# Hand checks for creaite 2.6.8 (judgement and live-only)

Scriptable checks are in the tests (`test_invite_clean_*`, `test_calendar_*`, `test_live_rescrub_369`). This sheet holds only what a person must see. Use fictional data.


1. **Agenda survival (#369).** Save a few realistic invites to a throwaway calendar and look at what Today and Prep show. Use agendas with lines such as "Code of conduct", "Annual refresher", "Password reset rollout", "Token refresh", "Secret santa", "Pin the roadmap", "Zip code lookup", "Lunch at 12:30", "Room 4", "Item 12", "Q3 forecast". Judgement: how many real agenda lines are lost? Known cost: lines that start with a label word followed by words off the short lists ("Pin the roadmap", "Zip code lookup", "Password reset rollout") are removed (the reviewer measured 48% of 58 lines before option B). Accepted for 2.6.8 because it fails closed; E1 (2.6.9) replaces the label approach. Under a heading like "Password reset" or "Code review", word-only agenda lines must stay. Times, dates, room numbers, "Item 12" and "Q3 forecast" must always survive.
2. **No value survives.** "Meeting ID." or "Password reset" followed by a number, ID or bulleted/numbered number ("- 4417", "1. 987 654 321", "ZQ7742", "hunter2") shows none of them. Known limit: a plain word typed right under a soft heading ("Password reset" then "hunter") stays; under "Passcode:" or "PIN" it goes.
3. **Glued labels.** "PIN4417", "Passcode4417", "Meeting ID123456" in a title or body are gone; "ID badges" stays. An organiser called pin.sharma@ or key@ keeps their address.
4. **bare20 glued labels and Key.** In a throwaway invite, "Token4417", "Secret4417", "PINZQ7742", "Zoom IDH2K7" and "API key987 654 321" are gone; "Tokenise", "Secretary 2026", "Pinot" and "ID badges" stay. A bulleted "Key" followed by a bullet with a value removes both; "Key milestones" stays. Known limits: a two-character code such as "X9" under a soft heading stays; "Key" unmarked then "- hunter" stays.
5. **Sent lines in client text.** Draft a client report or an email whose text contains "chased ABC by EMAIL", the same with a non-breaking hyphen, and a line with the name split over two lines. Each sent line is removed from the client text and the email body; the rest of the field keeps its accents and characters.
6. **Glued titles (bare20 cost).** Look at a few meeting titles with a word glued to a year or number (Pinterest2026, Keynote2025, Token2049 conference, Code2040 event, ID123 badge). They show as "Meeting"; this is expected in 2.6.8 (fails closed; E1 in 2.6.9 replaces the label approach). Product names such as iPhone15, Win11, Python3, ISO27001 and GPT4 stay.
7. **bare21 qualified labels and dates.** In a throwaway invite, "Passcode for dial-in:" / "4417", "Meeting ID (Teams)" / "987 654 321", "PIN to join:" / "4417" and "Host key (organiser only)" / "ZQ7742" show no value (the label line and its value are gone). After "Passcode:" a date goes. Known limits: a date typed as a passcode right under a soft heading stays; a sentence ending "... Password for the portal" removes the next line.

Known costs: "Password for the portal", "PIN for the card reader", "Token for the build" and "Code of conduct for suppliers" remove the next one to three lines; "Meeting ID for the quarterly review" also removes a "Date: 12-11-2026" line below it; "Bring your staff ID." followed by "Review on 12-11-2026" removes that line.

"Password for new starters" followed by "IT will issue on day one" keeps the sentence. A "- Key" legend ("- Red: blocked") keeps every line. "Password reset rollout" alone is kept; "hunter2" after "Meeting ID." is removed. Known limits: "AOB" or "Budget review" directly after a label with a phrase ("Meeting ID badges", "Password for the portal") is removed; "- Key" then "- ZQ7742" in a list leaks; a header-row label with a long value row can leak (closed by E1 in 2.6.9).

Accepted for 2.6.8 because it fails closed; E1 (2.6.9) replaces the label approach. Under a heading like "Password reset" or "Code review", word-only agenda lines must stay. Times, dates, room numbers, "Item 12" and "Q3 forecast" must always survive.

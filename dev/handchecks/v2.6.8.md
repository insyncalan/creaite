# Hand checks for creaite 2.6.8 (judgement and live-only)

Everything scriptable is in `dev/tests/handchecks/test_v268.py` (notes, guides, version) and in the invite and sent-line tests of the branches and the gate. This sheet holds only what a person must see. Setup: a throwaway vault (`dev/tools/handcheck.py`) with fictional data unless a check says "live". Chrome at 1400 px and 900 px (desktop only). Items follow `dev/release-notes/2.6.8-plain.md`.

1. **Invite scrub, live.** In a copy of a real calendar, make a test invite titled "Password swordfish" and another titled "Password reset policy". The first shows as "Meeting" in Today, Intake and Prep and its word is gone from the saved calendar; the second keeps its title.
2. **Ordinary titles.** Walk Today and Intake for a week of real titles. Judgement: does anything ordinary now read "Meeting" (for example "Code sprint" or "Secret Santa party")? Is the trade-off acceptable?
3. **The line after a label.** Open an invite whose text has the line "Password reset" followed by several ordinary lines. The next three lines with text are gone and the fourth stays. Judgement: is the loss acceptable?
4. **Old calendar cleaned.** Start a session on a vault whose saved calendar came from an earlier version and holds a "Password swordfish" title: Today and Prep already show "Meeting" when read.
5. **Sent lines in client text.** Draft a client report or an email whose text contains "chased ABC by EMAIL", the same with a non-breaking hyphen, and a line with the name split over two lines. Each sent line is removed from the client text and the email body; the rest of the field keeps its accents and characters.
6. **Nothing else changed.** One example each: approve an item and undo it, press Check now on Today, open Files, open Help and read What's new for 2.6.8.

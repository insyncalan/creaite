- **Live server.** Port 8971 (`CREAITE_PORT`; the default 8765 was in use), `CREAITE_NOW=2026-10-13 09:30`, and `CREAITE_CLAUDE` pointed at a stand-in Claude: `dev/tests/drafts_fake.py`'s replies plus a few ask creaite replies (a slow one, a question, a `CHANGE:` card). No model was called and nothing connected to Microsoft 365, so the draft wording in the screenshots is the stand-in's.
- **Browser.** One headless Chrome, driven by `dev/tools/cdp.py`, with the page's clock pinned to 09:30 (so a chat's own times show 09:30 beside the server's real times). Real clicks and keys wherever practical. Command-line output is shown as terminal-style screenshots.

Those four errors in 21a's Diagnostics come from the script, not from creaite. The page was reloaded and the check was redone.
- **Skipped.** Steps that need Microsoft 365, a real ask creaite model run, Claude Code itself, the double-click launcher or a pull request are marked **needs Alan**.

## Failures

### 3. After Undo, the same change can't be approved from the card again

- **What happened:** "Mark FAL-A-004 done" showed one card (Status Open → Closed). **Approve** applied it ("Applied: FAL-A-004", the item Closed), and **Undo** put it back to Open. But Undo also returned the change to Review as a pending proposal (`proposed-…-edit.json`, `source_run: ask creaite`). Asking again gave a card that said "FAL-A-004 already has a change waiting in Review, so this one can only be staged for review". **Reject** on that card then staged nothing (correct), but Review still held the first change.
- **What was expected:** "Undo puts it back. Again, and **Reject**: nothing in Review." After Undo, nothing should be left in Review, and the second card should offer Approve again.
- **Likely cause:** `tracker.py undo` returns an undone apply's proposals to staging as pending, which suits Review's own applies but not a change approved on the card.

### 16. Three of the six switches don't grey their control with the reason

- **Fine:** **Draft agenda** (aria-disabled, "Draft the agenda and invite text is off in Settings › General › AI drafts") and **Sharpen** (aria-disabled, with the same kind of reason). **Probably done** off: staging skipped the closing update with "skipped (probably done is off …)".
- **Polish (fail):** with it off, the whole "Polish the wording of the opening paragraph" row disappears from Draft email (16d). Expected: the Polish button greyed, saying why.
- **Forms (fail):** with it off, Generate shows the note "Write the status in other forms is off in Settings › General › AI drafts" under Form. But the four form buttons are neither disabled nor greyed. The remembered "Email paragraph" still shows as pressed while the title says "Generate current state". Clicking Teams message changes the title to "Write a teams message", and **Write it** stays enabled: it only shows the reason in a snack when pressed (16c-forms-off-after-click, 16c-forms-off-write). Expected: the form buttons greyed.
- **Spot repeats (fail):** with it off, staging no longer marks a repeat: the proposal for "Book the UAT testers and the rooms" has no `looks_like` (16g). But Review still shows "Looks like ORX-A-004 (same project, open) · Very likely" on that row, whether the switch is on or off (16h, 28f). Expected: no Looks like with the switch off. The page's own duplicate hint seems to use the new wording, whatever the switch says.

### 24. In "Increase contrast" the dashed draft stays

- **What happened:** with `forced-colors: active` (Windows contrast themes), the dashed copy goes and the word and bar turn CanvasText (24a, last cell). With `prefers-contrast: more` (what Chrome reports for macOS's Increase contrast), the dashed copy stays, only recoloured to `--muted` (24a, "prefers-contrast: more" cell).
- **What was expected:** "In macOS's Increase contrast … the dashed copy goes."
- **Cause:** BL-273's CSS hides `.logo .wmd` only under `@media (forced-colors: active)`, and there is no `prefers-contrast: more` rule for it. Alan should confirm on a real Mac with Increase contrast on, since this was emulated.
- **Fine:** every theme, light and dark, shows the dashed draft, the word and the saffron bar. Retro Arcade and Thermo Scan keep their lettering. At 460 pixels the rail turns to icons (48 px) and the wordmark fits (38 px). Whether 62 pixels and the dashed weight are right is Alan's call.

# Hand checks for creaite 2.6.9 (judgement and live-only)

Scriptable checks are in `dev/tests/handchecks/test_v269.py` and the E1 tests (`test_calendar_*`, `test_invite_blocks`, `test_invite_filter_*`). This sheet holds only what a person must see. Use fictional data in a throwaway calendar.

## Checks

1. **Teams invite with a passcode and a long agenda.** Save one invite with a Teams block (link, meeting ID, passcode, dial-in) between two rows of underscores and a 10-line agenda above it. Open Today, Prep and Draft email for that meeting. The agenda shows; no link, ID, passcode or phone number shows anywhere.
2. **Numbers and times survive.** In that agenda, lines such as "Budget review 12-11-2026", "Milestones 14:00-15:30", "Room 204 booking" and "Item 12" are still there. Known cost (accepted 26-10-2026): ranges like "14.00 - 15.30" or "2026-2027" and long figures like "1.250.000" may go until item A is fixed; note which ones you see.
3. **Prep page.** Press Prep page for the same meeting. The page body shows the agenda and shows no join details (the page is written by the live server from Claude's research, so this is live only).
4. **A body that looks too short.** A meeting body is kept to 1,500 characters, whole lines only. If a long agenda stops early, that is the cap, not a fault: the lines after the cut are not shown. Look at the invite in Outlook for the rest. Say if the cut falls somewhere that hides something you need.
5. **Old calendar, no sweep.** With a calendar saved by 2.6.8 or earlier (rows without the clean stamp), open Today and Prep before any sweep. Bodies already read clean. The file only gets the stamp at the next sweep.
6. **A title with a provider and a long number.** A meeting titled "Zoom licences 100 200" (or "Teams incident 482915", "Chime 12345678") shows as "Meeting": expected in 2.6.9, the scrub takes such a title for a meeting ID. To fix it, rename the invite in Outlook or type the title by hand.
7. **Lines lost by design.** Three lines on the invite fixtures are expected to go (accepted 26-10-2026): a link-only line such as "New to GoTo? ... https://...", the rest of a block after an unclosed script tag that holds a long number, and a bare label such as "Google Meet link" directly above a removed join link (82 of 130 fixture lines are kept; the 2.6.8 scrub keeps 86). Also expected: the scrub drops a few more ordinary lines than 2.6.8 (loss ratchets re-pinned). Judgement: is that acceptable on a real invite?
8. **Load retry wording.** Load a transcript while Microsoft 365 is not reachable (sign the connector out, or use a vault with it off). The message says nothing was loaded and "Press Process to try again"; the page looks once more by itself and, if that fails too, says "Looked again, and Microsoft 365 still could not be reached. Nothing was loaded. Press Process to try again." A load that works shows no such message.
9. **First start-up re-clean.** Start the live server after installing 2.6.9 on a vault with a saved calendar: Today and Prep read clean at once, and the live calendar file is cleaned and stamped at that first start-up (older copies, history snapshots, written Prep pages and backup zips are not re-cleaned: next release only).
10. **Untrack private files, live.** On your own vault, follow `dev/proposals/bundle/MANUAL-CHECK.md` (cx-setup, Git, "Untrack private files"): about five minutes in Terminal; the backup still checks out afterwards.

## Not in 2.6.9

- Digit and mixed passwords, PINs and IDs are held in every form tested; on the 3,024 word-value bodies the filter leaks 255 against 365 for 2.6.8 (digit values 120 -> 0). It does not hold every word-only value: a word after a hard label's second ordinary line (as in 2.6.8), a bare PIN in a table cell and a word after a Unicode line break can show it, where 2.6.8 held the last two.
- A password or PIN that is only a word (no digits) behind a label creaite does not know is not removed.
- Labels in languages not on the scrub's list (for example Swedish, Polish, Turkish) can let a short PIN through when its value sits in its own paragraph.
- A phrase with no label, such as "Use 6802 to join", is not caught.
- An ID split over several lines or groups (412, 889, 330) is not counted as an ID.
- An HTML Google Meet invite can lose its agenda with the join block (no secret leaks).
- Text typed after the last divider of a join block is dropped with it.
- Old calendar rows are not cleaned in one go; they are read clean on every read until the next sweep saves them.

# Hand checks for creaite 2.1.0

Run these once the integration (#75, `release-210`) has every v2.1.0 part merged (P1 to P10), after the v2.0.0 checks (`v2.0.0.md`). Prepared 15-10-2026 by Team Stark from the Done-when of #64 to #73, their comments (Alan's decisions), `dev/sprints/v210-reporting.md`, `dev/sprints/v210-contract.md` and BL-335 to BL-339 in `dev/BACKLOG.md`.

**Items (5):** BL-335 the weekly report, BL-336 the client report, BL-337 the reporting space and the Reports page, BL-338 the minutes, BL-339 the meeting hub retired.

**74 checks:** 62 scriptable, 4 measurable, 5 judgement (on the screenshot sheet) and 3 live only. `python3 dev/tools/handcheck.py v2.1.0` runs every one except the live-only checks, fills the Results table and makes the sheet; the Setup below is what it does by itself, for a hand run. Desktop (1400 px) and 900 px only: no 320 or 640 px checks (Alan, 15-10-2026).

**Alan's decisions these checks hold to:**
- A filed report or minutes is never saved over, and there is no approval card for it: Save on a filed one makes a new draft version, and approving that replaces the filed file in one apply with Undo (#64, #67, 15-10-2026). Checks 15, 45 and 46.
- "File the exports beside it" has two lines: reports off, minutes on (#65). Checks 17 and 58.
- **Client safety:** nothing that is Sensitive, partner-only or not client-visible reaches a client report, its exports or the follow-up email, and nothing Sensitive, partner-only or Internal only reaches the Distributed minutes. A partner-only label only withholds. The client health's reason (`health_diff`) is kept in the draft and never in a filed client file, its data block or an export (#71, 15-10-2026). Checks 20 to 23, 28 to 31, 56 and 57.
- The follow-up email is in the minutes view's Export menu, drafted from the version shown (#70). Check 56.
- Item-source "Meeting" links stay and go through the meeting's Prep, Review or minutes; the Intake and Files links are gone (#73). Checks 67 and 68.

**Sample data only.** The fictional demo portfolio (FAL Falcon Data Platform, ORX Orbit Customer App, MER Meridian Contact Centre AI, TID Tide Billing Migration) and its people. **Never Microsoft 365:** the meeting is a hand-written record, the calendar a hand-written file, and Claude a stand-in. Checks marked **live only** need the live vault or a real model.

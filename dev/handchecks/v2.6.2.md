Never holds the release; run in production. Items refer to `dev/release-notes/2.6.2-plain.md`.

## Live only (real Mac, real Microsoft 365)

1. **Live Outlook draft (#183).** In Settings, switch on the draft choice. In Draft email for a person with an overdue item, press Create draft in Outlook. The draft sits unsent in Outlook Drafts with its table and colours kept (Outlook may strip some formatting). Nothing is sent. With the setting off, the window shows the refusal in words and a Copy button.
2. **The first-run card does not shift Today (real Mac).** Open creaite on a fresh vault: the "About you" and start-on-sign-in cards do not move or overlap the page as it loads.
3. **Your profile, real people register.** Settings › Essentials › Your profile shows your name and your own entry's email, aliases, organisation and role; "Edit my details" changes only your entry; on a vault where you are not in the register it offers "Add my details".

## Judgement (look at it)

4. **Your profile card and the "About you" step.** Wording is plain, the email reads as a label (no check, no access), "Not now" is remembered for that browser, and the step comes once, and always before the start-on-sign-in question (that one can arrive a moment later from the server and is added after About you). Light and dark.
5. **The Draft email window wording** when the setting is off or the draft cannot be made: plain words, a visible Copy button, no paths or script names.
6. **Discard Undo.** Discard held minutes, then Undo: the suggestion is back in Review exactly as it was. If the set-aside file has changed or cannot be read, or a suggestion with that name is already waiting in Intake, the one-line message after "not put back" says why. The first two say "that file"; judge whether that reads plainly enough (a softer wording is a small 2.6.3 change).
7. **The New note drawer.** Dragging the edge feels right; arrow keys step it (bigger with a modifier), End goes to full width, double-click, Home or Enter return it to the default, and "Reset width" appears only when the width differs; the width is remembered after a reload; nothing overlaps at 900 px.
8. **Today with nothing to attend to.** The "Needs attention" heading is gone, and returns when an item appears (approve one, mark another overdue).
9. **Follow wording.** People and Settings say "Follow" and "Follow check", no old name anywhere you read.
10. **A failed save (optional).** Fill a small test disk or make the history unwritable on a throwaway vault: approving stops with a plain message and nothing is half saved.

## Checks

1. **A project's own folder.** Open a throwaway project's settings, set its own folder with a `~` path and save. The Folder row names the real location in plain words. Open Files and Search: they show that folder. Judgement: does the Folder row read plainly?
2. **A missing folder.** Point a project at a folder that is not there (an offline OneDrive folder, or one you rename for the test). creaite says it is missing and makes nothing on your laptop. Put the folder back: everything works again.
3. **Fetch transcripts as they appear.** Switch on Settings › Mail, Teams and Intake › Checking mail and Teams › Fetch transcripts as they appear. Run a mail and Teams check from the dashboard with a real Teams meeting that has ended and has a transcript: it is in Intake without pressing Process, the end line says how many were fetched, held back and left, and no minutes were made. A confidential meeting is not fetched. With the setting off, the check fetches nothing.
4. **The transcript link form.** On your real account, check that the transcript links Microsoft 365 returns are recognised. If one is not, it is left for Process and nothing breaks.
5. **Shareable minutes.** Make shareable minutes for a meeting with a Sensitive decision. **Review these lines before sharing** appears before the export and names what it would touch. The shareable file does not carry the lines that describe the Sensitive item. Judgement: is the warning's wording right?
6. **File paths.** The project settings Folder row shows the real OneDrive location. In Activity, a change ask creaite made names the file and project, and Show in Finder reveals the file in the real Finder (or its folder if it is gone).
7. **A missed mail and Teams check.** With Microsoft 365 unavailable at the time of a check, Today says "Today's mail and Teams check didn't run" with Check now, and Settings › Health says "A mail and Teams check didn't run", with the retry time (24-hour). It clears after a check completes. Judgement: is the wording right?
8. **Nothing else changed.** One example each: approve an item and undo it, press Check now on Today, open Files.

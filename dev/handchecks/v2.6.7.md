# Hand checks for creaite 2.6.7 (judgement and live-only)

Everything scriptable is in `dev/tests/handchecks/test_v267.py` (notes, guides, #328 words and structure) and in the Chrome tests of the branches and the release gate. This sheet holds only what a person must see. Setup: a throwaway vault (`dev/tools/handcheck.py`) with fictional data unless a check says "live". Chrome at 1400 px and 900 px (desktop only), light and dark. Items follow `dev/release-notes/2.6.7-plain.md`. Checks marked GUARDED apply only if the 2.6.7 chain (#347 words, #339 routing) lands; otherwise skip them.

1. **The chip and its reason.** Open Intake with a Matched, a Likely and a Not sure meeting. In List, Board and Two panes each row shows its chip with a short reason. Judgement: are the three states easy to tell apart, and does the reason read plainly, without crowding the row?
2. **Not sure offers projects.** A Not sure row shows up to three project buttons. Choose one: the row becomes Matched, and after a reload the choice is still there. A later meeting in the same series is Matched.
3. **Process all ready (Q9).** With one Matched (or remembered), one Likely and one Not sure row ready: Process all ready processes only the Matched one. Judgement: the skip list names the Likely and Not sure meetings and says to pick a project first; it is clear what to do next.
4. **A confidential row.** Add a confidential meeting that is ready. Process all ready counts it ("1 confidential meeting left out") and never shows its title or project.
5. **Single-row Process.** Press Process on the Likely row: it processes that meeting as before. Same for the Not sure row (it asks for a project if it needs one).
6. **Stop.** Stop on the running row ends the rest of Process all ready, as in 2.6.6.
7. **Remember <domain> for <project>? (L4, pending Alan's sign-off).** After choosing a project for a series, the offer appears once. Yes saves it; No does not ask again for that domain. Judgement for Alan: is this offer wanted? If not, it is removed from the notes and this check is dropped.
8. **GUARDED: "check my mail".** Type it in ask creaite: the check runs at once, marked as checking, with no Go ahead. "sweep" still works.
9. **GUARDED: "save" and "undo last save".** After a review list in the same session, "save" saves what you approved, and "undo last save" undoes it. Typed as the first message of a session, "save" is NOT a go-ahead (ask creaite asks). "apply" and "undo" still work.
10. **GUARDED: typed "refresh calendar".** It reads the calendar only: no mail check starts, and it finishes sooner than a check. Queue one and have it refused (a run is going): the reason stays visible.
11. **GUARDED: the mail check.** It still proposes items for people you follow, and does not read your home folder.

# Hand checks for creaite 2.6.1 (judgement and live-only: 14 checks)

Everything scriptable is in the release gate (full suite, green on 5bf7da29). This sheet is only what a person must see or do on a real Mac. Setup: a throwaway vault (`dev/tools/handcheck.py`) with fictional data unless a check says "live". Chrome at 1400 px, light and dark. Never holds the release; run in production. Items refer to `dev/release-notes/2.6.1-plain.md`.

## Live only (real Mac, real Microsoft 365)

1. **Add files in the app window (#307).** Open creaite as the app window (not a browser tab). Intake › Add files: the file chooser opens. Pick a .vtt: a row appears in Arrived. Pick an unsupported file: a note names it and why, and stays until dismissed. Repeat in a normal Chrome tab. *If the app window still shows no chooser, say so: this is the one case not proven fixed.*
2. **Drag a .vtt onto Intake** in the app window: "Drop to add files" shows and the row appears.
3. **Check again with real Microsoft 365 (#307).** After a real check of mail and Teams, the Intake count and "Approved this week" update without reload, with no internal words in any message.
4. **Start creaite when I sign in (#300).** Settings › turn it on, sign out and in: creaite is running and the dashboard opens. Turn it off, sign out and in: it is not running. Help shows the note beside Stop.
5. **First-run card shift.** On a fresh vault, open the dashboard: the first-run card does not jump or overlap as the page loads.
6. **Real minutes, real transcript (#306, #308).** Approve a real meeting's minutes that includes an item already approved elsewhere: it skips that item, names it in words, saves the rest. Then a held meeting whose transcript is gone: **File without the transcript copy** files the minutes, Undo puts them back.
7. **Follow-up email from real minutes (#309).** Draft the follow-up: it is the shareable version by default; a Sensitive item is absent from it and from the Word, HTML and PDF copies; the internal export says "Internal, not for distribution".

## Judgement (look at it)

8. **Minutes editor (#302).** The Track control is three icons and names the chosen one; Approve minutes says "tracks N actions"; the attention strip counts items still needing an owner or date and Next jumps to one; Decisions have room to write. Source time chip opens the transcript at that line. Reads cleanly in light and dark.
9. **HTML and PDF minutes (#303).** Wordmark, colours, fonts, headings and tables match the Word minutes.
10. **Today (#313, #318).** No item appears twice across Decisions needed, Your commitments, Needs attention and Week ahead; Week ahead says how many are above. The day line runs to the Friday of next week (check on a Monday and, by changing the date, a Sunday). An unread day says "has not been read yet" with Refresh.
11. **Week ahead milestone (#310).** Shows "depends on N open work items"; click opens the milestone, then Work filtered to it.
12. **Work items (#241) and the avatar menu (#295).** Marks, compact rows, one-click client-visible, history read well. The avatar at the foot of the sidebar shows your name and "Settings and health" (or the problem in words); the menu opens Settings, Health, Help and Customise sidebar; works in all three sidebar sizes.
13. **People page (#304) and health Change button (#305).** One pencil per person and a flag saying what is missing; the Change button is a small icon and still clear.
14. **Plain words (#289 to #291).** Skim Today, Intake, Settings and Health for "sweep", "staged", file paths or commands. Some older wording is expected in places; it moves in 2.6.2. Note any you see.

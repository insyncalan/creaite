# v2.2.0: your walk-through

About 27 steps in one sitting, in plain words, on sample data unless it says laptop 1. Each step says what to do, what you should see, and the matching check in `v2.2.0.md` (Team Stark's and Team Cap's technical checks; most are tests that run by themselves, so these steps are what only your eyes and hands can judge). Setup is in `v2.2.0.md` (a throwaway vault, never your real one, never Microsoft 365), in Chrome at the usual width, **creaite and Cursor themes only**, light and dark.

Mockups for what you approved are in `dev/mockups/` (open the HTML): `report-banner-mockup.html` (option A), `health-suggest-mockup.html`, `rail-icon-size-mockup.html` (that one is 2.3), `files-navigation-v2-mockup.html` (2.3). The real screens as they look today are in `dev/design-system/`.

**Not in this release (do not look for them):** Week ahead's redesign, the Intake page rebuild, Draft email's AI bar on client facts, the AI bar in plain places, the rail icon sizes and the Files navigation (2.3). Some parts may still be on branches and not in the cut (links in every editor, the plain Draft email, the Capture drawer AI, the board width, Review age, one Esc on a peek, the agenda as an editable document): if one is missing, say so and it comes out of the notes rather than failing.

## First: the few only you can do (laptop 1)

1. **A real suggestion on selected text.** In a real note select a sentence and use the sparkle. *Expect:* sensible wording; nothing changes until you press Accept; ⌘Z takes it back. (Check 24)
2. **Health ✦ with the real Claude.** On a project press the sparkle beside Health. *Expect:* a health and a reason that match what the tracker shows, with the evidence listed; nothing is saved until you accept and save. (Check 31)
3. **A real sweep.** Run a sweep. *Expect:* meetings from today forward only; mail and Teams from the last three days at most; no error. (Check 35)
4. **Draft email into Outlook,** if the plain editor made it. Edit the preview, Copy, paste into Outlook. *Expect:* the table, colours and bold are the same. (Check 27)

## Help (the biggest change)

5. **Search Help.** Open Help, type a word, then a plural, then something silly. *Expect:* results by part with the match marked; the silly one says nothing was found and offers ask creaite; `/` jumps to the search. (Check 2)
6. **The ? key.** Go to Today, then Review, press **?**. *Expect:* a panel on the right with two to five jobs for that screen; Esc closes it; a quiet "Press ? for help with …" line at the foot of each screen. (Check 3)
7. **The home and What's new.** Open Help's home, then What's new. *Expect:* twelve job cards that open their pages; a What's new tab with the notes for every release, newest first. (Checks 4, 5)
8. **The cards and the length.** Open the first-day card and skim two Reference pages. *Expect:* one page for the card; the Reference pages read short. (Checks 6, 7)

## Today

9. **The day band.** Look at today's meeting cards. *Expect:* no Note link and no Prepare pill; small dates; hover a meeting's title and a Prepare popup opens after a short rest. (Checks 8, 9)
10. **Past days and weekends.** Look at the day line. *Expect:* past days behind one small icon (hover it, pick one); Saturday and Sunday only if something is planned. Do this on a weekday. (Checks 10, 11)
11. **Your commitments.** *Expect:* five items, then "Show more". (Check 12)
12. **Intake and Review.** *Expect:* the section is called Intake and Review, sits just above Week ahead and folds; new items are marked. Tab to its "Reject, with a reason" buttons: they should take the keyboard (this is known to be red on the line). (Check 13)
13. **Midnight.** Leave Today open past midnight (or use the test clock). *Expect:* it rolls to the new day and yesterday's finished meetings stop saying "Not happened yet". (Check 14)
14. **Friday close.** Open Close the day on a Friday with a Saturday meeting planned. *Expect:* the weekend work is listed under Monday. (Check 15)
15. **Portfolio health.** *Expect:* six bars a project, tall green for on track and a short red stub for off track; a ? opens the key; hover a bar for the week and its reason. (Check 16)
16. **Review.** *Expect:* age in quiet text; when everything is old, one banner with "Oldest first"; when one is different, only that one marked. (Check 17)

## The left side bar and hovering

17. **The side bar.** Collapse it, expand it, switch labels off. *Expect:* three states; the wordmark toggles it; the current page is clear; icons only never opens the full bar on hover; it is narrower (188 px when expanded). (Checks 18, 19)
18. **Flyouts and hovers.** Point at Projects, Work and More; then hover a tooltip and a card. *Expect:* a flyout after a short rest, closing a moment after you leave; every hover waits the same time; only one flyout open. (Checks 20, 21)

## Notes and editing

19. **Autosave and focus mode.** Type in a note and stop; then open focus mode and close it a few ways (Esc with a menu open, then without). *Expect:* it saves about two seconds after you stop, with no draft step; Esc closes the top overlay first, then leaves focus mode; a refused save is visible. (Checks 22, 23)
20. **The selection bar.** Select a sentence in a note, then in Current state. *Expect:* a sparkle in the margin and a grouped bar on hover; Turn into is apart from the AI; Format notes appears for two or more paragraphs; Accept, Show changes and ⌘Z work. Select text in a Prep shape field: no bar there. (Check 24)
21. **Links and the plain editors.** Add, change and remove a link in a note and in a plain field. *Expect:* links work everywhere text is shown. (Checks 26, 27) *(may not be in the cut)*
22. **Generate current state, Steer it further.** Generate, then steer twice. *Expect:* a box with starters that add a sentence and never send; versions kept; a client-safe draft says why it cannot be refined. (Check 29)

## Reports, health and Prep

23. **The weekly report banner.** Open a weekly report and scroll down a little. *Expect:* the search bar and title block go at once, leaving one slim bar; scrolling up does not bring them back; at the top of the report they return and push it down; clicking Export or Approve and file does not bring them back. Repeat on the client report and the minutes view. (Check 30)
24. **Prep.** Open a meeting's Prep. *Expect:* one page, a peek on hover, notes and questions you can edit. (Check 32)

## ask creaite and quiet things

25. **Esc and a long reply.** Start a long request, press Esc, reload. *Expect:* the panel closes, the request keeps going and shows going after the reload; only Stop stops it. Open an old long chat: it appears whole and does not rewrite itself. (Checks 33, 34)
26. **The small ones.** Resize the Work board (is the width remembered?); open an item's peek and press Esc once (does it close?). Look at Settings › Diagnostics for the usage record: counts only, no text. (Checks 36, 37, 38) *(the first two may not be in the cut)*
27. **Themes.** Look at Today, the side bar and a note with the bar in **creaite and Cursor**, light and dark. *Expect:* nothing is off. (Check 39)

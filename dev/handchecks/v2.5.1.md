## Checks

### #234 Weekly report: a left-out row
1. Weekly report › Overdue actions: turn the eye off on the rows, save, reload the page → each row says "Left out" (and how to put it back), never "Click to write"; the ID stays.
   - Test: `test_v251.py::Hidden234.test_c01_left_out_row_says_left_out`.
2. Turn the eye back on → the row shows the item's text, owner and due date from the tracker, not blank cells.
   - Test: `test_v251.py::Hidden234.test_c02_the_eye_brings_the_row_back_from_the_tracker`.
3. With one row left out and one saved blank, export the report (Word and HTML) → neither file has an empty row, and neither holds the two rows' text.
   - Test: `test_v251.py::Hidden234.test_c03_no_empty_row_in_an_export`.
4. Side by side on Falcon: turn the eye on for a left-out row, and before the answer comes back open another project → the other project's page, its report and its saved drafts hold nothing from Falcon's row.
   - Test: `test_v251.py::Hidden234.test_c04_a_late_answer_lands_in_no_other_report` (the reply to `/api/report/reshow` is held 6 s, the technique of `test_reshow_switch.py` on `v250-hiddenrow`).
5. Client report › Overdue actions: leave a row out, save, reload → "Left out", not "Click to write"; turn the eye on → the client-safe words (the client message, owner and due), never the item's internal title or its internal note.
   - Test: `test_v251.py::Hidden234.test_c05_client_report_left_out_row`.
6. A left-out row reads as left out, not as a row waiting for text: the ID, "Left out" and the hint, in light and dark, at 1400 and 900 px (judgement, on the screenshot sheet).
   - Test: `test_v251.py::Hidden234.test_c06_left_out_row_look` (takes the screenshots).

## Judgement and live (13)
1. **#232 Intake** Open Intake from the sidebar, the old Review address and the old Intake address: one page, one number, and the same number on Today's line and Close the day's "Review now". Switch List, Board, Two panes; reload and the view is remembered.
2. **#232 Look** At 900 and 1440 px the page never scrolls sideways; stage boxes, rows and the peek read cleanly in the Claude palette.
3. **#232 Real files** Drop a real transcript and a "not sure" file on the page: each shows size, likely project and kind; the "not sure" one waits for you. Process one meeting, Stop another; a private meeting is skipped by Process all ready.
4. **#232 Find work items** Add minutes written by someone else; Find work items suggests items from them and drafts no new minutes.
5. **#268 Minutes view** Open real minutes: one continuous sheet, no Internal and Distributed tabs, a Track column on each table. Tick, cross and leave lines; edit words in place; owner, Priority and Workstream drop-downs work; an owner not in People says "Needs details" with Add details.
6. **#268 Approve and Undo** Approve minutes with a ticked item that has a real problem: the box says where. Approve with nothing ticked works. Undo leaves the minutes exactly as before.
7. **#268 Export** Export Word, PDF and HTML and draft the follow-up email: all follow your Track choices; the green "done" colour and monospace text show correctly.
8. **#275 From Intake** Approve the same minutes from a row in Intake: same result as Approve minutes; Approve routine never approves a crossed or untouched line. Stop one approval (e.g. a missing item) and see it say so and put everything back.
9. **#186 AI drafts** In a Draft email to an outside address the AI bar (Improve, Tone, Ask) is back; a suggestion that adds internal wording in the minutes is dropped with a reason; a reply like "I can't give a firm date, but…" is kept.
10. **#236 From rows** With a followed person's mail checked, Today shows a From <name> row after the milestone rows; Dismiss keeps it gone until the thread changes; no label says they are followed.
11. **#246–#248 People and Settings** Settings tabs read as one card per group; the People page has four tabs, search, add, edit and remove; removing someone who owns items is refused with the reason; project settings is one scrolling page and Follow is set only there.
12. **#288 Page keys** On every page except Intake press T P W I F A S R E: the right page opens. Type the same letters in a field: nothing moves. On Intake press A three times then R: three approved, one rejected, you stay on Intake. Work's filter picker is Shift+F. An open peek, ask creaite, a menu or a dialog holds the keys.
13. **#296 Source note** A new item's source note says "— Actions"; an older note written with "Action Items" still links to the right section.

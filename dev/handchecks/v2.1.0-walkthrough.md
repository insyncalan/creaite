# v2.1.0: your walk-through

About 20 steps, in plain words, on sample data unless it says laptop 1. Each step says what to do, what you should see, and the check it covers in `v2.1.0.md` (121 checks; about 100 are tests that run by themselves, so these steps are only what needs your eyes and hands). Do the first seven first: they can only be done by hand, on laptop 1.

Mockups for what you approved are in `dev/mockups/` (open the HTML): `generate-current-state-mockup.html`, `notes-header-mockup.html`, `notes-editor-mockup.html`. The reporting designs (weekly report, client view, minutes, Reports page) are your private claude.ai artifacts.

Not in this release (moved to 2.2.0, so do not look for them): the margin sparkle and hover AI bar, links in editors, Notes autosave, Notes focus mode, "Steer it further" on Generate. Checks 89, 91, 92, 94 and the sparkle and links part of 98 are for 2.2.0.

## First: the seven only you can do (laptop 1)

1. **Open a filed report in your real programs** (check 19). Approve a weekly report, then open its Word, PDF and web versions. *Expect:* each opens in its usual program and looks like the page on screen; pinching on the trackpad zooms the page.
2. **Process a real transcript** (60). Drop a real meeting transcript in Intake and process it. *Expect:* draft minutes appear in creaite (no Word file), written from what was said, with the transcript beside them.
3. **✦ on a report section with the real Claude** (72). On the weekly report press ✦ beside a section, then Polish, Shorten and Find the source. *Expect:* sensible wording; nothing changes until you accept it.
4. **The VIP check, once, for real** (101). Run the VIP check. *Expect:* it reads your mail and Teams and answers, with no error.
5. **Every hardened run still works** (115). Ask creaite something that reads a note; make a Prep page; ask Search a question; run Generate current state; run a sweep. *Expect:* each works as before. If one says it cannot, tell me which.
6. **Delete a note** (120). In a note's … menu choose Delete note. *Expect:* a confirm box, then one approval card; only Approve moves it. The note goes to the project's archive, and Files › Archived can put it back. Read the wording and say if it is clear.
7. **A VIP thread card** (121). *Expect:* a card showing the type, the ask, the due date, who owns it, and which item it matches; confidential threads show only a neutral line.

## Reports

8. **The weekly report** (3 to 9). Open one. *Expect:* an A4 page of twelve sections in order; zoom between 40% and 80%; Show as exported; moving, hiding and renaming a section is remembered; scrolling never jumps you back to the top.
9. **Edit it** (10 to 12). Type in a section; press ✦ and Shorten; click an item ID. *Expect:* it edits in place; ✦ offers Keep mine or Use this; the ID opens the standard peek.
10. **Approve, Undo, Edit again, export** (13 to 18, 74). Approve it. *Expect:* it is filed; Undo takes it back; Edit again makes a new version and keeps the old; HTML, Word and PDF match the page; a filed report is never saved over.
11. **The client view** (20 to 32). Open the client version. *Expect:* only client-visible items; nothing marked Sensitive; Partner only items are left out and never added; the client's own words; a check before every export; a health change needs a reason.

## Minutes

12. **A draft from a transcript** (33 to 36, 50). *Expect:* an A4 page with a source line, the transcript pane with time tags, and the actions, decisions and risks as rows in the page.
13. **Edit and approve** (35, 38 to 44, 53 to 55). Type a deadline in words ("next Friday"); remove a row and undo it; press Approve minutes. *Expect:* the date is worked out and shown; removing and undoing works; one popup lists what will be filed; approving is one apply, and Undo reverses it.
14. **Share it** (56, 57, 58). *Expect:* the Distributed version has no Internal only lines and nothing Sensitive; the follow-up email is in the Export menu; the exports match the page.

## Reports page and the meeting hub

15. **Find the reports** (61 to 66, 67, 68). *Expect:* Reports in the menu; the Reports page; each project's reporting space and cockpit card; a Friday reminder. There is no meeting page any more: an old meeting link goes to the meeting's Prep, Review or minutes.

## Notes

16. **The Notes header** (88, 118). *Expect:* "+ New note" like Work's button; "+ Tags"; the date accepts words like "last monday" and shows what it understood.
17. **The editor** (97, 98). *Expect:* a checklist whose boxes line up with the text; a "/" menu that opens where you typed; three heading levels; "#" inserts the item's ID with its type and title; one toolbar that fits on one line.

## Today and the small things

18. **Today** (80, 81, 83, 86, 117). *Expect:* the page content at 80% width and centred with the coloured header full width; the line about "Urgent work…" gone; Week ahead can be hidden like Resolved; Top risks and issues show two lines of resolution action.
19. **Peek and dialogs** (78, 82, 84, 85, 87, 116). *Expect:* ⌘↵ saves any dialog; the Latest update field grows as you type; Esc leaves nothing highlighted; the health form opens over the page; Review's multi-select bar follows you as you scroll.
20. **Generate current state** (93, 119). *Expect:* the dialog is 60% wider with the quiet settings strip, a roomy Steer it box, and Draw on folded; Generate is in view without scrolling.
21. **Settings and Health** (79, 110, 111, 113, 114). *Expect:* "Show changed only" is gone; a refused Apply says what to do; a Health fix that only raised an approval card says it is waiting on the card; Settings does not keep re-checking; if the live server stops, it says why.

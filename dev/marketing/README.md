## Facts the copy relies on (check before changing a claim)

- Everything described is built in v2.0.0 or earlier. The in-app reports and minutes arrive in v2.1.0: until that ships, the copy says Word reports.
- "Kept on your Mac": the tracker, engine and backups are local; Claude reads the content it works on, and the daily backup zip goes to the company OneDrive. So the copy never says "nothing leaves your laptop".
- creaite never sends a mail, a Teams message or a report; Microsoft 365 actions are off.
- The word VIP is never used on anything shared (BL-288): the copy says "people you follow".
- The contact line is a placeholder: `[YOUR CONTACT OR LINK]`.

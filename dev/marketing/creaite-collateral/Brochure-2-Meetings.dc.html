From meeting to tracked

Every meeting leaves work behind. creaite finds it.

Programme managers sit in meetings all day, then lose the evening writing them up. creaite does the first draft, and you do the judging.

1

It reads

Meeting transcripts, and the Outlook and Teams messages since your last check. It skips what you have told it to ignore, and alerts you first to anything from people you follow.

2

It proposes

Draft minutes, and the actions, decisions, risks and issues it found, each one matched to the right project and pointing to where it came from.

3

You approve

Review by project. Approve, edit or reject each item. Only then is it recorded, and the last approval can be undone.

In your control

AI that proposes. A person who decides.

creaite is built so that a mistake by the AI never becomes a mistake in your record.

Approval before anything is saved

Scheduled runs only write proposals. One command is allowed to write to your tracker, and only after you approve.

Every change can be undone

Applies, edits and file changes are logged and backed up first, so you can put them back.

A source on every row

Each item points to the file, email or chat it came from, so you can check it in seconds.

Kept on your Mac

The tracker, the engine and the backups live in a folder on your laptop. Nothing is published, and creaite never sends a mail or a Teams message.

Confidential stays confidential

Anything marked confidential is left out of client reports, and only items you flag as client-visible can appear in them.

An assistant with limits

ask creaite answers questions and makes changes within rules it cannot override. Secrets and the system are always out of reach.

What you need
  • A Mac
  • The Claude desktop app, signed in
  • Microsoft 365 connected
  • Chrome or Edge

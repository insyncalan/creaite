How it works

From a meeting to a report you can trust.

Five steps. creaite does the reading and the first draft. You make every decision that counts.

1
It reads

Everything where your work already lives

No retyping. creaite picks up what was said and written since it last looked, and skips what you have told it to ignore.

Meeting transcripts
Outlook mail
Teams chats
Your own notes
2
It proposes

The four things a programme runs on

Each proposal is matched to a project and points back to the line, mail or chat it came from.

Actions
Who does what, by when
Decisions
What was agreed, and by whom
Risks
What might go wrong
Issues
What has already gone wrong
3
You approve

Nothing is recorded until you say so

Review by project. Approve what is right, fix what is nearly right, reject the rest. The last approval can always be undone.

Confirm test environment access for the migration team
Approve Edit Reject
4
It records

One tracker, one truth

Approved items go into a single tracker on your Mac, through one door only, with a source on every row.

One writer
A single command is allowed to change the record.
Local
It lives in a folder on your laptop.
Backed up
A daily backup, and one before every change.
5
It delivers

The views and documents you need

Built from the tracker, so they never disagree with each other.

Today
Your day, ranked: meetings, what is due, what needs a decision.
Meeting prep
A page for any meeting, with sources.
Dashboard and Excel
Browse, filter and edit the portfolio.
Word reports
Weekly, client-safe and steering committee.
What creaite will never do
Send a mail, a Teams message or a report for you
Record anything you have not approved
Put confidential items in a client report
Publish your data to a web page or a cloud service
Meetings in. Reports out. You stay in charge.

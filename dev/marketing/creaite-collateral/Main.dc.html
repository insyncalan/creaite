Nothing falls through

Every action, decision and risk raised in a meeting, a mail or a Teams chat is proposed for you, with the source named on each row.

You decide, and can undo

Nothing reaches the tracker until you approve it. Every change can be put back with one click.

Reports in minutes

Weekly status, client-safe reports and a one-page steering committee summary, built from the tracker. Only items you mark client-visible reach a client.

Ready for every meeting

A prep page for any meeting: what is urgent, what is overdue, the latest reporting and the questions to ask.

Private by design

The tracker and the engine live on your Mac. creaite never sends a mail or a Teams message, and publishes nothing.

Ask it anything

Ask about your portfolio in plain English and get an answer with its sources. Any file it changes is backed up first.

What you need
A Mac, the Claude desktop app signed in, Microsoft 365 connected, and Chrome or Edge.

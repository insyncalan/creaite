# Review checklist: #239 step 1 (`v251-step1`), client output from one checked snapshot

Design: `git show origin/v251-snapshot-design:dev/proposals/client-output-snapshot.md`. Invariants I1 to I18: Team Cap's
comment on #239 (19:28), "FOR #239 / TEAM STARK'S DESIGN: the invariant each finding violated". Probe ids are in `INDEX.md`
(script, what it proves, args, known result); run them with `run_all.sh <checkout> <group>`.

Meta-invariant (I0): DERIVE, NEVER TRUST. What decides who may see a thing, or what is written into a client file, is derived at
render time from the ROUTE, the TRACKER and creaite's settings. Every input (request, draft file, stored document, upload) is read
ONCE into one in-memory object, gated, and that same object is rendered and filed. The output is checked again. Unknown keys never pass.

How to use: tick a row when the probe says PASS on the exact head AND the "also look at" line holds in the source. A probe that says
ERROR proves nothing (it never counts as PASS). `[GAP]` = no probe script covers it: do the manual step in the row.
Step 1 scope (design "Size and order" 1, plus the minutes refusal and the posted page): allow-list, render-time header/logos/kind/audience,
post-render check, one checked document under the lock, minutes refusal, posted page. Rules marked "step 2" are not expected yet.

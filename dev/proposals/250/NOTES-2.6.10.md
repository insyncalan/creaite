# 2.6.10 release-note draft: fetch transcripts as they appear (#250)

Draft paragraph for the release notes (Romanoff writes the final note).

**Fetch transcripts as they appear.** A new setting, **Fetch transcripts as they appear** (Settings › Mail, Teams and Intake › Checking mail and Teams), is off by default. When you switch it on, a mail and Teams check you ask for, and Close the day, also fetch the transcript of every Teams meeting that has ended and put it in Intake, so it is ready when you open the page. The scheduled morning check and the check at the start of a session do not fetch. It only reads. No extra AI run, nothing is added to your project records, and you still press **Process** to make minutes. Confidential meetings are never fetched. When the check ends, creaite says how many were fetched, how many it held back and how many are left for you to load with **Process**.

**Limits to know about.**
- A confidential meeting that was dropped from your calendar, while a normal meeting with the same title sits at the same times, can hold the normal one back. It is loaded when you press **Process**.
- A private all-day entry holds nothing back.
- When the clocks go back, a meeting in the repeated hour is held back rather than risk fetching a confidential one. Press **Process**.
- The fetch is off until you switch it on.
- The form of the transcript links from live Microsoft 365 is still to be checked on a real account; until then, a link creaite does not recognise is left for **Process**.

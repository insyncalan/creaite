# creaite UI/UX audit: consistency and design-token integrity

14-10-2026 · creaite 1.8.0 (main at c09a0d3) · branch `dev-ui-audit`

## Verdict

**Amber.** The token system is real and mostly respected: about 1,900 `var()` uses, every palette resolves in light and dark, and axe-core found no rendered colour-contrast failure. No page scrolls sideways at narrow width, and there were no console errors across 432 renders.

**The one thing that matters:** there is no shared scale beneath the colour tokens. Type, radius, spacing, shadow and z-index are written by hand, so the same control is drawn differently from page to page. For example, `.btn` is 32px tall on five pages and 28px or 30px on Today. Inputs come in six sizes, and section headings in four styles. Add type, radius and space tokens, and move the outliers onto them, and most of the drift below goes away.

## Consistency across pages (P2)

**The same control is drawn differently** (rendered, creaite light, desktop; full detail in [rendered.md](ui-audit-2026-10-14/rendered.md)):

| Control | Variants | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| `.btn` | 3 | 32px on Files, Intake, Review, Settings and Work; 28px or 30px and 12px text on Today |
| `.linkbtn` | 4 | 12, 13 or 14px; one is in Schibsted 700 |
| `.ib` (icon button) | 3 | 28px on four pages, 32px on Review and Work |
| `.mini` | 3 | 30px on Today, 26px on Review |
| Inputs and selects | 6 + 3 | heights 30, 32, 36, 40 and 58px; radii 6, 8 and 10px |
| Page title `h1` | 3 | 22px everywhere, 34px on Today (by design), 49.6px on Help |
| Section heading `h2` | 4 | Today uses 13px uppercase labels; Settings 15px; Review 14px; Intake 18px in Title Case with icons |
| `.tag` | 3 | two fonts, two weights on one page (Review) |

**Section headings need one pattern.** Today uses "GETTING STARTED" uppercase labels. Intake uses "Calendar Import" and "Upload Files" in Title Case with an icon. Settings uses "Appearance" in sentence case. Intake's Title Case also breaks the plain-English, sentence-case voice everywhere else.

**Primary actions vary:** a filled teal button on Today ("Draft weekly report") and Intake ("Process selected"), outlined with green text on Review ("Approve"), and a tinted "+" on Work. Decide which treatment means "the main thing on this page".

**Dates:** Projects writes dates out ("Saturday 7th November (In 24 days)") where Work, Intake and Review show `17-10-2026`. **Decided (Alan, 14-10-2026): keep it.** That is not drift. The only open point is the capital "In" mid-sentence, a small wording fix.

## Token and scale drift (P2/P3)

From the token audit ([tokens.md](ui-audit-2026-10-14/tokens.md)) and css-analyzer, `style.css` only:

- **No type, radius, space or z-index tokens.** There are 47 font sizes (17 px values in the dashboard and 30 rem values from the embedded guide), 23 radii, about 60 spacing values, 45 box shadows and 20 z-index values (0 to 210, ad hoc). Near-duplicates to merge include 11, 11.5 and 12px; 13 and 13.5px; 14 and 14.5px; and 5 and 6px radius.
- **Hard-coded colours:** 74 findings. Ten are P1, each a literal that exactly equals an existing token and should be `var(--x)`. The other 61 are P3 by pattern: shadows, scrims, print, theme swatches and canvas.
- **Near-duplicate tokens:** 23 pairs within a palette with identical or near-identical values; they could be merged.
- **Complexity:** 39 `!important` rules; the highest specificity is (2,6,2).

## Impeccable detector (P3, static)

54 findings ([impeccable.md](ui-audit-2026-10-14/impeccable.md)), mostly in the two guides:

- 22 side-tab accents;
- 20 tiny-text findings (text under 12px, many at 11px in `style.css`);
- 7 kicker or eyebrow labels above headings;
- 1 grey-on-colour;
- 1 cramped padding;
- 1 bounce easing.

These are the detector's taste rules against generic "AI-looking" UI. Weigh them against creaite's own design decisions; they are not defects.

## Phone width and touch

- Nothing scrolls sideways.
- Work moves extra columns behind "more columns".
- The rail stays at 72px with labels on narrow screens.
- At narrow width the search placeholder wraps onto two lines.
- Targets under 24px: checkboxes at 13 to 16px (Review, Intake, Activity), the 8px column-resize handles, and the 40×22px toggles in Settings. Inline text links are exempt from the rule.

## Method

| Tool | What it did |
|---|---|
| `dev/tools/ui_audit.py` (new) | Starts the live server in a throwaway vault with the tests' fictional portfolio. Renders all 12 pages in 9 palettes × light and dark × 2 widths (432 renders). Records computed styles, a style signature per control class, tap targets and overflow, takes screenshots, and runs axe-core on every page in Brightwater and on Today in every palette. |
| `dev/tools/token_audit.py` (new) | Static and standard library only. Checks unused and undefined tokens, theme completeness in four modes, hard-coded colours, scale drift, near-duplicate tokens, WCAG contrast of token pairs, and cross-surface colours and fonts (dashboard, guides, prep page, offline page, reference, Word, email drafts). |
| axe-core 4 | WCAG rules on the rendered pages. |
| Project Wallace css-analyzer | Counts unique values in `style.css` and the guides. |
| Impeccable 4.5.2 `detect` | Static anti-pattern scan of the dashboard source and guides. |
| Visual review | Screenshots of each page in creaite light, Brightwater dark and narrow width, plus a rendered prep page and guide. |

**To rerun** (axe and css-analyzer are npm packages and are not bundled):

```
python3 dev/tools/token_audit.py > tokens.md
python3 dev/tools/ui_audit.py --out <dir> --axe <path>/axe-core/axe.min.js --themes all
```

**Limits:**
- Headless Chrome would not go below 500px, so the "390px" pass ran at 500px.
- Rendered fonts are the first family in each stack; the run didn't check whether a theme's named font (ABC Diatype, SF Pro and so on) is installed or falls back.
- The person and meeting pages, dialogs, the side panel and ask creaite were not rendered.
- Contrast pairs in the token audit are chosen by token name.
- The sample data is small: two projects and three actions.

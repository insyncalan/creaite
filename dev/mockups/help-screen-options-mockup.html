The three ways

  • 1 · The ? key. Opens a side panel on the jobs for this screen. No new button; "Press ? for help with Review" sits quietly at the foot.
  • 2 · Quiet links under the list. Two or three text links, in the place the old key hints used to be.
  • 3 · At the moment you need it. A one-time tip on first visit, and a link in each empty state.

Chosen: 1, the empty-state link, and the first-visit line (screen 4)

  • The ? key already opens Help, so this changes where it lands, not what people learn.
  • The page keeps no extra chrome. The foot line says the key.
  • The side panel keeps your place; the full Help is one link away.
  • The empty-state link costs nothing and appears only when someone is stuck.
  • The first-visit line (screen 4): one quiet line under the title, once per screen. Any key or click clears it, and that key or click still does its normal job (it is not swallowed). Pressing ? clears it and opens the panel. It never shows over an open item, a dialog or ask creaite, and it waits until they close. It is remembered per screen in this browser; Settings › Essentials › Look and help can turn it off. No animation under reduced motion.

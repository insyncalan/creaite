How the four collapse designs differ

  • 1 · Icon in the top-left (as live): the panel icon beside the wordmark, in the top bar's left cell. Always visible, one click. Costs nothing in the rail, and it is where you already look.
  • 2 · Edge handle: no button in the top bar. Point at the rail's right edge (a small handle appears; the edge also highlights) and click, or drag it left or right. Quiet, but you have to know it's there.
  • 3 · In the More menu only: nothing on screen. More's flyout ends with "Customise sidebar" holding Show labels and Collapse. Cleanest, most hidden; best if you rarely change it.
  • 4 · The wordmark is the toggle: pressing "creaite_" collapses and expands, with a small chevron on hover. No extra icon, still in the top-left.

Rules built in

  • Expanded (224px) always has labels beside the icons. Collapsed is 76px with each label under its icon (Labels when collapsed: On) or 56px icons only (Off).
  • Icons only never opens the full rail. Point at Projects, Work or More and only their flyout opens (after 0.2 s, closing 0.3 s after you leave); other icons show a tooltip after 0.4 s.
  • More sits at the bottom, as live; its amber "1" means a problem and says so on hover. Review's count is the accent colour.
  • No navigation shortcuts. Targets are 36px tall in the list (52px with a label under the icon), with a 3px focus ring.

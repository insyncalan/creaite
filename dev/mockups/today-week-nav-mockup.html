How it behaves

The day lineEvery working day the saved calendar holds, from Monday of this week to seven days ahead (inbox.meetings_from). Here that runs to Tuesday 20th, with a faint "Next week" divider. Days are in small capitals, and the chosen day is written out in full (THURSDAY 15TH OCTOBER). Today is in the accent colour, with a small dot when you're on another day. Weekends appear only when they have meetings. Clicking a day opens it. Inside the line, ← and → move between days, as on tabs.
SwipeWith the pointer over the band, swipe two fingers left for the next day and right for the previous one. The band slides in from the direction you swiped. At the first or last day the calendar holds, it nudges and stays put. Swipes follow one another with the pointer just resting on the band, with no click needed. One gesture moves one day, and its momentum is ignored. Over the band, the swipe never triggers Chrome's back gesture (overscroll-behavior-x: none on the band). On a touch screen, drag the band sideways.
MouseHovering the band shows a faint chevron at each side, for when you aren't on a trackpad. They're hidden at the ends of the week.
PrepEvery meeting from today onwards has one pill that opens its Prep popup, on any day the calendar holds. A meeting worth preparing for shows Prepare, with accent-coloured text, and the pill is the button: there's no separate Prep link. Other meetings show Prep. At rest the pills take the band's own colour with a hairline, so they don't stand out. On hover they lift to white (the surface colour in dark). When a prep page exists, an Open icon sits beside the pill and opens the page (BL-287), followed by a "Prep page made" chip (kept: Alan, 13-10-2026). Past days show minutes and transcript follow-up instead.
Getting backT, or the TODAY entry in the day line. There's no separate button. A page refresh, reopening creaite and the next morning all open on today. The chosen day isn't kept in the address or anywhere else.
Below the bandUnchanged and always as of today. On another day, one quiet italic line under the meetings says so.
Keys[ ] T act only on Today, and never while you're typing. T isn't used elsewhere in the dashboard. [ ] already switch projects in a cockpit, so the keys mean "previous" and "next" in both places.
Never on screenThe swipe and the keys are never hinted on the page: no hint line, no tooltip naming a key, no first-run tip. Help's Today section and its keyboard shortcut list describe them.

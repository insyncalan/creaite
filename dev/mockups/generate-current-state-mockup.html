    What changed, and why

    • No chip rows. The four choices are quiet fields in one strip. They look like text until pointed at, then show a hairline and open a menu: discreet, and still one click.
    • Steer it is the body: full width, about six lines, resizable, with three one-click starters (never filled in for you).
    • Draw on is one line ("Drawing on 5 sources") that opens the ticks. It changes rarely, so it costs one line, not six.
    • Dates… opens its two date boxes under the strip, only when chosen.
    • The hint under the strip follows the Form and Audience you pick, so it is read once, in one place.
    • Buttons are the ordinary 32px ones. Generate shows ⌘↵ (with #101).

    Kept exactly as built

    • Period default from dashboard.generate_days; Audience and Length remembered in the browser; Internal and Standard the defaults.
    • Length locks for the forms that fix it (the field dims, with its reason on hover).
    • Client-safe switches the sources off and says so; Project documents off by default.
    • The sweep note; the result opens in the editor as today.
    • Desktop and 900px only: at 900px the strip stays four across (each field is about 150px).

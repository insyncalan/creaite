Close the day, reimagined

Three ways to offer a catch-up sweep of mail and Teams before you close the day, so nothing that came in this afternoon waits until tomorrow's sweep. Sample data from your portfolio; every option keeps the rule that a sweep only proposes, and nothing reaches the tracker until you approve it in Review.

What the research says

  • Every open loop gets a home. A shutdown works when each unfinished thing has a plan or a place you'll look again; planning an unfinished task is what quiets it (Newport's shutdown ritual; Baumeister's study as reported).
  • Short, same steps, same order. Sunsama's shutdown is three steps: what you did, a reflection, what moves. Akiflow and Motion make "process the inbox" part of the evening routine.
  • A clear end. A closing line ("Shutdown complete") marks that work thoughts can stop.

creaite rules every option keeps

  • The sweep is offered, never forced: Skip is always one click, and Close the day is never blocked by it.
  • The sweep only stages proposals for Review (approval before writes); it reads Microsoft 365 and changes nothing there.
  • It is cx-sweep through the run engine: progress, Stop, one run at a time; skipped automatically if a sweep ran in the last 30 minutes (a setting).
  • British English, DD-MM-YYYY, project names not codes, Today's warm band and serif.

How it behaves

  • Today's card, unchanged below, with one new strip at the top: Catch up before you close? with the time of the last sweep.
  • Sweep now runs cx-sweep in place: which source it's reading, a progress bar and Stop. The rest of the card stays readable meanwhile.
  • When it finishes, the strip lists what it found (type, words, project) and refreshes "Still open" and "Moved today".
  • Close the day while it runs closes when it finishes ("Closing when the sweep finishes"), so you can walk away.
  • Swept in the last 30 minutes: the strip just says "Caught up at 17:41" with a quiet "Sweep again".

Good for

  • The smallest change: one strip, the card you know.
  • A one-minute close.

Watch

  • The new items sit above the summary; a long sweep result pushes the rest down (capped at five, "and 3 more").

Close the day

Tuesday 13th October · about three minutes
  1. Catch up
  2. Today
  3. Loose ends
  4. Tomorrow

How it behaves

  • Four short steps, the same every evening, then a clear end. Back and Next with the arrow keys; Esc is "Not yet" and keeps your place.
  • 1 Catch up: the sweep starts as the card opens (if the last one is over 30 minutes old), so it runs while you read steps 2 and 3. Skip stops it.
  • 2 Today: what moved and what you promised, each promise one click from your commitments.
  • 3 Loose ends: everything still open, the sweep's new items included, gets a home: Review now, First thing tomorrow (top of tomorrow's brief) or Leave. Nothing is approved here; Review stays the only place that writes.
  • 4 Tomorrow: the next working day's meetings; a meeting without a prep page offers Make prep page (it runs after you close).
  • Close the day ends on "Day closed at 18:04. Everything has a place." and Today shows it.

Good for

  • The research's strongest pattern: every open loop gets a decision, then a clear end.

Watch

  • Longest of the three; on a quiet day steps with nothing in them are skipped.
  • "First thing tomorrow" is new: a short list kept with the day's record, not a tracker change.

Opened by the door icon, or by itself at 17:30 (your evening time). The sweep started when it opened.

How it behaves

  • No dialog: from your evening time (day.close_from) Today shows a small Closing the day panel beside the day band, also opened by the door icon (BL-270).
  • The panel is a checklist that ticks itself: the catch-up sweep runs as it appears (one click to stop), Review and Intake tick when empty, each promise and missing prep page has its one action.
  • Nothing is required to close: Close the day works at any point; what's unticked is listed in tomorrow's brief.
  • You keep working on the page while it runs; the panel stays put and updates.

Good for

  • The least interruption: closing is part of Today, not a pop-up.
  • Sees progress at a glance.

Watch

  • Starting the sweep by itself is the one "automatic" step; it can be set to ask first (one switch in Settings › Close the day).
  • Needs room beside the day band; on a narrow window it sits under it.

Try it

  • Wait for the sweep to tick, then Add and Make it.

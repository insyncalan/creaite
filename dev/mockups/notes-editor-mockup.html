2. The / menu, and three headings

What is wrong today (from Alan's screenshot): the menu opens below the last heading at the end of the note, not under the "/" he typed in the middle of it, and it runs off the bottom of the note card. It is also one flat list of nine rows with no descriptions, and the only heading is "Sub-heading". The cause is for the build agent to find (the menu's position is measured from the caret). The mockup follows the Notion pattern (from what I know of Notion; its help page is thin on this): it opens under the caret, groups the blocks, shows an icon tile, a name and a one-line description per row, narrows as you type, flips above near the bottom, and shows the keys at the foot.

4. What "Draft kept" means

Typing is saved in two steps, so nothing is lost. A second after your last key creaite keeps a draft copy ("Draft kept"): it is stored, but the note's file is not rewritten yet. The file itself is written after 30 seconds without typing, or when you leave the note, and the line then says "Saved". If the page closed in between, your draft comes back. "Draft kept" is accurate but opaque. Proposed wording:

While you typeToday: Editing
Proposed: Editing
A second after the last keyToday: Draft kept
Proposed: Draft saved, not filed yet
After 30 s or on leavingToday: Saved 14:02
Proposed: Saved 14:02 (unchanged)

5. The toolbar: 23 buttons to one line

Team Stark's shared toolbar has 23 buttons and wraps to two lines at the editor's width (screenshots at 1400 and 900px). Option (a) accepts two lines. Option (b), recommended: group the rarely used ones in hover menus (the same behaviour as the AI bar): 11 controls, one line at 900px. Nothing is lost: Unlink is Remove in the link popover (#120), Indent and Outdent are Tab and Shift+Tab (and in the List menu), Clear formatting is the first entry of the Heading menu ("Text"), and the Insert menu holds [[ note, @ person, # work item, Table and Image.

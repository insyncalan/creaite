What is new in this version

  • Every / command works in any section: headings 1 to 3, lists, checklist, quote, table, image, link, # work item, @ person, [[ note, templates. Typing # adds an item as a row or a link, as in Notes.
  • Every selection and AI command works: the margin sparkle, Turn into (instant), Improve, Structure, Make into, Ask, and Format notes when you select several paragraphs.
  • ✦ beside every section (Objective, Agenda, To raise, Talking points, Ask, Notes) redrafts that section with an optional steer, then Keep mine or Use this and Steer it further with versions: the same pattern as Generate current state and the minutes.
  • The standard work-item peek (half the window, over the page, J and K through the agenda's items, Sneak peek, Draft chase, Copy link, More). Not a custom one.
  • Draft chase as designed (BL-298): the envelope in the peek, the pane, the modes, the tone, the email; nothing sent.
  • One version: no Internal or Distributed variants (Alan). Exports are Internal-marked.

Kept from the last version

  • Save agenda opens the document at once; no banner, no "Open it" or "Show in Files".
  • An A4 page, zoom 40 to 80%, Show as exported, Export ▾ to HTML, Word or PDF, saved as you go with versions.
  • Delete agenda and Start over under …, last, Delete in red (confirm, then the approval card; restore from Files › Archived; Start over keeps the current version).
  • The hover card on an ID opens after 0.6 s and closes 0.3 s after leaving; click for the full peek.

AI on selected text, redesigned

Today a selection shows an "AI ▾" button that opens one tall list of ten rows with descriptions. It sits over the text it is about, scrolls inside itself and runs off the note. Proposed: a slim horizontal bar above the selection, with the options grouped into three sub-menus and a place to say what you want. Try it: the text is selected; open a group and choose an option.

Place:The groups follow the place, as the menu does today.

Purpose

Agree the data-sharing approach with the client's legal team before UAT starts.

What changes

  • Horizontal bar above the selection, 38px, one row, never over the selected words; flips below when there is no room above. A sparkle, then groups, then Ask.
  • Three groups instead of ten rows. Improve (Fix spelling and grammar, Rewrite clearly, Shorter, Longer, Make it executive), Format (Format nicely, Turn into a checklist, Summarise at the top), Make into (Action, Risk, Decision, Update). Each sub-menu has at most five short rows and never scrolls.
  • Ask… opens a one-line box in the bar: type what you want, Enter asks, Esc goes back.
  • Fix spelling is one click away inside Improve; nothing is pre-selected, nothing runs until chosen.
  • Places differ: Email draft shows Tone (More formal, Warmer) in place of Format and Make into; a one-line field shows only Improve. Nothing is added that the place does not offer today.
  • Keyboard as now: Alt+/ opens the bar, arrows move, Esc goes back; focus returns to the text.

The suggestion (same rules as today)

  • Shown under the selection with the option's name, the suggested text or the changes word by word, the plain-code check ("Keeps your dates, numbers, names and item IDs"), and Show changes, Try again, Discard, Accept. Nothing changes until Accept; a confidential text still asks first.
  • Make into keeps today's rule: it proposes an item for Review and records nothing until you approve it there. (Check against the current flow when building.)
  • Desktop and 900px: the bar clamps inside the window; at 900px the groups stay one row.

AI on selected text: five options to test

Today a selection shows an "AI ▾" button that opens one tall list of ten rows. It sits over the text it is about and runs off the note. Below are five ways to replace it, each working on the same text. Switch options, places and the kind of selection, and try each: how many clicks to Fix spelling? to Make an action? to say something of your own?

The five options

  • A · Grouped bar: a slim bar under the selection; Improve, Format and Make into open short sub-menus. Everything is two clicks; nothing is hidden.
  • B · Ask first: a small box under the selection with the cursor in it (say what you want, Enter), three suggested chips for this text, and "All options" folded below. The prompt is the front door.
  • C · Quick actions: one row of the three most used actions (Fix spelling, Rewrite, Shorter), then More ▾ and Ask. One click for the common things.
  • D · Margin sparkle: a small sparkle in the left margin beside the selection; it never touches the text. It opens a short list (Ask… at the top, then Improve ▸, Format ▸, Make into ▸) with side flyouts.
  • E · Smart bar: like C, but the three buttons are chosen from the selection: a typo-ridden line offers Fix spelling and Make into; a long paragraph offers Shorter, Executive, Summarise; several lines offer Turn into a checklist and Make into. More ▾ and Ask stay.

What they borrow from (research)

  • Notion AI: select text, then an Ask AI menu: Improve writing, Fix spelling and grammar, Make shorter, Change tone (a sub-menu), and the result below the original with Replace, Try again, Discard. Third-party guides; labels vary. (A, B)
  • Apple Writing Tools: Proofread, Rewrite and three tone presets as one tap each; changes underlined, with a switch between original and edited and a revert. (C)
  • Microsoft Copilot in Word: an icon in the margin beside the selection with a few actions (Auto rewrite, Make shorter, Make formal) and "Make changes" for your own prompt; versions you can cycle through. (B, D)
  • Google Docs and Gemini: nothing confirmed in my search.
  • Every option keeps today's rules: nothing changes until Accept, the "keeps your dates, numbers, names and item IDs" check, Show changes, Try again, Discard, and the confidential ask. "Make into" still goes to Review only. Places still offer only what they offer today.

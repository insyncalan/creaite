Sneak peek

Change the open work item by typing what you want. With an item open in the peek, type //; one / still opens ask creaite, as now. The change shows as you type. Enter makes it, with Undo straight after. The few that need a confirm are marked in amber and take ⌘⏎ or a click. With the scope you agreed on 14-10-2026, on four kinds of item. The sparkle in the peek's top bar opens it too.

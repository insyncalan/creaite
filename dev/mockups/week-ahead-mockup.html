Week ahead: three options

#112. From your screenshot, the clutter is: overdue work listed as "Today" (a second copy of Needs attention), the same item twice (Escalate… is in Your commitments and again on Monday), seven things on every row (tag, title, who, project link, Meeting link, why pill, icons) in four styles, and mixed kinds with weak day headings. Below are three layouts on the same data. The first row of buttons switches layout; the second says what happens to overdue work (your Question 4). Today's own meetings stay in the day band, as now.

Overdue work:Week ahead:

Rules in all three

  • One line per item. Priority is a small dot (red Urgent, amber High), not a tag; kind is a small icon; the project is the muted line under the title. The "why" is one quiet word on the right (due, due over the weekend, needed by, at risk). Actions (Done, Move date, Chase, Open) show on hover or focus, never all at once.
  • Meetings and work are separate within a day: meetings first, with their time; "worth preparing" is a single quiet Prep chip, and "Prep page made" replaces it.
  • No repeats: anything already under Your commitments or Needs attention is not listed again.
  • Weekend: Saturday and Sunday appear only when they hold a meeting; weekend-due work sits under Monday with "due over the weekend".
  • Hide toggle in the header, remembered like Resolved. The old note line is gone (#104).

Which to pick

  • A · Calm rows: the smallest change from today; reads top to bottom like a to-do list; scales to a busy week (rows, "+n more"). My recommendation.
  • B · Week grid: shows the shape of the week at a glance; each cell is small, so a heavy day gets long; best for few items per day.
  • C · Two lanes: meetings on one side, due work on the other, a row per day: clearest split of "where I must be" and "what is due"; widest, so it needs the full column.

All three fit the 980px reading width and 900px; the grid becomes one column at 900px.

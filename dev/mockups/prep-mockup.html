What each option is for

  • A · One page: the aim (what Shape produces) sits at the top, then what is in play with a Raise / Note / Skip choice per item, what research found, the questions, and the agenda built from your choices; the right rail holds who, reporting and the series. No popup, no second place. The Raise choices feed the agenda, so the page is the whole job. Closest to today's page, with Shape and Draft agenda folded in.
  • B · Four guided steps: Read, Choose, Shape, Agenda, one at a time with Back and Next, each marked done. Best when you prep big meetings step by step; slower for the daily ones.
  • C · Peek, then page: a 30-second peek from Today and Week ahead (aim, three things to raise, the ask), and the full page one click away. The popup brief becomes the peek, so the two stop disagreeing.

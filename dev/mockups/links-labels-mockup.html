Item panelNew: Blocks

A dependency on another project. Attaching is never required: it stands on its own, and creaite only suggests.

Why this example

Tide’s cut-over needs the billing history feed that Falcon delivers at its go-live. Falcon’s go-live moved to 19-12-2026; Tide’s cut-over is 22-11-2026. Attach the dependency to what it blocks and the date clash shows at once.

Try: press a suggestion, open Attach to… and tick another target, remove a chip, or press None needed.

Work: one place for filters

On Work, "Status: Open" appears three times: as a pill, as the view "All open" and as an applied chip, and four icon buttons carry the rest. Two ways to say each thing once. Click the views and pills; the table and the count follow. Fictional data, desktop only.

What changes in both options

  • A view is the starting filter. Its filters are not repeated as pills or chips.
  • A pill shows only what you add on top; a set pill carries its value and an ✕.
  • Clear appears only when you have refined, and returns to the view's own filters.
  • The count ("3 of 8") sits on the right, once.
  • Icon buttons keep their place but gain names on hover and focus, and the filter icon becomes "+ Filter".

Keyboard

  • Views are a roving group: arrows move, Enter picks.
  • Pills open a menu: arrows, Enter, Esc closes and returns focus.
  • ✕ on a set pill is a button with a label ("Remove owner: Sara").
  • Nothing animates under reduced motion.
Recommendation: A. The views (Mine, Due soon, Chase) are how you switch the job you are doing, so they stay one click away. B is tidier, but it hides them behind a menu and costs you a click on every switch. A still removes the repeats: it drops from three rows to two and says "Status: Open" zero times.

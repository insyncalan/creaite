What changed from the earlier version

  • Spelling and grammar is gone as an option. Every AI rewrite also fixes spelling and grammar (British English) and says so in the suggestion; the plain-code check still keeps dates, numbers, names and item IDs.
  • Sparkle, then bar. The sparkle appears in the margin beside the selection; a click opens the bar under it; Esc or clicking elsewhere closes both.
  • Menus open on hover and close when the pointer leaves (a short delay so a diagonal move does not close them); Enter or click also opens them, for the keyboard.
  • AI is now Improve, Structure, Make into, Ask. Today's "Turn into a checklist" (AI) becomes "Pull out the tasks" so it is not confused with the plain Checklist under Turn into; "Format nicely" becomes "Add headings and bold".
  • Places: Email draft: Improve (Rewrite, Shorter, Longer), Tone (More formal, Warmer), no Turn into. A field: Improve only.

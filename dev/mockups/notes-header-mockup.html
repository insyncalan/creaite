What changed

  • + New note is the Work page's own tonal "+" button: quiet until pointed at, then it shows its word. Same component, nothing new.
  • The collapse control is a small quiet double-chevron at the right edge of the list header (it flips when collapsed); it sits away from the word Notes.
  • The title row carries Find updates and "…" at the far right. Nothing else wraps onto a second line.
  • Under the title, one line of properties: Created, the Date (with the site's calendar) and With. The word "Working session" is no longer repeated there: it is the note's kind, already in the title and the list.
  • Tags sit on their own line: Internal (fixed), then your tags as chips, then "+ Tags". Click a tag's text to rename it; × removes it. A few suggestions show until you have some.

The date

  • Type a date (DD-MM-YYYY) or words: today, yesterday, last monday, next friday, in 3 days, 3 days ago. The popup shows what it understood before you press Enter.
  • Or open the calendar and click a day. Today is ringed; the chosen day is filled.
  • Stored as YYYY-MM-DD as today; shown DD-MM-YYYY. A phrase it cannot read says so and keeps the old date.

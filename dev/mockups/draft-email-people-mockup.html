Draft email from a person: "I sent it", and right-click a name

For #153 and #154 (v2.3.0). The Draft email popup on a person's page is not redesigned: the one addition is an "I sent it" button that logs every ticked item. Right-clicking a person's name anywhere opens a small menu whose Draft email opens that same popup. Try both; the menu works with a right-click, the Menu key or Shift+F10 on a focused name.

"I sent it" on the People popup (#153)

  • The popup is as built: modes (Update request, Chase, Follow-up), the ticked items, the email, Copy email and Copy as plain text. Only the button is new, in update and chase modes.
  • I sent it adds a dated Latest update to every ticked item ("Chased Tom Reid by email (reminder 1); reply asked for by 19-10-2026", or "Emailed Tom Reid by email asking for an update"), records each in the chase record so the next one steps up, and shows "Logged on 3 items. Undo". Unticked items are untouched. Nothing is sent and no email text is stored.

The right-click menu (#154)

  • Draft email opens this popup for that person, with the item as the first tick when you came from a row. Open their page, Copy email address; Add details when they are not in the register (Draft email then greyed with the reason). Never on your own name.
  • Keyboard: Menu key or Shift+F10 on a focused name; arrows move; Enter chooses; Esc closes and returns focus. Opens at once (no hover delay). The browser's own menu is suppressed only on these links.

Health update: a ✦ to suggest

For looking at, nothing decided. A ✦ beside the health, on the project page and on the weekly report's health row, reads the tracker's facts and drafts the health and its reason. It is a draft only, with the evidence shown; nothing is saved until you accept, and saving follows the usual approval. Fictional data, desktop only. Nothing here is built.

Screen Suggest Sample

What the ✦ reads

  • Overdue actions and how late they are.
  • Milestones: slipped, at risk or forecast later than agreed.
  • Open risks and issues, and whether they have an owner and a mitigation.
  • Decisions that are overdue and waiting on someone.
  • The movement since the last health you set (a week ago).
  • Nothing else: no mail, no Teams, no files. It is the tracker only.

Rules it follows

  • A draft only. It writes into the form's fields; you edit, then Save, as for any change.
  • The evidence is shown as a list of facts with item links, so you can check each one.
  • Client-facing wording (a client report's health row) uses only client-visible items and never anything marked Sensitive.
  • Never silent. Nothing changes a project's health without you pressing a button.

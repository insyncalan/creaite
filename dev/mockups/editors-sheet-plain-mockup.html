A plain field: no formatting toolbar, no "/" menu, no Turn into, no Format notes, no Make into. A title and short labels get nothing at all. The same sparkle, hover menu and suggestion card as everywhere.

What Alan sees

  • Weekly sheet: a one-line grouped toolbar appears inside the sheet when a field is active (Heading, List and Insert menus, Bold, Italic, Strike, Quote, Link, Undo, Redo); the "/" menu and the selected-text bar work as in Notes; the sheet's own frame, claim marks and scrolling are untouched.
  • Client sheet: the same toolbar and bar, with client-safe rules: the # picker offers only client-visible items (no owners, nothing Sensitive or partner-only) and says how many are held back; Make into is off; the AI only gets what the client may see, and a result is checked again before it can be accepted.
  • Plain item field: the margin sparkle, Improve and Ask only.

Rules behind it

  • One editor, two profiles (rich and plain): the same features work the same wherever a profile is used.
  • The toolbar and bar mount outside the measured field, so heights and the saved text do not change when they appear.
  • Nothing reaches a client sheet from an internal item; the client-safe AI place is a separate server place (see #123 and #127).
  • Fields that are keys or short labels (IDs, dates, titles) get no bar.

Work board: resize the columns

For #144. You asked to grab the right-hand side of the board and make it wider to see more of each card, with a minimum width, and to have the board remember it. Three ways to do it. Try them: drag the thin line on a column's right edge (it shows when you point at a column), double-click it to reset, or focus it and press the arrow keys. Fictional data only.

Open question for Alan

  1. Should one drag change every column, or only the column you drag? Recommend option 1: every column together, one width per board. The board stays tidy and "the board's width" is one thing to remember. Option 2 is there if you want one wide column, for example In review.
  2. Limits: minimum 240 px (a card's title, owner and date never clip), maximum 480 px, so one column cannot push the rest off screen. Recommend these.
  3. Remembered per board, meaning per tab and grouping (Actions grouped by Status is one board), in this browser, like the other widths. Recommend yes.

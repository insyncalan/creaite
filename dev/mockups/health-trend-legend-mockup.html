Health trend: a legend and a tooltip

The Portfolio's 8 weeks column shows eight small bars, oldest to newest. Today it has no key and only a browser tooltip. The bar height as well as its colour carries the health, and On track is the shortest, grey bar, which reads as "nothing" until you know. Fictional data, desktop only.

One bar per week ending Friday, the oldest on the left. The dot under the last bar marks this week. Hover or focus a project's bars to read a week.

What the tooltip shows (all options)

  • The week ending date (DD-MM-YYYY), the health in words, and whether it changed from the week before.
  • "No update approved this week" for a gap, not a blank.
  • The eight bars again, with the one you are on outlined.
  • Moves with the pointer along the strip; follows the arrow keys for keyboard users.

Keyboard and screen readers

  • The strip is one tab stop; ← → move between weeks, Home End jump to the ends, Esc closes.
  • It reads as "Health, last 8 weeks, At risk this week, up from On track". The tooltip text is announced as the week changes.
  • Colour is not the only signal: height carries it too, and the key says so.
  • No animation under reduced motion.

Approve what creaite found

Nothing reaches the tracker until you approve it. About two minutes for a normal day.

The short way

  1. Open Review. Proposals are grouped by project, with items from minutes under their minutes.
  2. Press Approve routine (the double tick). It shows what it will approve first. Minutes, new decisions, new risks and anything that closes an item are never in it: do those one at a time.
  3. Open anything that needs a look. Change what is wrong, then Save and approve.
  4. Press Reject on anything that is not right and pick a reason. creaite learns from it.
Not sure about an item? Press One at a time. You see one proposal with its source beside it, and the fields creaite guessed come first.
Or say: review approve minutes for the Falcon steering reject 3, 5 as chatter

Keys: J K move · A approve · R reject

What changes for the reader

  • One search over every page of Help, with the matching line shown, instead of titles only.
  • A home of jobs: the dozen things people do most, in their words, not the feature names.
  • What's new: the release notes in plain words, with a dot after an update.
  • Short pages: a job is the short way first, then the detail. No page is longer than about 500 words.
  • Help for this screen: a small button beside each page title.

Rules behind it

  • One home for each fact: Reference keeps what a feature is; How do I… keeps how to do a job. Neither repeats the other.
  • The three printable guides and this Help come from the same file (help.md), as now.
  • Search runs in the page over text already loaded: no server, nothing leaves the laptop.
  • Ask creaite is the fallback, never the first thing.

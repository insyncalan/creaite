What changes in the top half (and why)

  • Width 640 → 960 px, so the lists sit in two columns rather than one long scroll.
  • One header pattern, like the peek and the Work views: the date line, the title, then chips. Today's free-floating "For" dropdown becomes a project chip, and the series gets its own chip.
  • One toolbar with a clear order: Shape (AI), then Open prep page (primary). Draft agenda and its POAD / Lighter style become a single split control instead of three unrelated button styles (today's outlined, orange pill and black chip).
  • The invite is no longer a raw list of emails: addresses are resolved to names through the people register, attendees show as avatars, and the invite text is one click away.
  • The long sentence becomes facts: "3 decisions, 1 new risk, 2 overdue" (A), or clickable tiles that filter the lists (B).

How shaping works

  • Suggested focus chips come from what already ranks items today (the invite, overdue, not covered last time). Alan picks one or more, or searches any item or milestone.
  • "Only the focus" reshapes the page: work items and documents are filtered to what is linked to the focus (links, workstream, milestone, same thread, cited in minutes). Each shows why, and can be ticked out. "Focus first" keeps the rest below.
  • Notes are pre-populated and editable (objective, talking points, asks, carried over), with every line cited. Accept saves them with the meeting; they feed the prep page and Draft agenda. Regenerate keeps your edits unless you say otherwise.
  • B is the same result driven by one sentence, for when the focus isn't a single item. It shows how creaite read the instruction, so you can correct it.

What the in-app page changes

  • It opens inside creaite (a Meetings › Prep view), not as a separate HTML file in a new window. The saved file in the Agendas folder stays as the record, and Print and Open the file remain.
  • Work items open the peek, exactly as Work does today: edit owner, due, status inline, staged and applied the usual way. Try clicking a row.
  • Latest reporting carries its date and health ("Weekly report · week ending 09-10-2026 · ▲ At risk"), with the headline and three points, not a block of pasted text.
  • Every source is a link: Word and Excel open through /api/open, mail and Teams open in Outlook or Teams (the web link the sweep already stores), tracker items open the peek. The type icon says what will open.
  • Past meetings in the series are listed with their minutes, what each raised, and what was not covered.
  • Layout uses the width: 1 is a reading page with an outline and a context rail; 2 is structured by agenda point (each with its items and sources, and it becomes the Draft agenda); 3 is a dashboard grid. All collapse to one column on a narrow window.

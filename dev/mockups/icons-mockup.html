Icons instead of words: mockup

Each section shows Now beside Proposed. Hover or tab to any icon for its tooltip. Everything here is live except saving.

1 · Work toolbar and the New button

List and Board become a two-icon switch, Display becomes sliders, and one funnel carries the filter count. The three New buttons become one quiet control; its label is the tab's own word.

New button style

Now

On RAID the button reads “New RAID item”; on Decisions “New decision” plus a second “Decision needed” button beside it. Both open the same form: New decision starts it as Made (today), Decision needed starts it as Pending.

Proposed
Why these choices. The filter field buttons (Project, Owner, Status) keep their words because they are names holding values, and an icon per field would be harder to read. The funnel with a count replaces “More ▾” and “+ Filter”. The New button needs its type, so the tooltip and the grown label say it. On Decisions, New decision and Decision needed become one button with a two-item menu, because they open the same form in two starting states (Made today, or Pending with a needed-by date). Open the Decisions tab and click it. List and Board are also repeated inside the Display menu today, so the proposal drops the copy in the menu.

Consistency. Every glyph is the same inline SVG family at the same stroke weight, so it takes the theme’s colour and contrast. The chevron rotates instead of swapping between ▸ and ▾. The theme button shows the destination (sun when light, moon when dark) and says so in its tooltip.

6 · Every icon, and the rule it follows

IconReplacesTooltipStyle
Tooltips and access. Every icon button carries an aria-label and the same text as its tooltip, with the shortcut shown as a key. The tooltip appears on hover and on keyboard focus. Targets are 28 to 32px, above WCAG’s 24px minimum, and icons use the theme’s own text colours, so contrast follows the existing theme rules.

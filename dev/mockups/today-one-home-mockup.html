The sidebar

  • Collapse is a discreet panel icon at the top-left, before the wordmark, in exactly the same place collapsed or expanded (the common pattern; GitLab's own research asks for a toggle that never moves). Its tooltip says what it will do; ⌘\ toggles; your choice is remembered.
  • Collapsed is a 56px rail of icons: each has its name on hover and to a screen reader; counts become small badges; the current page keeps its accent bar.
  • More sits at the bottom, opening up (or sideways when collapsed), with Customise sidebar… at its foot; right-click the sidebar for the same.
  • Customise: drag items between "In the sidebar" and "In More", or use the arrows (keyboard-friendly); Today stays first; Settings can live in either; Icons only; Reset. It saves to the same dashboard.rail setting you have today (order, hidden, display), with a new "more" list.

What it is

  • One screen, under More, that lists only what needs you, each as one line with a Fix it button; what's fine folds into one line.
  • The same checks as doctor and Diagnostics, in plain words, with the fix built in.

The fixes

  • Launcher: rebuilds it for this vault (BL-305 makes the dev copy its own "creaite (dev)").
  • Chrome's copy: points the manifest at new icon files so Chrome offers its "app icon changed" prompt.
  • Microsoft 365: opens Claude's connector settings; creaite can't sign in for you.
  • OneDrive copy: makes it now instead of waiting for tomorrow.

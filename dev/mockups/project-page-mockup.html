What it is

  • A Status tab on each project, first of its tabs: health with its reason, Current state, help needed, milestones, top risks and the week's decisions. It reads the tracker live; you only write the words.
  • Edit in place. Click any sentence and type. Evidence chips (like FAL-A-007) keep each claim tied to its source (BL-207). Health changes need a reason, as today.
  • What moved this week lists what changed in the tracker since last Friday. Add writes a suggested sentence, with its evidence, into Current state. creaite never writes there on its own.

Weeks

  • The page is always "this week". Close the week on Friday freezes it as that week's snapshot and starts next week from it.
  • Past weeks open read-only, and the page marks what changed since then.

Reports

  • Client view shows exactly what a client report would: client-visible items only, nothing Sensitive, risks in their client wording.
  • Export to Word builds the branded weekly or client report from the page, as a draft for Review. Nothing is sent, and the Word templates stay in the Brightwater brand.
  • The weekly and client reports take this page as their source, so Friday's report is a check, not a write.

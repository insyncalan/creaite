# creaite 1.6.1 (Beta)

The sixth update, and the largest so far (there was no 1.6.0). The main change: **creaite drafts the wording and you decide**, with every sentence showing where it came from. Around it: a page for each meeting from invite to follow-up, ⌘K that makes changes, Close the day, Review one proposal at a time, a right-click menu on every work item, moving a proposal or a whole set of minutes to another project in Review, and one Python for every script.

**Still a Beta:** built and tested on sample data. None of it has run on your real vault yet (see "Check once by hand").

## Before you update

- **The Week ahead lists Urgent items only** (BL-235). The update adds `dashboard.week_ahead_priorities` set to Urgent, so the Week ahead's actions and RAID items are the Urgent ones; milestones and meetings are unchanged. To see more, edit the list in `system/config/settings.json` (Urgent, High, Medium, Low; an empty list shows everything). Its control in Settings comes in 1.7.0.
- **Make the launcher again after updating** (BL-237, BL-248): `system/scripts/run launch.py make-launcher`. The new one starts creaite natively on Apple silicon through the new interpreter wrapper. Until you do, the health check flags the old launcher.
- **The login agent is optional** (BL-243). If you want the live server to start when you log in, run `system/scripts/run launch.py agent install` in Terminal after updating. ask creaite can't install or remove it.
- **New permission rules are added, none removed** (BL-237). The update adds a `system/scripts/run …` twin of each script rule to `.claude/settings.json` and keeps every rule you have, the old `python3` and `python` ones included.
- **AI drafts are on.** Each kind of draft has a switch in Settings › General › AI drafts. A draft runs only when you press its button.
- **Windows is best effort and untested** (BL-241). ask creaite's protection of secrets is defined for macOS only.
- **This update is installed as before:** download the zip into `system/releases/` and say "update creaite". From the next update, creaite can fetch the zip itself with `gh` (BL-238).

## New

### Drafting and evidence

- **creaite drafts the wording, you decide** (BL-219, BL-222, BL-225, BL-226): a **Draft** (or **Suggest**) button beside the fields that are slow to write. creaite picks the facts from the tracker and your files; Claude only words them. Each sentence shows its sources, and one with none is marked **no source**. Nothing is saved until you use it: the text goes into the field for you to edit, or into Review as a proposal you approve on its own.
  - **Latest update:** one or two lines from what happened to an action since its last update (changes, meetings and sweeps that raised it again, minutes that name it, linked items), or "Nothing new since …" with no model asked. **Draft updates** on Work's bar and on Today's new "no update for 7 days" line drafts several, as proposals in Review.
  - **Client wording:** from the item's own text only, listing what it took out and anything to check (a number, code or name that isn't in the item). **Draft all** on a project's Client messaging card. A confidential item is never drafted.
  - **Reasoning, options and responses:** from the meeting where it came up and, for a risk, closed risks and their responses, with impact and likelihood one click each. In Review, a new risk with no response offers **Draft a response**.
  - **Health and forecast reasons:** one sentence from the evidence the form already shows. Movement's slip range gets a one-line explanation from its own numbers.
  - Settings › General › **AI drafts** turns each kind off and shows how often each was used, edited or rejected (counts only).
- **Where each sentence came from** (BL-207): a Current state drafted by Generate or Make it executive arrives with every sentence underlined. Click one to see the facts it rests on, the tracker items behind them and the line in the minutes, in a panel marked Internal. A sentence with no source is marked at once, and Save waits until you edit it or keep it with a note. The links are kept when you save; a sentence you change loses its link until **Check sources**.

### Meetings and the day

- **A page for each meeting** (BL-209): from invite to follow-up on one page. A strip shows where it is (Invite, Prep page, Transcript, Draft minutes, Coverage, Review, Follow-up), with the next step as one button, what the meeting produced and what was promised to you. Open it with **Meeting** from Today, the Week ahead, Intake, the prep brief, Files, an item's source or the minutes in Review.
- **Was the agenda discussed?** (BL-209): when a meeting had an agenda or a prep page, the minutes record each point as Covered, Touched on or Not discussed. **Carry to the next agenda** puts a missed point on the next prep page of the same meeting, under "Not covered last time".
- **Close the day** (BL-206): a button on Today (and in ⌘K) opens a one-minute card: what moved today, what you promised, what is still waiting, and the next working day's meetings with any prep page still to make (and, on a Friday, the weekly report). Closing it only notes the time; the next morning Today says "Closed yesterday at 18:10". A gentle reminder after a set time is in Settings › System › Advanced, off unless you set it.

### Work and Review

- **⌘K makes changes** (BL-205): type "move ABC-A-002 to fri", "mark the UAT room action done", "Omar owns the SOW action" or "add action for ABC: send the SOW, Sara, Friday". The palette shows exactly what will change, and what else it touches, before you press Enter. Enter stages it (nothing reaches the tracker until you apply); ⌘Enter also opens Review. When two items or people match, it asks.
- **Right-click a work item** (BL-197): a menu of the common actions on any action, RAID item or decision in Work, Mine, a person's page, a project page, Today and Activity's item links: **Open**, **Close** or **Reopen**, **Status**, **Owner**, **Due**, **Priority** (RAID: Impact and Likelihood), **Workstream**, **Milestone**, **Copy ID**, **Copy link** and **Draft email** to the owner; a decision offers Record the decision, Withdraw, Needed by and Decision maker. Each change saves as a change in the list does, with Undo. Highlight several first and it acts on all of them, offering only what fits every one. In Review it changes the proposal before you approve it, with **Approve** and **Reject** at the top. Shift+F10 or the menu key opens it from the keyboard. Nothing in it deletes.
- **Change the project in Review** (BL-200): a proposed item from the wrong project can be moved from its row (**Project**), its open view or its right-click menu. Its old project's milestone and workstream are cleared and it waits for your decision. On a set of minutes, **Project** moves the minutes and every item from them in one step, after showing what will happen ("Move 9 items from Example Programme to Delta Rollout"); if the minutes cover several projects it asks whose items first. Updates to items already in the tracker stay with their project. The minutes and transcript are filed in the new project's folders when the minutes are approved. **Undo** moves them back. In Claude Code: `9 is DEF` and `minutes 3 is DEF`.
- **Review one at a time** (BL-208): **One at a time** in Review shows one proposal with its source beside it, the fields creaite guessed underlined with why, and A, R, E, J and K. Routine items are approved together at the end, after a spot-check of one in ten (the rate is in Settings › System › Advanced).

### Milestones

- **History on milestones and the project panel** (BL-250): a milestone's editor and a project's health panel show their history in the same feed as actions, risks and decisions (All, Activity, Updates, Source). A milestone's history is internal and is hidden in the Client view.

### Setting up and updating

- **Start creaite when you log in** (BL-243, optional, Mac only): `system/scripts/run launch.py agent install` makes the live server start at login and restart only after a crash; a clean stop stays stopped. `agent status` and `agent remove` check and undo it. It needs no admin rights.
- **"update creaite" can fetch the release itself** (BL-238): with the GitHub command-line tool (`gh`) installed and signed in, it downloads the newest release into `system/releases/`, checks it, shows what changes and installs on your yes. Without `gh`, it says so and you download the zip by hand as before.

## Changes

### Work, Review and milestones

- **The open item follows your click** (BL-198): with an item open over the page, a single click on another row or card shows that item, on Work, the Board, a person's page and Review. ⌘-click and Shift-click still only select; with nothing open a click still only highlights.
- **The ▾ sits beside the date** (BL-251, a fault): in the Work table, a Due cell's ▾ edit button sits on the date's line, directly after it, instead of dropping under the "3 days late" line.
- **Undo waits for you** (BL-215): the message with **Undo** stays 10 seconds (others 5) and waits while the pointer is on it or you have tabbed to it; Esc hides it. In Work, ⌘Z (Ctrl+Z on Windows) undoes your last change, as it already did in Review, whenever you aren't typing in a box.
- **Milestones: the client view looks like any card** (BL-244): the Client view of the milestone card and page is the plain card, with no pill or note, and it is what opens the first time. The Internal view has a bright border and an **INTERNAL VIEW** pill, on screen, in a screenshot and on paper.
- **Add milestone in one step** (BL-245): **+ Add milestone** opens with every field ready (name, state, agreed and forecast dates, progress, description, owner and client view). Fill in what you know and press **Create milestone**; it is checked and saved as before, with **Undo**.
- **An item's history is complete** (BL-250): an item's feed shows every change made to it, its "created" line too, however long the change log has grown (Activity still lists the newest 2,000 changes). Adding or removing a link shows in both items' feeds ("Linked to ABC-A-004 (blocks)"), and adding or removing a label in the item's feed ("Label added: Sensitive").
- **What you changed is kept** (BL-232): when you edit a proposed item, creaite keeps what it first proposed alongside your change, for the quality counts to come. Nothing you see changes, and nothing extra is written to the tracker.

### ask creaite

- **ask creaite sends what you typed** (BL-199): suggestions no longer choose themselves. Type `apply` and press Enter, and `apply` is sent, not "undo last apply". ↓ or ↑ chooses a suggestion and Enter puts it in the box; Tab puts the first one in; a click still picks one; Esc closes the list.
- **A refused command, said plainly** (BL-201): when ask creaite tries a command creaite doesn't allow, the chat shows one plain line (what it tried, why not, and what it does instead) instead of the raw command. **Show the command** opens it. A ⚠ marks only a refusal the request didn't get past. What ask creaite may do is unchanged.
- **ask creaite uses the right field names** (BL-249, a fault): a change that names a field a table doesn't have is still refused, and the message now lists the valid fields and suggests the likely one ("title → name, note → description"). ask creaite looks the fields up before writing a change, and checks the change before saying it is ready.
- **ask creaite works when creaite is opened from the app** (BL-248, a fault): opened from creaite.app, ask creaite's scripts could run Apple's stand-in Python under Rosetta and fail with an `xcrun` error, and ask creaite then wrongly suggested `xcode-select`. Every run now uses the live server's own Python, the new launcher runs natively on Apple silicon, the health check and the server warn about Rosetta, and a script that fails before it starts is reported as creaite's fault, never yours.
- **Back where you were after ask creaite** (BL-211): closing ask creaite with Esc or × puts the keyboard focus back on the button, row or field you were on.
- **Looking inside a file** (BL-202, already in 1.5.2): ask creaite uses its own Read, Grep and Glob tools, never the shell.

### Reading and safety

- **Formatted text, never raw markdown** (BL-196): the release notes in Settings › System › creaite version › Review are shown formatted, with **Copy the notes** for the text as written. A confirmation in ask creaite and a Markdown file's preview in Intake are formatted too. Any HTML in the text is shown as text, never run.
- **Easier to read and to hear** (BL-213, BL-214): the milestone line's month labels and "Hide milestone details" are darker in every theme; every box and list in Files and Settings has a name a screen reader can say.
- **The live server is tighter** (BL-212): no other website can show creaite inside its own page, and the server's "are you running" check no longer tells anyone your vault's folder name.
- **A failed confidentiality check is shown, never skipped** (BL-228): if creaite can't check a client report or a client-safe one-pager for items a label excludes, Review says so and holds the approval, and the one-pager isn't built, instead of passing it silently. Errors creaite deliberately sets aside are narrowed and noted in the debug log.

### Running creaite

- **One Python for every script** (BL-237): creaite's commands now run as `system/scripts/run <script>.py …`, which picks the newest Python 3.9 or later each time, so a Python upgrade or a second Python no longer breaks a command, the launcher or the login agent. It runs only creaite's own scripts and refuses anything else; ask creaite's guard is unchanged in strength. The `python3 system/scripts/…` spelling still works.
- **The bundled libraries are checked** (BL-239): the health check (`doctor.py`, now 25 checks) fails if a bundled library file is missing, changed or added.
- **What a new Mac needs, said plainly** (BL-241): the portability rule, the new-machine checklist (`dev/SETUP.md`) and the README list the real requirements in three tiers: what creaite needs, optional Mac conveniences, and development tools.
- **Transcripts load on Python 3.9:** loading a meeting's transcript and chat from Microsoft 365 failed on Python 3.9 (it used a feature of 3.10). Found by the new 3.9 test run.

## For developers (not in the app)

- **`dev/tools/check.py`, a health ratchet** (tooling-check, BL-228 part B): a static check (about 3 seconds) that fails on syntax errors, grammar or names newer than Python 3.9 in `system/scripts`, unused imports, undefined names, duplicate functions and bare `except:`, and holds the figures in `dev/check-baseline.json` (complexity and length per function, swallowed exceptions, missing docstrings, the dashboard's HTML sinks, in-place wrappers and empty `catch`, and per-rule ruff, Biome and vulture counts). A number may fall, never rise. Branches built before it merged had their figures accepted once, at merge. `dev/justfile` holds the everyday commands; pyright is advisory.
- **Test tiers and a Chrome cap** (BL-246): `run_tests.py` sorts each file into a quick tier and a Chrome tier from the file itself; `--quick` runs the quick tier. Chrome files share a machine-wide cap of 3 across every worktree and session. Each file gets a private temporary folder, removed when it ends. `--changed` and `--flaky` pick what to run and list files that failed under load.
- **No orphans** (BL-247): every live server and headless Chrome a test starts ends with its test, however it ends; `run_tests.py --clean` (and the start of every run) stops leftovers and removes stale test folders.
- **Full suites run on GitHub Actions** (about 26 minutes a suite); local runs are single files.
- **The CI matrix** (BL-242): `tests.yml` runs Python 3.9 and 3.14 on Ubuntu and a non-blocking macOS 3.14 job, by hand only for now; automatic runs are BL-234 (v1.7.0).
- **Brewfile** (BL-240): `dev/Brewfile` lists `gh`, `git` and the test Python. Never needed to run creaite and never in a release.
- **The single writer split** (BL-229, first part): `tracker.py apply`'s behaviour is pinned by characterisation tests (19 cases), then `write_item`, `cmd_apply`, `cmd_undo` and `check_row` are split into smaller steps, as are `report.steerco_data` and `report.current_context`. Output, `change_log` and error messages are unchanged. The rest is v1.7.0 work.
- **Smaller tools:** `dev/tools/vendor_manifest.py` rebuilds the bundled-library manifest (BL-239); `dev/tools/skill_fields.py` regenerates cx-edit's field lists from `schema.sql` (BL-249); `drafts.py` is the scaffold for new draft kinds (how to add one is in `dev/BUILD.md`).

## Check once by hand

1. **After updating:** make the launcher again (`system/scripts/run launch.py make-launcher`) and open creaite from it. `system/scripts/run doctor.py` runs 25 checks, "Bundled libraries match their manifest" passes, and there is no Rosetta or old-launcher warning. In Activity Monitor › CPU the live server's Kind is Apple.
2. **ask creaite from the app:** ask "what's pending?": it answers with no `xcrun` error and no refusal.
3. **The wrapper:** `system/scripts/run reach.py classify x.md read` prints `{"kind": "ordinary", …}`; `system/scripts/run -c 'print(1)'` is refused.
4. **The Week ahead:** Today's Week ahead lists only Urgent actions and RAID items; milestones and meetings are all there.
5. **Drafts:** open an action with recent activity and press **Draft** beside Latest update: read the card and its sources, press **Use this**, change a word and click away ("1 change staged"); **Undo last** takes it back. Highlight three actions › **Draft updates**: Review shows them as "Drafted", none taken by Approve routine.
6. **More drafts:** on a client-visible item, **Draft** under Client messaging (the "Taken out" line and the checks). On a risk, **Suggest**, then **Set impact**. In a project's health form and in a milestone's forecast change, **Draft the reason**. Settings › General › AI drafts: turn one off, and its buttons grey and say why; the counts show what you did.
7. **Evidence:** on a project, Generate a Current state; click a sentence (the panel opens), Tab to the next, Esc. Save with a "No source" sentence: it asks you to edit it or keep it with a note.
8. **Meeting page:** Today › a meeting's **Meeting** link: check the strip and the next-step button. In Review, the minutes row's Coverage.
9. **⌘K:** "move <an item> to fri", read the preview, Enter; the staged pill offers Undo last.
10. **Close the day:** Today › Close the day › Close the day; reload: "Day closed at …".
11. **One at a time:** Review › One at a time; A on one, then J, K and Esc.
12. **Review, one item:** on a proposed action press **Project** and choose another project: it moves, its milestone is cleared, and it is still waiting. Undo moves it back.
13. **Review, minutes:** on a set of minutes press **Project**, choose a project, read the line ("Move n items from … to …") and press **Move**. Approve the minutes and apply: the Word minutes and the transcript are in the new project's Minutes and Transcripts folders.
14. **The open item:** open an item in Work, then click another row once: the open item changes. ⌘-click a third: it is only selected.
15. **Right-click:** a row in Work (change its owner; Undo), two highlighted rows (set a due date on both), a proposal in Review (set its priority), Shift+F10 on a highlighted row, and a blank part of the page (the browser's own menu).
16. **Undo:** in Work change a priority from the list: the message stays about 10 seconds, waits while you point at it, and Esc hides it. Change it again, click elsewhere and press ⌘Z: it is put back.
17. **Suggestions:** in ask creaite type `apply` and press Enter: `apply` is sent. Type `apply`, press ↓ and Enter: "undo last apply" is in the box, not sent.
18. **A refusal:** ask ask creaite to save a script's output to a file with `>`: one plain line, and **Show the command** opens the command; if it carried on another way, no ⚠.
19. **Focus after ask creaite:** focus People on the rail, press `/`, then Esc: the focus ring is on People again.
20. **Release notes:** put a newer release zip in `system/releases/` and open Settings › System › creaite version › **Review**: the notes read as formatted text, light and dark; **Copy the notes** pastes the original.
21. **Milestone views:** on a project page, switch the Milestones card to Internal: a bright border and an **INTERNAL VIEW** pill, in your theme and in one dark theme, and in a print preview (⌘P). Switch to Client: it looks like the cards around it. In Notion light, the month labels and "Hide milestone details" read clearly.
22. **Add milestone:** on the Milestones page press **+ Add milestone**, fill in a name, an agreed date, progress and an owner, press **Create milestone**: it appears on the line; **Undo** in the message removes it. Start another, type a name and click the page: it stays open; Esc closes it and nothing is saved.
23. **History:** open Go-live (or any moved milestone) in Internal view: History lists its forecast moves, newest first, with the reasons under **Updates**. Switch to Client: History is gone. Open **Edit health** on a project: its health changes and weekly updates are listed under the form.
24. **Screen reader:** with VoiceOver on, Tab through Files' filters and Settings › General: each field is read by its name.
25. **The launcher:** with creaite already running it says so and opens the window; with another vault's server on the same port it says "another creaite vault".
26. **Update with gh** (at the next update): with `gh` signed in, say "update creaite": it fetches the newest release and waits for your yes. Signed out, it says so and uses a zip you downloaded. Help › How do I… › Update creaite says the same.
27. **Login agent** (if you want it): `system/scripts/run launch.py agent install`, then `agent status` says loaded and a server answering. Log out and in: the dashboard answers. Settings › Stop: it stays stopped. `agent remove`: `agent status` says not installed.
28. **The rule:** read `system/creaite-rules.md` › Portability rules and `dev/SETUP.md`'s opening: the three tiers match your Mac, and Windows reads as best effort.
29. **Tests on GitHub:** Actions › Tests › Run workflow once: three jobs (Ubuntu 3.9, Ubuntu 3.14, macOS 3.14 optional). Note the time for BL-234.

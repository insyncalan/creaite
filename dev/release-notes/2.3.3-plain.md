# What's new in creaite 2.3.3 (plain words)

A small clean-up of two buttons that were easy to mistake for each other, in Review and in Intake.

## What changed for you

- **Review: approving everything from a meeting is no longer a lookalike.** The button that approves all items from a set of minutes now looks different from the Approve button on a single row, and its words say what it will approve.
- **Intake: no more duplicate buttons.** "Select new" and "Process selected" appear once, and "Process selected" looks switched off until you have selected something, then switches on.

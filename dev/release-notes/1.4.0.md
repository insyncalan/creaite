# creaite 1.4.0 (Beta)

The fourth update. Three things matter most: **work items open in a peek** (an action, risk or decision slides over the page, already editable: click a field and change it, each change staged with Undo, ⌘↵ to apply and close; new items open the same way), **every count is a link** (a number of actions, risks or decisions anywhere opens Work showing exactly those), and **ask creaite opens over the page** instead of squeezing it. Around them: rename a project's Current state card, arrange the left rail, search Activity and undo several older changes, weekend dates pointed out, errors as a banner you can dismiss, and a tidier status line on the project page.

**Still a Beta:** built and tested on sample data. None of it has run on your real vault yet (see "Check once by hand").

## New

- **Rename a project's Current state card** (BL-162). Click the card's title on a project page and call it what you like for that project ("Where we are", "Status"), up to 40 characters; Esc cancels and an empty title puts "Current state" back. It is a label on the page only: the section in the project's `CLAUDE.md` keeps its name, so the reports, Generate and search are unchanged. Kept as a line in the project's `CLAUDE.md` beside the client-report rhythm, with a backup, a settings-log line and Undo. Engine change: the dashboard template and `build_dashboard.py`.
- **Arrange the left rail your way** (BL-163). The sliders button at the foot of the rail (or Settings › General › Appearance › Left rail) lets you hide pages, put them in your own order (drag, the arrow buttons, or Alt with ↑ ↓), and choose icons only for a narrower rail. Today and Settings are always shown; Activity and Settings stay at the bottom. A hidden page still opens from ⌘K, and a "…" button lists the hidden pages with anything waiting, such as Review's count. **Reset to default** puts it back. Kept in `settings.json` (`dashboard.rail`, with a backup and a log line), so the app and a browser tab agree. Engine change: the dashboard template, `build_dashboard.py` and a new default in `settings-defaults.json` (added to your settings by the update).
- **Activity: search, a type icon on each line, and undo several older changes** (BL-164). Each line shows what kind of item it is. A search box (press **/** on Activity) finds changes by ID, words, project, person, the change or the date, with type buttons and a date range; it stays in the address. Tick older changes (or ⌘-click and Shift-click, as in Work) and press **Undo n**: creaite shows what will happen, then puts each field back only where it still holds the value that change gave it, and lists the rest as skipped. It is one new apply ("Undid 3 changes"), so it can be undone too. Engine change: the dashboard template, `build_dashboard.py`, a new `tracker.py undo-changes` command and a live-server route (`/api/undo-changes`); no database change.
- **Open an item and just change it** (BL-176). An action, RAID item or decision opens over the page at half the window, already editable: no Edit, Save or Cancel. Click the title or the text and type; its key values sit under the title as chips (status, priority, owner, due date) that open a list or a calendar; everything else is under **Details**, and its history (All, Activity, Updates, Source) is below. Each change is staged on its own, a bar at the foot of the page counts them, and nothing reaches the tracker until you press **Apply** (or ⌘↵, which also closes the item); **Undo last** takes back the newest. The page under it doesn't move, and it keeps clear of ask creaite. J and K step through the list; the corners icon takes it to full width.
- **New items open like any item** (BL-179). New action, New RAID item, Record a decision and Ask for a decision open the same panel as an open item, empty, with the cursor in the title: type the title, choose the values under it, and it is staged as soon as it has a title and a project (a decision also needs where it was decided). Changes after that update the same new item until you apply it; ⌘↵ applies and closes, and **Apply** in the bar leaves it open with its new ID. Close an empty one and nothing is kept; with a title it asks **Keep** or **Discard**, and a new item you haven't finished survives a reload. The Create form is gone for these; milestones and project health keep theirs. Engine change: the dashboard template only.

## Changes

- **Tests clean up after themselves** (BL-172, development only). Every throwaway vault a test makes is removed when the test ends, even when its set-up fails, and the test runner warns by name about any file that leaves one behind. A run never removes a vault another run made.
- **The panel's "⌘↵ saves and closes · Esc cancels" line waits until it helps** (BL-169). While you edit an item it shows once a field has changed (for a new item, as soon as you are in its first field), in its place above Save, never over a field.
- **An unsaved edit in the side panel survives a reload** (BL-167). What you have changed but not saved is kept in this browser for that item; open it again and choose **Restore unsaved changes** or **Discard**. Nothing is saved until you press Save, and changes over 7 days old, or for an item changed since, are dropped with a note.
- **ask creaite opens over the page** (BL-173; reverses 1.3.0's "makes room"). The page keeps its full width and layout under the chat and doesn't move when the chat opens, closes or is resized, and Work's filters no longer fold for it. An item's details panel is unchanged: open with the chat, it keeps its place beside the chat with Save in view.
- **Wide tables show that they scroll** (BL-165). On a narrow window, or beside an item's details, Projects, Work and Settings › People show a fade and **more columns →** at the right edge until you reach the last column, and the first column (the name or ID) stays in place while the rest scrolls. Work's list now scrolls inside its frame when the details open beside it, instead of running under them.
- **Help's loop diagram fits the room it has** (BL-168). On a narrow window (or a narrow guide) its five steps stack one under another instead of squeezing into thin columns, and its small labels are never under 12px. The guides are rebuilt to match.
- **Set-up from the dashboard** (BL-166). Settings › System › Set-up runs the health check and rebuilds the dashboard and Excel view on your click (a rebuild shows what it rewrites first). Connecting Microsoft 365 and making the launcher can't be done from the page, so it gives the exact thing to say or paste with **Copy**, and **Check again** confirms when it's done; the checklist on Today does the same for Microsoft 365. Engine change: two live-server routes, `/api/setup/status` and `/api/setup/run`, which run only commands ask creaite's allow list already permits; no permission is added.
- **Review's proposals open in the same layout** (BL-177). A proposed action, RAID item or decision opens over the page like a work item, with **Reject**, **Cancel**, **Save** and **Save and approve** at its foot; your edits stay in it until you choose, and the project, duplicate and Remember notes are kept.
- **Work and Review toolbars** (BL-175). The Keys chip is gone (every key is in Help: press ?), the funnel, List and Board and Display sit at the right end, and their menus open leftwards and stay inside the window.
- **Replaced for work items:** the read-then-Edit panel, the E key, Save and Cancel, More fields, the "⌘↵ saves and closes" line and the unsaved-edit bar (BL-80, BL-154, BL-167, BL-169) no longer apply to actions, RAID items and decisions; milestones and project health keep them (since BL-179 new items are the peek too). A decision is recorded or withdrawn with its Status (or **Mark as made**); on the Board, moving one to Made opens it with Made staged.
- **Errors show as a banner you can dismiss** (BL-180). The red “n errors in the last day” link in the top bar is gone. When something failed, a quiet banner under the top bar says how many and what kind (a fault that repeats counts once: “1 error (30 times)”), with **Investigate** (Settings › Diagnostics), **Ask creaite to look** (with the live server) and **×**. Dismissed, it stays away, reloads included, until a new error, and then counts only the new ones. Engine change: the dashboard template and the live server's diagnostics route (grouped counts and the newest time).
- **A weekend date is pointed out** (BL-181). Choose a Saturday or Sunday as a due or needed-by date (in an item, a table cell, the bulk bar or the Board) and creaite says so, offering the Friday before and the Monday after in one click, or **Keep this date**; it never stops you. The calendars show weekend days muted, Review notes a proposal's weekend date with the same two buttons, and a new early warning, **Weekend due date** (on by default, switchable in Settings), lists open items due on one. Minutes and the sweep prefer a working day when the source doesn't say. Engine change: a `weekend` note in `tracker.py` used by `propose.py` and `review.py` (never a refusal), the warning in `build_dashboard.py`, and `work.weekend_days` and `warnings.weekend` in `settings-defaults.json`.
- **The project page's status figures are tidier** (BL-185). The “No due date” figure is gone from the row under the status line (the No due date card below stays as it was), the date words are shorter (“Due 7d”, a slip of “21d”), a figure that needs attention carries a small warning sign beside its words, and every figure, Milestone slip included, opens its detail.
- **Every count opens its list** (BL-190). A number that counts actions, risks and issues or decisions (the Projects table's Evidence, a project page's Flow bars and cards, the People list, a person's page, Today, meeting prep, Settings › People) is now a link: it opens Work on the right tab, filtered to exactly those items, and the list holds as many as the number said. A zero stays plain text. Work gains a **Closed between** filter for the Flow card's closed bars. Engine change: the dashboard template only.

## Fixed

- **Undoing an applied change from the peek stays undone.** Undo returned the change's proposal to the queue, and the page then showed it as staged again, with the old value back on screen. Now only changes still waiting to be applied show as staged.
- **The owner list suggests people again, and a workstream can be added from an item** (BL-187). In an open item, typing in the owner list finds a person by name, alias or email, offers likely matches ("Is “Sara” Sara Khan?", which remembers the alias) and adds a new person with their details, as the old panel did. The workstream list ends with **Add workstream…**: give its details and it is staged with the item's change.
- **Staged changes show in every window** (BL-188). A change staged in an open item now shows wherever creaite is open on this vault (the bar at the foot, "staged" in the item's history, the new value), not only in the window that made it, and any of them can apply it.
- **The errors banner stays usable with ask creaite open** (BL-180). It now stops at the chat's edge, shortening its words if it must, so **Investigate**, **ask creaite to look** and **×** are always in reach, and clicking them leaves the chat open. Its button reads "ask creaite to look", in lower case like the assistant's name everywhere (BL-105).

## Check once by hand

- **The peek:** open an action from Work. Change its owner (type a few letters of a name or alias), its due date (try a Saturday: the alert offers Friday or Monday) and its priority. The bar at the foot counts the staged changes; **Undo last** takes one back; ⌘↵ applies and closes. Open the same vault in a second window: the staged changes show there too.
- **New items:** press **New** on Actions and on Decisions (Record a decision, Ask for a decision). Type a title, set a project, ⌘↵. Press Esc on an empty one: nothing is staged.
- **Review:** open a proposed action; edit it; **Save and approve**.
- **ask creaite:** open it on Work at your usual width; the page doesn't move. Open an item too: its fields stay reachable.
- **Counts:** click an Overdue figure in the Projects table, a bar on a project's Flow card, and a person's Open actions: Work lists exactly that many.
- **Rail and Activity:** rename a project's Current state card; hide a rail page and move another; in Activity, search for a person, tick three older changes and undo them.
- **Errors:** if the banner shows, Investigate, then Dismiss; reload: it stays away.
- **Set-up:** Settings › System › Set-up › Run the health check.

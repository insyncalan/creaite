# creaite 1.9.0

"Meeting prep." Preparing for a meeting is reimagined: a wider Prep popup with a tidy header, a facts line and Draft agenda as one menu, every item opening the full peek, and the prep page shown inside creaite in a Briefing layout, with dated reporting, the meeting's series and every source a link. Today now moves between the calendar's days, back to follow up on Monday's meetings and on to prepare for next week's. Close the day becomes a guided shutdown that catches up on mail and Teams first and gives every loose end a place. And VIP is now private to you: nothing on screen or in anything creaite writes says who you follow closely. Around them: smarter updates from mail and minutes, Draft chase from any item, promises you owe and are waiting on, filters in Settings › People, saved emails in search, and fixes to the launcher, ask creaite and Work.

## Before you update

- **A full release.** 1.9.0 is not a pre-release, so "update creaite" finds it with no version named.
- **The launcher has a new name in the development repository** (BL-305). Your vault's launcher is still **creaite**; one made from the development repository is now **creaite (dev)**, with DEV across its icon. If the creaite in your Dock opens the sample data (port 8766) instead of your vault, make it again from your vault: `system/scripts/run launch.py make-launcher --replace`. Without `--replace`, make-launcher leaves a launcher that opens another vault alone and says so.
- **A script policy, in report-only mode** (BL-255). The dashboard now carries a script policy that blocks nothing: Chrome only reports what an enforced policy would stop, and the health check counts the reports. If anything looks or behaves differently, it is a fault: **Settings › System › Diagnostics** › Copy diagnostics, and send it. Enforcing comes in a later release, after a week of reports.
- **Settings added.** An update adds each with its default and keeps every value you have: `live_mode.script_policy` (`"report-only"`; `"off"` removes the policy), `day.catchup_sweep` (`"start"`; `"offer"` asks first, `"off"` skips the catch-up), `day.catchup_after_minutes` (30), `drafting.shape` (`true`: Shape the meeting on; it is under Settings › General › AI drafts) and `promises.from_sweep` (`false`: the sweep proposes no promises until you turn it on).
- **The health check has 30 checks:** one new check reports the dashboard's script-policy reports (BL-255), and one warns when the creaite launcher opens a different vault (BL-305).
- **To install:** say "update creaite". With `gh` signed in, creaite fetches the zip itself; without it, download the zip into `system/releases/` first.

## Changes

### Meeting prep

- **The Prep popup, reworked** (BL-286 A, B and E; BL-287): the popup is 960 px wide with its lists in two columns (one on a narrow window). Its header follows the peek: the day and time, the title, and chips for the project (it opens the project picker, remembered for the series), the series and who is invited. One toolbar holds **Open prep page** and **Draft agenda**, now a menu like Work's New decision (**POAD**, **POAD lite**, the last one used marked and remembered, and **Include who's invited**); the POAD and Lighter chips are gone. A facts line replaces the old sentence ("Since the last meeting (29-09-2026): 3 decisions, 1 new risk, 2 actions now overdue"), and the invite is one line of names, resolved through the people register. Every item opens the full Work peek over the popup, with ↑ ↓ through its items; the popup comes back when the peek closes and shows an applied edit. On Today, a meeting with a prep page has an **Open** icon beside its Prep, and a **Prep page made** chip.
- **The prep page inside creaite** (BL-286, part D): a meeting's prep page now opens in creaite as Meetings › Prep, with its own address (`#prep=<meeting>`), in the Briefing layout: a header with the date, project, series and who is invited, Change focus, Refresh research, Draft agenda, when it was saved to Agendas, Open the file and Print, and Prep, Invite and History tabs; an outline on the left; the focus and its notes (editable in place once a meeting is shaped, saved to `prep-focus.json` and the Agendas page), the work items in focus as a table ("n of N"), the researched notes with links to their sources and the questions and asks (editable, kept in `prep-focus.json` through Refresh research) in the middle; dated reporting with its health, this series' timeline and every source as a typed link on the right. Files open through the live server, mail and Teams by their web link, items in the peek. One column below 1000 px. The file in the Agendas folder stays the record. Claude never runs to show it.
- **Shape the meeting** (BL-286, part C): **Shape this meeting**, first in the Prep popup's toolbar, takes the focus and the aim in your own words (a box you can drag larger, with suggestion chips for the items the invite puts first, overdue work, milestones due and what wasn't covered last time). Claude reads it read-only through the run engine (progress, Stop, one run at a time), says how it read you ("creaite read this as": the focus items, the aim, focus only or focus first, with **change** to correct it), and drafts the Objective, Talking points, Ask and Carried over, every line with its source. The boxes are editable and drag larger; Regenerate keeps the ones you edited. What's in focus lists the work items and documents linked to the focus (the links, the same workstream or milestone, the same minutes or thread), each with why, as "n of N" of those listed, each one ticked out on its own, and Show everything instead. Accept saves it for that meeting in `system/staging/prep-focus.json`, which the prep page shows. A confidential meeting is shaped from your text and the tracker only, with no tools. It has its own switch in Settings › General › AI drafts, and is counted there like every AI draft.
- **Prep on any day the calendar holds** (BL-293): the Prep popup, the prep page, cx-prep and the live server's Prep page run accept today and any later day in the saved calendar, not only today and tomorrow; a past day, or one beyond the calendar, is refused with the reason.

### Today

- **Today moves between the calendar's days** (BL-293): a quiet line of days at the top of Today's band replaces the date, from Monday of this week as far ahead as the saved calendar goes, with a "Next week" divider. Click a day, swipe two fingers over the band, drag it on a touch screen, or press `[` and `]`; `T` returns to today (the keys and the swipe are described in Help only). Only the band changes: a past day shows each meeting's follow-up (Minutes made, Draft in Review, Transcript ready with Process, No transcript, or Not checked yet with Check); today and later days show one pill per meeting, **Prepare** for one worth preparing for and **Prep** otherwise, with BL-287's Open icon and "Prep page made" when a page exists. Confidential meetings show only their title and times. A reload opens on today. The first screen built from the shared Tabs and Chip blocks.
- **Close the day, a guided shutdown** (BL-295): the evening card becomes four short steps and a clear end. **Catch up:** if the last sweep is more than 30 minutes old, a catch-up sweep of mail and Teams starts at once and runs on while you read, with Stop and Skip; it only stages proposals for Review. **Today:** what moved and what you promised. **Loose ends:** everything still open (the sweep's new items, other pending proposals, Intake, overdue items), each given a place: **Review now**, **First thing tomorrow** or **Leave**; nothing is approved or rejected here. **Tomorrow:** the next working day's meetings with their prep state, **Make prep page** for one without (made after closing, one at a time), and on a Friday the weekly report. It ends "Day closed at 18:04. Everything has a place." First thing tomorrow is kept with the day's record (IDs and references only) and heads tomorrow's brief. Settings › General › Close the day sets the catch-up (start it, ask first or off) and its 30 minutes (`day.catchup_sweep`, `day.catchup_after_minutes`).
- **You owe and Waiting on** (BL-125 stage A): what you have promised, and what others have promised you, now show on Today (after Your commitments, each hidden when empty) and on each person's page. You owe lists the soonest due first; Waiting on lists the overdue promises made to you, with how long each has been quiet, and links to Work's Chase view. Promises come from the minutes and from what you add (ask creaite, cx-edit or the Excel Promises sheet); the sweep proposes none until you turn on `promises.from_sweep`. A meeting or message can mark one kept, dropped or re-dated, and Approve routine covers a promise marked Done or Dropped (a "Looks done" guess is still approved on its own). Your own edits restart a promise's quiet clock. The weekly report lists promises first in Next week and Waiting on first in Help needed (never in a client report).
- **Today › Portfolio, one line a project** (BL-285): the description under each project's name moves into a tooltip on the name (by pointer or keyboard; none when empty), and the table's columns resize like Work's, remembered per column; double-click a handle to reset it. A resize never opens the project. Projects draws the same table.

### VIP is private

- **VIP is private to you** (BL-288, part 1): nothing creaite writes says who is a VIP. The early warning for an overdue item one of them owns reads "Overdue 3 days; owned by Sara Example", without "(VIP)", so it no longer reaches the weekly report or the steerco one-pager; a prep page's "why" says "Sara Example is invited"; the Excel export's Review sheet no longer shows the `vip` flag; the weekly report puts their overdue items in help needed without saying why; Review still lists their items first, unlabelled; ask creaite, the VIP check's gists and the skills never call anyone a VIP. VIP alerts, sorting and the early warning work as before. Help describes it.
- **VIP is private on screen too** (BL-288, part 2): no screen says "VIP" except the person form's own field and Settings › Capture and alerts › VIP alerts. Their messages head Today's Needs attention under **Messages to see first**, with no chip; the weekly reminder says "1 message about it not yet seen: check it first."; Work's filter is **People I follow** (Owned by someone I follow, Made by someone I follow, Not followed) and the owner cells have no chip; Review still lists their items first, unlabelled; the person page has no chip; the early warning is "Overdue item owned by someone you follow closely"; the Draft email's note is gone; the rail badge reads "2 messages to see first", the window title "(2) creaite…", and the notification shows only the sender's name. A test searches every screen for the word.
- **Dismiss a followed person's message, or make it a work item** (BL-296, server side): the live server can now dismiss a message from Needs attention (Seen's replacement, with Undo; the old Seen route still works), fill a new item from the message's references only (subject, project, you as owner, the source with its link), optionally draft a sharper title and a stated due date by reading that one message in Microsoft 365 (read only, never a shell or a write), and stage the item, dismissing the message and remembering what was made (its ID once applied).
- **Dismiss a message, or make it a work item, on Today** (BL-296): each message under Messages to see first has **Create work item** and **Dismiss** (Seen's replacement, with Undo). Create work item opens the new-item peek filled from the message (subject, project or Choose a project, you as owner, the source with its link), with Action, Risk, Issue or Decision, an optional **Draft from the message** (follows the Sharpen switch; never for a private or confidential message), **Save** (⌘↵: staged, applied as usual, the message dismissed) and **Cancel** (the message stays).

### Work and Review

- **Smarter updates** (BL-297): a message that moves an open item forward ("can we rebook for next week?") now updates it instead of raising a new item. The sweep reads each matched project's open items, as the minutes already do, and stages a new latest update and due date. "Looks like" matches on the item's ID, the same mail thread, the people named, the less common words both share and the item's timeframe (never an item raised after the message), and says which ("Same thread · Dana · due this week"); Claude's own judgement that two are the same now stands even when the plain check found nothing. In Review, any new item can be turned into an update to an item you pick (`review.py retarget`), undone like a merge, and Activity › Quality counts how often you had to do that. An update that changes only the due date and latest update is routine, so Approve routine covers it; any other change to an item is approved on its own.
- **Update an existing item instead, in Review** (BL-297 D): a pending new action, RAID item or decision with a project has **Update an existing item instead** in its peek and its right-click menu. It lists that project's open items, best matches first, with the strength in words (never a percentage); choosing one turns the proposal into an update of that item, and Undo in the message puts it back.
- **Draft chase from an item, server side** (BL-298): the live server can now draft one item's email from the item itself (`/api/chase/pane`, plain code, no model call): a chase to the owner of someone else's item (BL-224's wording, unchanged), a decision request to a pending decision's maker by its needed-by date, or, for your own item, a request for help ("I've been asked to follow up with you on …"), with suggested recipients from the people named in the item, who raised it, the meeting it came from and the project's stakeholders, resolved through the people register. Reply by starts two working days ahead (weekends skipped), the tone from how often the item was chased, the latest update on. **I sent it** records help and decision requests as well as chases (item IDs, the date and the mode, never text), so the next one steps up. Help describes it.
- **Draft chase in the peek** (BL-298): an envelope left of **Copy link** (key **C**) on an open action, risk, issue or pending decision opens the chase inside the peek (Back or Esc returns to the item): the mode line, To (for your own item, suggestions with why each is suggested, and + Anyone), To get … done and What I need from you for your own item, Reply by, Tone (First, Second, Firmer), Include the latest update, and the email as Outlook will show it, updated as you type. **Copy for Outlook**, **Copy as text** and **I sent it**; it never sends and never makes an Outlook draft. With AI drafts on, **Polish the wording** suggests a sharper opening (and, for your own item, the two lines), shown beside yours until you choose **Use this** or **Keep mine**. A chase about one item is worded in the singular.
- **A reload on Work keeps the view (BL-318).** Work with a view and a project filter (for example All open on one project) came back after a reload as "Custom", with every filter off but the project. The view and every filter now come back exactly as they were.

### ask creaite

- **A change you confirm is applied once** (BL-306): when ask creaite asked you to confirm a tracker change instead of showing the change card, **Go ahead** only staged it, so it then needed "apply" and a second confirmation. Go ahead is your approval: with auto-apply on, the live server now applies what that confirmed run staged as soon as it finishes (only that change, nothing else waiting in Review), and the chat says "Applied: FAL-A-010 · Owner: Alan Carter → Ivo (apply 106)" with Undo; with auto-apply off it stays staged and the chat says so. A chat you haven't confirmed still can't change the record (BL-267 unchanged).
- **`sweep` in ask creaite runs straight away** (BL-309): typing a sweep (`sweep`, `sweep mail for ABC`, `refresh calendar`…) used to stop before staging and ask you to confirm. Typing it is now the confirmation: the live server recognises a plain sweep from its words and runs it in the guarded sweep mode Close the day uses (BL-295), which stages proposals for Review and still can't apply, approve, reject or undo. A sweep with anything else in the message is an ordinary chat, as before.
- **A prep page asked for in ask creaite is written** (BL-307): ask creaite used to offer a prep page and then refuse it; now it shows a Prep page card, and Go ahead has the live server write the page (BL-98).
- **The chat follows the reply while you're at the bottom** (BL-311): only scrolling up stops it, so Jump to latest no longer shows while you are already at the bottom.
- **Esc from ask creaite no longer lands on the page heading (BL-312).** Opened with ⌘J from nowhere, Esc now returns focus quietly to the page, with no ring, and the next Tab continues in the page. Opened from a button, row or field, Esc returns there, as before.

### Settings, search and the project page

- **Settings › People has filters** (BL-264): a search box (`/`) and Work's funnel (`F`) with its picker: Project (from the items people own and the project's stakeholders), How they're involved, Organisation, People I follow, In the register, Needs attention (no email) and Removed people, each as is or is not, with live counts, chips and a count line. Removed people are hidden by default. The filters are remembered until you clear them; the search is not.
- **Search reads saved emails** (BL-276): an email saved as `.eml` in a project folder is found by its sender, recipients, subject, body and attachment names (not their contents); a malformed one is found by what could be read, or by its name. The search index is rebuilt once to read existing files.
- **A new project's Current state is no longer cut in two** (BL-310): the project template's guidance comment quotes the second-pane marker, so every new project's Current state was split through that comment. A marker inside another comment is now text, in the dashboard, Word and the reports; normal content splits and joins exactly as before.

### Looks and opening creaite

- **The wordmark without its dashed copy** (BL-308): the rail's wordmark is now the word "creaite" and the saffron bar only, in every palette, light, dark and high contrast. The icon is unchanged.
- **One launcher per vault** (BL-305): making the launcher in the development repository replaced your creaite app, so it opened the sample data on 8766 instead of your vault on 8765. Your vault now makes **creaite** and the development repository **creaite (dev)**, with DEV across its icon; both can sit in the Dock and run at once, each opening its own vault and port. A launcher that opens another vault is never replaced silently: make-launcher says so and leaves it until you add `--replace`, and the health check warns about it.
- **The dashboard carries a script policy, in report-only mode** (BL-255). Every page the live server sends (the dashboard and the offline page) now has a `Content-Security-Policy-Report-Only` header naming the SHA-256 of its own inline script, and of its styles, taken from the exact page sent, with `worker-src 'self'` for the installed app's service worker. Report-only blocks nothing: Chrome only reports what an enforced policy would stop, to a new local `/csp-report` endpoint, which counts each report in the debug log by directive and kind of source (never a URL or page text). The health check shows "Script policy (report-only): n violations in 7 days". `live_mode.script_policy` set to `"off"` removes the header; an update adds the setting as `"report-only"`. BL-212's headers are unchanged. Enforcing comes later.

## For developers (not in the app)

- **Preact with signals** (BL-256): Preact 10.29.8 and signals 2.11.2 are vendored in `system/templates/vendor/preact/` (only the ES modules the bundle needs, their types and MIT licences, read from the pinned tarballs after a SHA-256 check by `dev/tools/vendor_preact.py`, every hash in its `manifest.json`; `dev/tools/fetch_preact.sh` fetches them). JSX is compiled by the pinned esbuild (`--jsx=automatic`), each library import aliased to its vendored file, so the page stays one self-contained file and a vault never needs Node. `check.py`'s lexer-based checks and `tsc` read the components too (0 errors).
- **Components** (BL-256): screens move to components one at a time, only when they are being changed anyway. The building blocks so far, each with its own Chrome test: **StatusDot** (the health icons as element data, first mounted on Today's portfolio table, with no snapshot changed), **Icon**, **Chip**, **Tabs** and **Menu**, with `mount()` for mounting a component. 1.9.0's new screens are built on them: the Prep popup and the prep page (stores `prepPopup` and `prep`), Today's day line (`stores/today.js`) and the Draft chase pane.
- **`dev/COMPONENTS.md`, the component guide, agreed before the first build** (Alan, 13-10-2026): one component per file under `src/components/`, named for what it is; signals in one store per area; the building blocks written once with their keyboard, focus and screen-reader behaviour built in, and no third-party component library; text only through JSX, with no raw HTML but the one sanitised Markdown renderer; the existing design tokens only; a test per block; JSDoc types under `checkJs`. Changes to the guide go through the Scrum Master.
- **The pane splitters agree** (BL-310): `docx_tools.pane_markers` and `panel.js`'s `paneMarkers` count only a marker outside every comment, and `split_panes`/`splitPanes`, `md_blocks`/`mdBlocks`, `md_normalise`, `report.current_entries` and `replace_current` use them. `dev/tests/test_pane_marker.py` covers both, in Python and Chrome.
- **Steadier tests** (Team 2): the tests that failed only under load now wait for the condition they need instead of a fixed time, and three tests that depended on the real date pin their clocks (`dev/archive/notes/v1.9.0/DEV-TEST-STEADINESS-NOTE.md`, `dev/archive/notes/v1.9.0/DEV-DATE-TESTS-NOTE.md`). `run_tests.py --repeat N` runs N copies of each file side by side with no re-run alone, and `--stress N` keeps the processor busy meanwhile, so a fast laptop behaves like a shared CI runner.
- **One branch per release** (`dev/RELEASE-BRANCHES-NOTE.md`): the waiting v1.9.0 branches were stacked on `release-190` with `git merge --no-ff`, in order, each conflict and fix in its own commit, so main takes one green branch.
- **New guard commands classified** (BL-297, BL-309): `review.py retarget` and `unmerge` are record commands, `review.py candidates` and `sweep.py expected` read commands; a typed sweep runs under BL-295's `sweep` mode, with `reach.py` and `test_guard_pinned` unchanged.

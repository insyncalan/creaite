# What's new in creaite 2.2.0 (plain words)

DRAFT, 17-10-2026. Written from the issues for v2.2.0 on the board, not from a cut: nothing below is confirmed until its item is closed. Each line carries its issue number; the numbers come out before the release. Strike any line whose item misses the cut. Written for you, not for engineers; the technical notes are separate.

## Notes and editors

- **AI on the text you select.** A small sparkle in the margin and a grouped bar that appears when you hover, with Turn into and Format as well as the AI actions. (#117)
- **Notes save by themselves** about two seconds after you stop typing. There is no draft step. (#119)
- **Links in every editor:** add, change and remove a link anywhere you write text, and see links wherever text is shown. (#120, #121)
- **One editor everywhere:** the plain version and Draft email, report pages and the capture drawer join the same editor and toolbar; the Current state editor gets a checklist and a focus mode. (#133, #134)
- **Notes focus mode:** a quiet writing view, with the same note and cursor, and clear rules for how it closes. (#136)
- **Generate current state, further:** steer a draft again and again, keep versions and refine. (#131)

## Today

- **Intake and Review.** The section that was called "To decide" has its true name, sits just above Week ahead and folds like the others. (#168)
- **A calmer day line:** Saturday and Sunday hide unless something is planned, past days fold into one small icon, the Note link and Prepare pill are gone, a meeting's title opens its Prepare popup and the dates are smaller. (#138, #147, #140)
- **Your commitments** shows five items, then "Show more". (#150)
- **Calendar import** no longer lets a finished meeting say "Not happened yet", and a window left open past midnight keeps today's date. (#173, #171)
- **The sweep reads only today and the future** for meetings. (#146)

## Around the app

- **A left side bar you can read:** visible labels, a clear current page, grouped pages, tighter spacing, the wordmark as its toggle, and hover flyouts on Projects and Work. (#156, #139)
- **One hover standard:** every tooltip, card and menu that opens on hover waits the same time. (#143, #160)
- **Health bars on Portfolio:** tall green for on track, a short red stub for off track, six weeks, a ? key and a tooltip showing the week and its reason. (#162)
- **Work board:** the board's width can be resized, and the choice is remembered. (#144)
- **Prep:** one page, a peek when you hover and the agenda as a document you can edit. (#111)
- **Esc:** one press closes an item's peek (it took two), and Esc in ask creaite closes the panel but only **Stop** stops a running request. (#161, #130)
- **A long old reply in ask creaite no longer rewrites itself on screen** when you reopen it. (#148)

## Help

- **Help is shorter and easier to find your way in.** Pages are shorter, one home for each fact, every Settings path checked, the Four rules now say what stays on your laptop and what may sit in OneDrive, and there are new pages for archiving a project and for why a meeting is not in Intake. (#158)

## Not in this release

Anything not listed above arrives later. AI models, with a connect journey, are planned after 2.2.0.

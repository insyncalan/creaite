# creaite 1.9.2

"Calmer." Settings opens on the sixteen things you actually set, in plain words, with everything else under Advanced, searchable, and a dot and Reset on anything you've changed. Health moves inside Settings: it lists only what needs you, each with what to do, and five problems mend themselves with **Fix it**. From the next update, creaite remakes the launcher, refreshes the app's icon and restarts the live server itself. Review's reject gets seven reasons and creaite's own guess, in one menu that also works from Today. Today becomes one home, with **To decide** first, and the left rail becomes a sidebar that collapses to icons, with the rarer pages under **More**.

## Before you update

- **A full release**, not a pre-release, so "update creaite" finds it with no version named. No schema change, no migration.
- **Settings has moved** (BL-320). It now has four places: **Essentials** (opens first), **People and projects**, **Advanced** and **Health**. The health check is now **Settings › Health**; Diagnostics, Copy diagnostics, the version and Stop are under its list. Old links (`#settings/general`, `/system`, `/capture`…) still open the right place. A few lines in the page and in Help still name the old places ("Settings › General › Appearance", "Settings › Work and views"); their links work, and the words follow in a later release.
- **Defaults** (BL-320). **Close the day** is now offered from **17:30** (it was off). **Week ahead** shows **Urgent only**, as before (Alan, 14-10-2026). Your own values are kept: an update only adds settings you don't have, so if you have set either, including turning Close the day off, it stays as it is. To take a default, use **Reset** on that setting.
- **Do the after-update steps by hand this once** (BL-320). From the update *after* 1.9.2, creaite remakes the launcher for this vault, refreshes Chrome's copy of the app's icon and restarts the live server itself, reporting each step and rolling the update back if one fails. This update is installed by the older `update.py`, so do them yourself after it, in Terminal in the vault folder:
  1. `system/scripts/run launch.py make-launcher --if-present` (remakes the launcher only if you have one for this vault);
  2. `system/scripts/run launch.py refresh-icon` (Chrome then offers its "app icon changed" update);
  3. restart the live server: **Settings › Health › Stop**, then open creaite from the launcher (or start it in Terminal as before).
- **The rail is now a sidebar** (BL-319). **Today**, **Work**, **Review**, **Projects** and **People** are at the top; **Intake**, **Files**, **Activity** and **Settings** are under **More**, at the bottom. A page you had hidden goes into More. The panel icon at the top left (or ⌘\) collapses it to icons; under 1200 px wide it is always collapsed.
- **To decide on Today** (BL-319). Today opens with your day as before, then **To decide**: what waits in Review, transcripts ready in Intake and messages from people you follow, newest first, with icon actions on each row. Approve and save is offered only where Review's bulk rule allows; everything else opens in Review. Proposals now appear only here: Needs attention no longer lists them by meeting.
- **Rejecting asks for a reason** (BL-315). Review's **Reject with a reason** (R), a row's ✕ and To decide's reject open one menu of seven reasons (1 to 7), **Use each guess** (G) and **No reason** (0). A reject can be changed, undone or turned into an approval afterwards, even after it is applied.
- **The health check still has 30 checks**, as in 1.9.1. Settings › Health shows only the problems and folds the rest into "n checks fine".
- **To install:** say "update creaite". With `gh` signed in, creaite fetches the zip itself; without it, download the zip into `system/releases/` first.

## Changes

### Settings and Health

- **Essentials first** (BL-320): Settings opens on sixteen rows in six sections, each with one line on what it does: **You** (your name, weekend days), **Your day** (morning sweep from, what the sweep reads, offer Close the day from, catch up before closing, weekly report reminder), **Saving and review** (save my changes at once, Week ahead shows), **People I follow** (tell me when they write, check every), **Look and help** (theme, style, motion, AI drafts) and **Safety** (daily backup and the OneDrive copy). The sweep's five sources are one row; AI drafts is one switch for every kind (the per-kind switches, and Shape the meeting, are in Advanced).
- **Advanced holds the rest** (BL-320): every other setting, grouped as before, searchable by its words, its one line, the reason for its default or its old key. Set-up-only settings (folders and OneDrive, the port and the like) are shown read only, with why.
- **A dot, Show changed only and Reset** (BL-320): a dot marks anything changed from its default; **Show changed only** filters to those; **Reset** puts a setting back, backed up and logged like any settings change.
- **Health, inside Settings** (BL-320): only what needs you, one line each, in plain words, with what to do. Anything fine folds into one line ("n checks fine"); errors that have stopped show as stopped. The number of problems shows on Settings in the sidebar (and on More), from the last check, kept in this browser and checked once a minute after start-up when the last is over a day old. Never a pop-up.
- **Fix it** (BL-320, on Alan's guard decision of 14-10-2026): five problems now mend themselves at a click. Each button says what it will do first, runs exactly one known command and logs it:
  - the launcher opens another vault: makes this vault's launcher again;
  - Python under Rosetta, or an old launcher: makes this vault's launcher again, leaving another vault's alone;
  - Chrome shows the app's old icon: gives the icon new addresses, so Chrome offers its "app icon changed" update;
  - Microsoft 365 not connected: opens Claude's connector settings (creaite can't sign in for you);
  - no OneDrive backup copy yet, or an old one: makes today's backup if missing, then the copy.
  Everything else (a database repair, a reinstall, permissions, set-up steps) keeps its manual step, with Copy for a command.
- **Updates finish themselves, from the next one** (BL-320): after an update you approved, `update.py` remakes the launcher, refreshes the icon and restarts the live server in place, reporting "Launcher: …", "App icon: …" and "Live server: …", and rolls the whole update back if a step fails.

### Review

- **Seven reasons to reject, and creaite's guess** (BL-315): Not an action, Duplicate, Chatter, Already done, Wrong project, Doesn't need tracking, For another team. creaite guesses one for each pending item in plain code, shown in grey on the row: a thank-you or scheduling chatter, a near-duplicate (with the item it looks like), something already done in the tracker, or an action with no owner. Your own words and no reason are still allowed.
- **One menu, every way in** (BL-315): Review has its own selection (click, ⌘-click, Shift-click, Esc) and a bar above the list. **Reject with a reason** (R, the bar, a row's ✕ or ▾, the row's menu or Shift+F10, the peek, One at a time, and To decide on Today) opens one menu: the seven on 1 to 7, **Use each guess** (G), **No reason** (0). Duplicate takes the look-alike creaite found, or asks which item. Minutes, reports and file changes are rejected as before.
- **After a reject** (BL-315): one line with **Undo** (every row back in Review). The rows stay greyed with a chip ("Rejected · Chatter") until you leave Review; the chip changes the reason, and **Approve instead** approves in one step (an item that must be approved on its own, such as a new risk or decision, goes back to Review and opens). This works before or after the reject is applied; Activity › Quality counts your latest choice.
- **What you rejected** (BL-315): Activity › Quality lists recent rejects, each with its chip, Approve instead and Undo.

### Today and the sidebar

- **Today as one home** (BL-319): the day band exactly as it was, with the arrows fixed (1.9.1), now full width; then, in one centred column without boxes: **To decide**, **First thing**, **Decisions needed**, **Needs attention**, **Your commitments** (with You owe and Waiting on inside it), **Week ahead**, **Resolved** (one closed line with the count) and **Portfolio**.
- **Proposals in one place** (BL-319, Alan): Needs attention no longer shows the per-meeting proposal groups (with "Approve n routine"); what waits in Review appears only in To decide, and Today never shows a staging file name.
- **To decide** (BL-319): what waits in Review, transcripts ready in Intake and messages from people you follow (marked only "Message"), newest first, the top five, then **Open Review (n)**. Icons: Approve and save (only where the bulk rule allows; otherwise Open in Review), Reject with a reason (the same menu as Review), Process the transcript, Make a work item, Dismiss.
- **Week ahead** (BL-319): day by day to next Monday: meetings, the work of the priorities you choose (Urgent only by default), every milestone and every decision needed. Late work and late decisions lead the first day; each row says why it is there (due, due over the weekend, n days overdue, needed by, at risk or on track, worth preparing).
- **Icons on every row** (BL-319): row actions are icons, named on hover and to a screen reader, quiet until you point at the row; Mark done and Move the date have Undo. An 8 px rhythm: 28 px between sections, rows at least 44 px tall, one heading style.
- **The sidebar** (BL-319): a panel icon at the top left, in the same place open or collapsed, collapses it to a 56 px column of icons, each named on hover and to a screen reader, with counts as small badges; ⌘\ does the same and the choice is remembered. Under 1200 px it is always collapsed. **More**, at the bottom, opens upwards (sideways when collapsed) and carries a badge when something inside needs you. **Customise sidebar** (at the foot of More, and on right-click) has two lists, "In the sidebar" and "In More": move pages by drag or with the arrow buttons, from the keyboard too; Today always comes first; Icons only; Reset to default.

### Work

- **A fixed first column never hides a control**: on a narrow window, tabbing to an owner's link in Work could slide it under the fixed Action column. Focus now always lands to the right of a fixed column, in every table that has one. Layout is unchanged.

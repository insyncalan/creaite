# creaite 1.0.0 (Beta)

The first release for real use. creaite turns your meetings, mail and Teams into proposed actions, decisions, risks and issues; you approve them, and it keeps the record, the dashboard and the reports. Everything stays on your laptop.

**Beta:** built and tested on sample data, not yet proven on a real portfolio. Expect small fixes in 1.0.x during the trial.

## What's in it

- **Meetings to minutes:** cx-minutes turns a Teams transcript into draft Word minutes and proposed items. It also proposes updates to open items the meeting discussed. Nothing is filed until you approve.
- **Mail and Teams sweep:** runs daily at the first session after 07:00 and proposes items from Outlook and Teams.
- **Review and apply:** proposals are grouped by project and nested under their minutes. Bulk approval covers routine items only. Every apply can be undone.
- **Dashboard (live server):**
  - Today, with your day's meetings, meeting prep and what needs your attention;
  - project pages, with current state (rich text and images), health checked against evidence, milestones and early warnings;
  - Work lists, with saved views and filters;
  - People and person pages, with Prep 1:1 and handover;
  - Inbox: drop files, list this week's meetings, quick capture;
  - Review, Files and Activity;
  - **vault search** with AI answers that cite their sources;
  - **Ask creaite**, the assistant: it confirms before it writes anything;
  - themes: creaite standard, Minimals, Brightwater.
- **Registers:** projects (with matching signals), people (one full name per person, from aliases and emails), and workstreams for each project.
- **Reports:** a weekly report and a client-safe report, in Word, in house style.
- **Excel:** a browse-and-edit export with a Review sheet; edits come back as proposals.
- **Safety:**
  - one writer for the database;
  - approval before every write;
  - a daily backup;
  - a health check (`doctor.py`);
  - updates that back up first and roll back if a step fails.

## What you need

- A Mac (Windows is supported) with Python 3.9 or later. No other installs.
- The Claude app, and the `claude` command signed in.
- Chrome or Edge.
- Microsoft 365 connected in Claude, for the sweep and calendar. Minutes from files work without it.
- Anthropic's docx skill, for Word minutes and reports.

## Install (new vault)

1. Download **`creaite-1.0.0.zip`** from this release's assets. **Not** "Source code (zip)".
2. Run `python3 ~/Downloads/creaite-1.0.0.zip` and choose a folder: press Enter for `~/creaite`, or type your own.
   - It must be new or empty, and outside OneDrive and iCloud; it refuses synced folders.
3. Open the folder in Claude Code, connect Microsoft 365 and say **"setup"**. It asks before each step: git, your name and role, your projects, the people on them, a memory file per project.
4. Start the live server with `python3 system/scripts/live_server.py`, then open the address it prints.

## Update (later releases)

Download the new zip, open your vault and say **"update creaite"**.
- It shows what changes and waits for your yes.
- It backs up first, never touches your data or your `CLAUDE.md`, and can be undone ("undo update").

## Known limits

- **Beta,** as above.
- **Loading transcripts from meetings:** the Inbox lists this week's meetings, and processing one fetches its transcript through cx-minutes. One-click loading of transcripts and meeting chats from the list comes next (BL-42 phase 3), once it's tested on the live vault.
- **The live server is started by hand,** in Terminal. If it's stopped, the dashboard says so, and edits wait.
- **Local only:** no sync or sharing. Back up the vault folder the approved way.
- **Not yet built:** portfolio-level reports, archiving, unattended minutes, chase-up drafts, decision records with options, and promises and waiting-on.

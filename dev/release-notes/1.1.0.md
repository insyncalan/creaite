# creaite 1.1.0 (Beta)

The first update after 1.0.0. The big change is **where your project files can live**: your engagement folders can stay in the company OneDrive, in your own folder template, and creaite reads from them and files into them. The engine and the record still never leave your laptop. The rest is the dashboard: Today reads like a brief, editing is roomier, there is a Draft email for the people you chase, and Help is rebuilt.

**Still a Beta:** built and tested on sample data, not yet proven on a real portfolio.

## What's new

- **Project folders in the company OneDrive** (optional):
  - a `locations` setting points creaite at your Engagements folder, one folder per engagement named without a project code, with `_Portfolio` beside them; the vault itself stays on the laptop;
  - minutes and transcripts are extracted; weekly **and client reports file to `04 Reporting/Weekly`**; `04 Reporting/Steerco` is left alone (creaite never writes to it); current-state images go to `04 Reporting/Content`, made the first time one is filed; everything else (apart from `zz Archive` and `_Templates`) is indexed as a document;
  - new projects copy `_Templates/_Skeleton`; an engagement folder that already exists is adopted as it is;
  - Teams' own file names (`Title-20261001_093000-Meeting Transcript.docx`) give the meeting date, with the file's date as the fallback (Review says so), and your files are never renamed;
  - setup asks, per project, whether to mark existing meetings as already processed or to process the last few weeks. It never processes them all silently;
  - the newest daily database backup is also copied into OneDrive (the last 14, written whole), never the live database;
  - only the company OneDrive (or a plain local folder) is accepted. Personal OneDrive, iCloud and other sync services are refused, and the vault itself is still refused in any synced folder;
  - online-only OneDrive files are never read or downloaded; `doctor.py` lists them.
  Without the setting, nothing changes.
- **Today as a brief:** one centred column of short numbered sentences. **Needs attention** (grouped by meeting or sweep, with a quiet Approve for routine items only, and your report reminders), **Your commitments**, **Resolved**, **Week ahead** (to Friday, eight days on a Friday) and **Portfolio**. The headings are bold in every theme.
- **Draft email:** on a person's page, a ready-to-copy email asking where their items stand: a greeting by the time of day, open items by project with overdue in red and due soon in amber (always in words too), and a proposed subject starting with the project code. Paste it into Outlook; creaite never sends mail. It takes the place of Prep 1:1.
- **Editing is roomier:** the side panel is half the window wide while you edit (420px while you read), has a white background in every theme, and **⌘+Enter (Ctrl+Enter) saves and closes it**.
- **A quieter frame:** Ask creaite and the theme button sit at the right edge; the Live indicator is a dot alone under Settings (solid when running, a hollow ring when stopped; the hover text keeps the details); Inbox is now **Intake**, in three sections (Calendar Import, Upload Files, Quick Capture), and Quick Capture picks its date from a calendar.
- **Help in three tabs** (Start here, How do I…, Reference), from one source that also builds the printable guides; a new vault opens Help on Start here. A new **creaite teal** theme matches the guides.
- **One-off Word documents** from the tracker in house style (an actions register, a RAID extract, a decision log), and **permission requests in Ask creaite**: when a request needs one more rule, the panel asks for exactly that rule, and you approve it.
- **Fixes found in a real browser:**
  - Work filter menus closed the instant they opened;
  - a list with no filters came back as "All open" after a refresh;
  - the project page's "Urgent risks" figure opened a different list than it counted;
  - "All mine" kept earlier filters and search;
  - in the Current state editor, new pictures showed "not found" after saving, a removed picture came back, and a failed save lost your text and pictures.

## Before you update

- **Say "update creaite"** in your vault. It shows what changes, backs up first, adds the new settings (`locations`, `help`) and never touches your data, your `CLAUDE.md` or your projects. Then **restart the live server** and refresh the page.
- **Using OneDrive for projects is your choice.** To switch it on, say **"setup"** after the update: its new **Projects folder** step checks the folder, shows the template it found and the folder mapping for you to confirm, adds each engagement as a project, and asks about existing meetings. In Finder, set the Engagements folder to **Always keep on this device**. Claude Code also needs permission to read that folder from outside the vault: setup asks, and adds it only with your yes.
- **New vaults:** run the zip as before; add `--projects <folder>` (or answer the question it asks) to put the projects in the company OneDrive from the start.
- **Prep 1:1 is gone.** Draft email replaces it. Hand over, the closed-in-14-days card and the row warnings stay.

## Known limits

- **Beta,** as above.
- **Loading transcripts from meetings** with one click from Intake still comes next (BL-42 phase 3), once it's tested on the live vault.
- **One machine only:** there is no lock for two laptops sharing the same OneDrive projects folder.
- **The live server is started by hand,** in Terminal.
- **Not yet built:** portfolio-level reports, archiving, unattended minutes, chase-up drafts, decision records with options, and promises and waiting-on.
- **Check once by hand:** paste a copied Draft email into Outlook desktop and Outlook on the web and check the table, the red and amber and the font.

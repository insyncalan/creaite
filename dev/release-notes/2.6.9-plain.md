# What's new in creaite 2.6.9

## What changed for you

2.6.9 contains all of 2.6.8. The headline: the text of your calendar invites is now cleaned by a stronger filter that removes whole join and dial-in blocks, instead of hunting for secret words one by one, and your backups no longer carry your chats or your follow list.

### Calendar invites: a stronger filter
- **Join and dial-in blocks go as a whole.** An invite's Teams, Zoom, WebEx or Meet block, a dial-in number, a join link or a long run of digits is recognised by its shape, in any language, and the block it sits in is dropped together with any passcode beside it. The 2.6.8 word-by-word clean-up stays underneath as a second layer. On the provider invites we tested, nothing leaked and no ordinary line was lost. (#375)
- **Saved calendars are cleaned again at the first start-up after the update.** Each saved meeting carries a stamp of the filter that cleaned it; when the filter changes, the meeting is cleaned once more before anything reads it. This covers the current calendar only. Older copies keep what they held: the dashboard's history snapshots, Prep pages already written, and the backup copies. (#375)
- **Fewer ordinary lines lost after a label word.** Agenda items such as "Pin board", "Token budget", "Pin the roadmap" and "Secret Santa party" no longer take the lines below them with them. The item's own line is still removed, and a number or ID right below it still goes. A heading such as "Password reset – ABC" or "Code review – ABC" is a finished heading. (#383)
- **A line that holds a numbered label ("Meeting ID …") or a provider name followed by a number of six or more digits is now removed** from the invite text. (#383)
- **Nothing you rely on changes.** Today, Prep, drafts, Intake and the follow-up email read the cleaned calendar through one place. Matching a meeting to its project still uses the attendee addresses, and a follow-up email still has its recipients. A private or confidential meeting still shows only its title and times.

### Load the transcript
- **It starts with no context and searches the meeting's day within a limit**, so a failed search can no longer run on. (#382)
- **A failed load is tried once more, and the message says what to do:** "Press Process to try again." The log keeps counts only. (#371)

### Your backups
- **The backup copy of your history no longer includes your chats with ask creaite, their pictures, your follow list or the saved calendar.** New folders are set up that way. An existing creaite folder needs a one-time step: ask creaite to run setup, and say yes when it offers to stop tracking those files (it makes a backup first). If your folder's history already held them, the backup holds a single snapshot instead of the full history. (#384)
- **Old backup copies still hold them.** Daily backup copies made before this update, including the copies in OneDrive, still contain your chats, pictures, follow list and calendar. creaite cannot change a closed copy: delete the old ones when a clean one exists.

- Update from the release zip as usual.
- Reload any dashboard tab that was open before the update.
- Look at Today and Prep once for a few real meetings, to see what the filter keeps.
- Use ask creaite to run setup once, to stop your backups carrying your chats.

### Good to know
- **Ordinary text the filter can lose.** Lines that share a block with a secret or with a join line go with it, by design. A bare figure of seven or more digits (an order or invoice number, a money amount) drops its block. Three numbers in a row on separate lines are read as one split ID. A line that is only "one-time-use" is read as a meeting code. A link on a line of its own is dropped. A long numbered agenda is cut at 1,500 characters. A line with a `sip:` address or an `id@host` address takes its block with it. A "Google Meet link" label is removed together with its join link. Some titles still show as "Meeting" because of a label word in them, and the clean-up now also turns titles such as "Zoom licences 100 200", "Zoom licences 1234567", "Teams renewal 123 456", "Zoom spend 100 200 300", "Webex 2552 123 4567 renewal", "Chime 12345678", "Jitsi 1000000 users", "Zoom ticket 482915", "Teams incident 482915" and "Teams INC0482915" into "Meeting" (a provider name followed by a long number reads as a meeting ID). "Dress code for visitors" and "Cost code changes" still lose their own line (a number directly below them stays), and a line such as "Password for the HR portal" still takes the next one to three lines.
- **Codes the filter can miss.**
  - A code that is a word ("Use falcon to join") or a short mix of letters and digits ("Pass zqsm45").
  - A bare four-digit year used as a code, a number of fewer than seven digits behind a label the filter does not know, and a Meet code inside a sentence with no link.
  - A third separate block of values after a dropped block.
  - A word-only value that sits more than two ordinary lines below a "Passcode:" label, and a bare PIN in a table cell that has only a word as its value.
  - Hebrew labels.
- **Only the current calendar is cleaned again.** The dashboard's history snapshots, Prep pages already written and the backup copies keep what they held until you delete them.

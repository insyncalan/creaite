# What's new in creaite 2.6.6 (plain words)

## What changed for you

2.6.6 contains all of 2.6.5 (also called 2.6.4b). The headline: a mail check now tells you what it is doing, a meeting can no longer be filed under the wrong project, and a round of design decisions makes the screens quieter and plainer.

### The mail check
- **It says what it is doing.** While a mail and Teams check runs, a true line replaces the joke: "Looked in so far: Outlook mail, Teams", with the number of searches and items read and no total. Other runs keep their quips. (#358)
- **It ends with a count.** "Finished. Found 7 new, 4 need a project": the new suggestions this check saved, not everything waiting.
- **No mail subjects or names in it.** While a check runs, the steps show fixed phrases only. Steps drawn from an old chat keep their earlier text.
- **Close the day** ticks its rows one by one from each row's own search, not all at once.

### The right project for a meeting
- **A meeting in a remembered series no longer takes the wrong project.** If you had remembered a series such as "Weekly sync" for one project, a meeting titled "[DEF] Weekly sync" was processed under that first project, so its minutes and items could be filed under the wrong client. The project code in the meeting's title now wins. A meeting with no code still uses the remembered project, and a title with two different codes is left as Not sure. This affects 2.6.4 and earlier. (#362)

### Design decisions
- **Review** shows the "what changes" line at the same size as the title, with emphasis by weight or colour, not size. (#341)
- **Plain titles:** "Open risks and issues", "Progress over time", "Late milestones". (#341)
- **Files** keeps two ways to narrow the list, the tree and Filter, with the view chips folded into Filter. (#341)
- **Quieter screens.** Side-tab accents are replaced by a chip or icon. Projects shows only counts above zero and dates as DD-MM-YYYY. Work loses the glyphs before titles. Intake's three "selected" looks become one and file rows shrink to one line. The Milestones audience switch is quieter. Settings loses the Help card. Activity gets select-all. (#341)

- Update from the release zip as usual.
- Reload any dashboard tab that was open before the update.
- Walk Review, Projects, Work, Intake, Milestones, Files, Activity and Settings once, looking for anything crowded or oddly worded.

### Good to know
- Not in this release: one colour (amber) meaning one thing across the screens, which waits for your yes, and the changes that need the guarded files, which come in 2.6.7.
- A step drawn from an old chat keeps its earlier wording.

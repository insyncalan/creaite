# creaite 1.5.1 (Beta)

A smaller update: **four new themes** (Claude, Apple, Retro Arcade and Thermo Scan, nine in all), the left rail customised with a right-click, a gear for Settings, ask creaite's approval card showing how risky each change is (yellow for settings, red for permission rules, with the way back), and a lighter touch while ask creaite works.

## New

- **Claude theme** (BL-40a): a warm cream page, white cards on cream hairlines, warm near-black text and one coral, after Claude.com's look. Coral marks the current page and tab; buttons, links and text use deeper corals so they stay readable. Dark mode uses the design's own dark warm surfaces. Today's greeting keeps a serif.
- **Apple theme** (BL-40b): white tiles on Apple's pale grey, near-black text and one blue, with flat cards and a blue pill for the main button. Health keeps green, amber and red. Dark mode uses Apple's own dark greys on black.
- **Retro Arcade theme** (BL-40c): a 1980s arcade screen: black, white type, electric blue, bright yellow, lime and neon cyan, cards with a neon border and a hard pixel shadow, monospace headings. The pure arcade colours are kept where they are readable; where they aren't (blue text on black, red on the raised panels) lighter versions are used. Light mode is the same arcade in daylight.
- **Thermo Scan theme** (BL-40d): a thermal camera's display: dark navy, white type, golden yellow buttons and links, monospace headings, and the thermal spectrum (cold blue to hot red) marking the current page and tab. Warnings are orange so they never look like a button. Light mode is the scan printed: navy on white, with blue links and the same gold buttons.

## Changes

- **Settings has a gear** (BL-192): the Settings button at the foot of the left rail is now a gear, so it no longer looks like Today's sun or the Display sliders.
- **Customise the rail with a right-click** (BL-191): the small sliders button at the foot of the left rail is gone. Right-click anywhere on the rail (or press Shift+F10 on a page in it) to show, hide and order its pages; **Settings › General › Appearance › Left rail: Customise…** and ⌘K › "Customise the rail" still open the same panel.

- **Risk tiers on ask creaite's approval card** (BL-194): each change waiting for your approval is marked **Sensitive** (a triangle, on a yellow band: a settings file, a delete or a move, a file outside the vault and the like) or **High-risk** (a shield, on a red band: a permission file, or any edit that changes a permission rule, wherever it is). Under each High-risk change is the way back, with **Copy**. One Approve and one Decline still cover the whole card, and **Activity › What ask creaite changed** shows each change's tier. What ask creaite may reach, and what is refused, are unchanged.

## Behind the scenes

- **Every colour is part of the theme** (the colour audit): the last few fixed colours (the count badges' text, the peek's staged dot and switch knob) are now theme colours, so a theme sets every colour on the page. Your current theme looks exactly as before; this was checked screen by screen.
- **Nine themes**: creaite (the default), Brightwater, Notion, Linear, Cursor, Claude, Apple, Retro Arcade and Thermo Scan, in Settings › General › Appearance › Theme and ⌘K › "Theme:". Each has light and dark, keeps text readable (4.5:1) and shows health as an icon and a word.

## Check once by hand

- **Themes:** Settings › General › Appearance: try each new theme in light and dark on Today, Work and a project's Milestones. Your usual theme should look exactly as before.
- **The rail:** Settings is a gear; right-click the rail to customise it (the browser's own menu still appears elsewhere).
- **ask creaite's card:** ask it to add WebSearch to the allow list and create `~/Documents/test.md`. One card shows the settings change as **High-risk** (red, with the way back and Copy) and the new file as **Sensitive** (yellow). Approve; Activity › What ask creaite changed shows both with their tiers; undo each. Ask for a plain settings change: Sensitive, no rollback line; Decline it.
- **While it works:** ask creaite a question and watch the status line; the steps fold into one line you can open.

# creaite 1.9.5

"One look, and less to wade through." Every screen now draws its buttons, fields, headings and spacing from one shared design scale, with the UI audit's worst findings fixed (dark-mode badges, faint text, missing borders, screen-reader labels), and no screen scrolls past its own bottom. Messages from the people you follow now arrive as one card per conversation, saying what it came to. Items closed long ago archive themselves; old reports and minutes move into `_Archive/` on your yes, and one can be moved back from Files. And menus, pickers and dialogs behave the same on every screen.

## Before you update

- **A full release**, not a pre-release, so "update creaite" finds it with no version named. No schema change and no migration: archiving uses the `archived` flag 1.9.4 added (BL-09).
- **Archiving starts the first day** (BL-10). Once a day, after the daily backup (and, in the live server, after Tidy up), creaite flags as archived every closed or cancelled action and RAID item, and every done or dropped promise, that closed **6 months ago or more**. On the first day after the update that may be many items at once, all in one apply: to see them first, run `system/scripts/run archive.py items --dry-run`, which lists them and changes nothing. It is automatic because the flag is undoable: **Undo** in Activity brings them all back, and nothing is deleted. Decisions, milestones and open items are never archived. It never runs from an ask creaite run or in the development repository.
- **Reports and minutes move only on your yes** (BL-03). Settings › Health offers, once a month, "Archive 14 reports and minutes older than 12 months" (weekly and client reports, minutes and Steerco one-pagers, by the date in their file name). Nothing moves until you Approve the card.
- **New folders, made on first use:** an `_Archive/` inside each engagement folder (`<engagement>/_Archive/<the same folder>/…`) and one in the portfolio folder for its minutes (`_Portfolio/_Archive/<minutes folder>/…`, or `projects/_portfolio/_Archive/…` in the vault). Stored paths never change: links to a moved file keep working. Files in an `_Archive/` aren't searched, and ask creaite can't read them.
- **New settings, added with their defaults** (Settings › Advanced › Archiving): `archive.after_months.actions`, `.raid` and `.promises` (6 each) and `archive.reports_after_months` (12); 0 turns a kind off. Every existing value is kept. **Show archived projects** (Settings › People and projects › Projects and register; the same setting and value) now brings back items archived for their age too, tagged Archived in Work.
- **The guard gains two agreed move shapes** (BL-03 and BL-331; Alan, 14-10-2026, confirmed 15-10-2026), inside BL-09's one `_Archive` exception for `tracker.py apply`: one dated report, minutes or one-pager into its own engagement's `_Archive/` (the portfolio's minutes into the portfolio's), and the exact reverse for **Move back**. Move back has its own route, which only puts the move on the approval card. Anything in `_Archive/` stays on ask creaite's never list, and ask creaite can't make a move back. No permission rule or deny list changes.
- **The guard knows the new archiving script** (Alan, 15-10-2026): `archive.py daily` and `archive.py items` change the record, so a plain ask creaite chat can't run them; `archive.py items --dry-run`, `reports` and `status` only read. `propose.py archive` and `restore` (BL-09) stay read commands: they print or save a spec, and the move itself is the approved apply.
- **Alerts already on Today stay as they were.** From this release, new ones come one per conversation (BL-334). `vip.json` still keeps no message text, and the check still uses only its read-only Microsoft 365 tools.
- **Three Intake names change** to sentence case: "Calendar import", "Upload files" and "Quick capture" (also in Help, Settings and creaite's rules).
- **The health check has 37 checks** (35 in 1.9.4): the archiving of old items, and old reports and minutes.
- **To install:** say "update creaite". With `gh` signed in, creaite fetches the zip itself; without it, download the zip into `system/releases/` first.

## What's new

### One look on every screen

- **The UI audit's fix-first findings** (BL-324), in every theme, light, dark and system:
  - the saffron bar under the wordmark is right when the Mac is in dark mode;
  - strong borders (table headers, Settings' section rules) no longer vanish in the creaite, Brightwater and Notion themes;
  - the rail's red count badges are readable in dark mode (dark text on red, was white on pale red);
  - faint text (dates, hints) and Today's past hours and now-line are easier to see;
  - screen readers hear a column-resize handle's width, and People's and Settings' sortable headings are real buttons, so they sort from the keyboard;
  - buttons, fields and section headings are one size each from page to page (Files' large centred search stays, by design);
  - Help follows the theme's fonts, and a Prep page uses the dashboard's fonts (where installed) and status colours.
- **One design scale** (BL-325): type sizes, spacing (a 4px base), corners, shadows and layering now come from one shared set, so a button, icon button, field, chip or tag looks the same on every page and in every theme. Each theme keeps its colours, and each page keeps its own kind of main action (Today's and Intake's filled button, Review's outlined Approve, Work's tinted "+"); only their size and corners change.
  - **Headings:** Today's section labels are sentence-case headings, like Settings', no longer small capitals. Today's large title stays.
  - **Easier to hit:** checkboxes, the Settings switches and the column-resize handles are at least 24px.
  - **Small fixes:** Projects' "(In 24 days)" reads "(in 24 days)"; the page shown when creaite isn't running uses creaite's fonts and colours.
- **No screen scrolls past its bottom** (BL-333). On Today, Intake and the Prep popup the window could scroll down into empty space, taking the rail with it. Each screen now stops at its last content.
- **Menus, pickers and dialogs behave the same everywhere** (BL-316). Every screen now builds its menus, pickers, dialogs, tabs, filters, the side panel and toasts from the same shared parts, so they all take the same keys (↑ ↓, Enter, Esc, Tab), give focus back to what opened them, use the same wording and empty states, and offer Undo after a change. Most of it looks as before; what you will notice (Alan, 15-10-2026):
  - **Activity's filter** is a **Kind** list, each kind with its count, and a **When** funnel for the dates, as on Work and Review. It replaces the row of type toggles and the two date boxes.
  - **Moving minutes to another project** in Review shows what moves in a dialog, Review's confirmation dialog, before anything changes.
  - **Easier to hit:** a saved view's ▾ on Work and the milestone editor's progress slider are 24px.
  - **The proposal peek at phone width** (#80): at 320px its footer wraps, Save and approve on a row of its own, so every action is on screen.
  - **Dialogs dim the page in dark mode** too: the backdrop is darker there (it barely showed before), the same for every dialog.
  - **Undo inside a dialog:** a toast shown while a dialog is open appears inside it, so its Undo can be reached.
  - **More Undo:** deleting, moving and renaming a saved view, moving a Review item to a project, and the Prep popup's project choice.

### Alerts read the conversation

- **One card per conversation** (BL-334; Alan, 14-10-2026). Messages from the people you follow come to Today as one card per Teams chat or mail thread, never one per message: the chat's or thread's own title (never "Group chat"; without one, the people in it), "updated HH:MM", one or two lines on what it came to, and the actions, decisions and risks in it, each with **Create work item** and **Dismiss**.
  - **It reads the whole conversation:** everyone's messages since the last check, a few earlier ones for context, the title, the people and attachment names. Greetings and chatter are never an item.
  - **Nothing for you:** a conversation with nothing actionable is one quiet line ("… with …: nothing for you"), with no buttons.
  - **It carries on in place:** a later message updates the same card. The check waits up to 15 minutes for a conversation to go quiet.
  - **The project** is matched from the title first, then the people, the attachment names and the content.
  - **Nothing goes to Review unless you ask:** Create work item stages that one item; Dismiss stages nothing. The sweep doesn't propose the same items again from a conversation already summarised, unless newer messages came after.
  - **Unchanged:** the summary and items are Claude's own words, never message text; a private or confidential conversation gets only a neutral line; the word VIP is never shown.

### Archiving

- **Items closed long ago archive themselves** (BL-10). Once a day, closed or cancelled actions and RAID items, and done or dropped promises, closed 6 months ago or more, are archived (the periods are settings). They leave Work, Today and the Excel export; search still finds them, marked Archived, and opens them; **Show archived projects** brings them back, tagged Archived in Work. Editing an archived item brings it back for another full period. A client report that cited one is a saved file and doesn't change. Restoring a whole project (BL-09) leaves items archived for their age archived. Settings › Health › Archiving shows the last run ("Archived 12 items on 15-10-2026").
- **Old reports and minutes move into `_Archive/`** (BL-03). Once a month while there are any, Settings › Health offers **Archive them**: the moves go on the approval card in ask creaite, and on **Approve** each file moves into its engagement's own `_Archive/`, in the same place below it (the portfolio's minutes into the portfolio's `_Archive/`). Links still open; nothing is deleted; **Undo** puts them back and removes the folders it made. Once done, the line stays quiet for the rest of the month. Transcripts, agendas, documents and applied proposals are never archived.
- **Move one back** (BL-331). Files › **Archived** (shown while there are any) lists the archived reports, minutes and one-pagers, each with **Move back**. It goes on the approval card; on Approve the file returns to its own folder, is searched again, can be undone, and isn't offered for archiving again. If a file of the same name is already there, the move back is refused (move or rename that one first; Alan, 14-10-2026). ask creaite can't move one back.

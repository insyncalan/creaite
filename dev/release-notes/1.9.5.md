# creaite 1.9.5

## Changes

- **Messages from people you follow read the conversation, not the message** (BL-334; Alan, 14-10-2026). Today shows one card per Teams chat or mail thread, with its own title (never "Group chat"), "updated HH:MM", a short summary of what it came to and the candidate actions, decisions and risks, each with **Create work item** and **Dismiss** (Dismiss stages nothing). A conversation that carries on updates the same card; one with nothing for you is one quiet line with no buttons; the check waits up to 15 minutes for a conversation to go quiet. The project is matched from the title first, then the people, attachment names and content. Nothing goes to Review unless you make it, and the sweep skips a conversation already summarised unless newer messages came after. `vip.json` still keeps no message text (the summary and items are Claude's own words), and the check still uses only its read-only Microsoft 365 tools.

# creaite 1.5.0 (Beta)

The fifth update. The main change: **milestones get a page of their own, Plan and Movement**, with five clear states, a client view you can show on screen, the history of every move, and (once there is enough history) a likely date range. Around it: a page left open across a server restart says to reload, the peek's staged changes wait for its own Apply, and one Esc leaves a table cell on every platform.

**Still a Beta:** built and tested on sample data. None of it has run on your real vault yet (see "Check once by hand").

## New

- **Milestones: Plan and Movement** (BL-186). The pencil on a project's Milestones card opens the page (it no longer adds a milestone; **+ Add milestone** is on the page). **Plan** puts every milestone on one line you can scroll, achieved ones in green, and a later forecast as a red dashed ring with its date; rows below give each one's state, agreed date, expected date and progress. **Movement** (internal only) shows how each open milestone has moved against its original and agreed dates, a chart by reporting week, what changed and why, your internal note, and **Decide what to do**: hold, rebaseline, add a recovery action or escalate, each staged for Review. Back, Forward, a reload and a link keep the page and the open milestone.
- **Five states** (BL-186): Achieved, On track, At risk, Slipped and Overdue, each with its own shape, used on the page, in the weekly and client reports and in the steering one-pager. The dates suggest one; you can set any, and creaite asks why only when you pick one better than the dates. **Missed is gone:** your Missed milestones become Overdue when you update (you'll see one line in Activity).
- **Internal view and Client view** (BL-186). A switch on the Milestones card (and the page) shows what a client sees: only milestones you have made visible to the client, with your client wording, in their words (On plan, At risk, Expected later, Late, Achieved). A dark **INTERNAL VIEW** or an accent **CLIENT VIEW** mark says which one is on screen. Your choice is remembered in this browser.
- **Each milestone has more to it:** an owner from the people list, the date it was achieved, a percentage you set by hand (never worked out from actions), a description, a client view with its wording, and an internal note. Every date is chosen from a calendar. **A rebaseline records a decision**: who agreed it, why and where.
- **The slip range** (BL-184). Movement gives a likely range for an open milestone ("Likely 27-11-2026 to 11-12-2026") from how your own milestones have slipped, once there are 8 closed milestones that slipped as often; until then it says how many it has. It is internal only.

## Changes

- **All open now lists Open items only; Delayed items have their own Delayed view.** Mine, Due soon and Chase are Open only too, and **Clear all** goes back to All open. Every count that says "open" (a project page, People, a person's page, a meeting's prep) counts Open items only, so the number matches the list it opens; overdue and due-soon counts still include Delayed items, and their links add Delayed to the Status filter.
- **Client reports now list only client-visible milestones** (BL-186), with their wording, in the client's words, the same as the Client view. Mark the milestones a client should see (the milestone's **Visible to client**) before your next client report.
- **A page left open across a restart says so** (BL-174): after the live server restarts, the page's next action shows "The live server was restarted since this page was opened" with **Reload**, instead of failing quietly.
- **The peek's staged changes wait for its own Apply** (BL-189): changing a value in a list, a bulk change or approving in Review no longer applies what you staged in an open item; those wait for the item's **Apply** or ⌘↵.
- **One Esc leaves a table cell** (BL-178): the list for a cell in Work opens in the page, so Esc and Tab work the first time on Windows and Linux too.
- **Activity's search looks further back** (the newest 2,000 changes, not 300); the list still shows the newest 300 until you search.
- **Engine changes:** a database change (seven milestone columns and an internal note; Missed retired), run by the update's migration with a backup first; a new read-only script, `forecast.py`; `/api/apply` takes `hold_peek`; the dashboard template, `build_dashboard.py`, `report.py`, `status_facts.py`, `excel_sync.py` (new Milestones columns), `propose.py`, `review.py` and four skills.

## Before you update

- **Missed is retired.** Any milestone marked Missed becomes **Overdue** when you update. The change is logged, but that one step can't be undone with Undo (Undo update puts the whole version back).
- **Client reports show only client-visible milestones.** Until you mark milestones visible to the client (with their client wording), a client report lists none. Mark them on the Milestones page before your next client report.

## Check once by hand

- **The card:** switch a project's Milestones card between Internal view and Client view; reload; it stays as you left it. The marks say which view you're looking at.
- **The page:** press the pencil on the card. Change a forecast and set a better state than suggested: it asks why. On Movement, rebaseline a milestone, then approve it in Review: a decision is recorded with it.
- **After updating:** a milestone that was Missed is now Overdue.
- **Client view:** mark two milestones visible to the client, with wording, then draft a client report: only those two appear.
- **Restart:** restart the live server with the page open: a banner says to reload.
- **The peek:** stage a change in an item, then edit a cell in the list: the staged change is still waiting.
- **Windows or Linux:** one Esc closes a table cell's list.

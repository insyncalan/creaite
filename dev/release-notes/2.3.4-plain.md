# What's new in creaite 2.3.4 (plain words)

The weekly report gets tidier on screen, and what you export now looks like what you see and is saved where you can find it.

## What changed for you

- **A calmer section menu.** The menu on each section is small, with an icon beside each word, and always fully on screen.
- **A neater health row.** The six-week strip and its dates are exactly as wide as the health flag, and Change is a small button.
- **Eyes instead of dots.** The switch that includes or leaves out a figure, a row or a milestone is now an eye (a crossed-out eye when it is left out).
- **Add more from the tracker.** Under Risks and issues and under Overdue actions, "Add from the tracker" lets you tick open items that aren't in the report yet and add them. Nothing in the tracker changes.
- **HTML exports look like the report.** The health flag and its six weeks, the figures at a glance, the milestone timeline as a picture and the milestone list as a table all keep their structure and format, with no stray headings or empty sections.
- **Exports are saved, and you're told where.** After you export to HTML, Word or PDF, the file is saved in the project's Reports folder, the message names the folder, and you can choose Show in Finder or Open.

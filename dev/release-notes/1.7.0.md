# creaite 1.7.0 (Beta)

The seventh update. creaite's drafts now reach your meetings and your Review: it drafts the agenda and the invite text, the follow-up and chase emails and a project's status in the form each audience needs, and it spots a proposal that repeats an item you already have and an item that is probably done. Around them: a new wordmark at the top of the rail, a quieter Today header with Close the day as a door icon, **Decisions needed** on Today, an ask creaite that reopens at once, queues what you send and applies a change from one card, a dashboard that shows changes within a second, and two security fixes.

**Still a Beta:** built and tested on sample data. None of it has run on your real vault yet (see "Check once by hand").

## Before you update

- **ask creaite now asks before it changes the record** (BL-267, a security fix). In a chat you haven't confirmed it can no longer approve, reject, stage approved items, apply, undo or change the registers: it says what it would do and asks you to confirm, or, for a change to items, shows one card with **Approve** (BL-258). Reading is unchanged. Where ask creaite used to apply something in one step, expect one more click.
- **Make the launcher again after updating** (BL-272): `system/scripts/run launch.py make-launcher`. The browser tab and the installed app's window get the new icon at once; a launcher you have already made keeps the old Dock icon until you do.
- **Minutes with no Word draft can't be approved** (BL-260). If Review holds minutes staged without their Word draft (it happened on 12-10-2026), the health check lists them and Review says "No Word draft was made for these minutes": reject them and process the meeting again.
- **The new AI drafts are on.** Draft agenda, the forms, Polish, Looks like, Probably done and Sharpen each have a switch in Settings › General › AI drafts. A draft runs only when you press its button; Looks like and Probably done are worked out when the minutes or the sweep stage.
- **Word files from the command line are written to drafts only** (BL-271, a security fix). `docx_tools.py fill`, `build-adhoc` and `clean` write only inside `system/staging/drafts/`, as every skill already does; a project's Word file is changed through `filechange.py`, with a backup and undo.
- **The health check has 28 checks** (was 25): staged minutes have their Word draft, refused commands counted, and record changes refused in a plain chat.
- **To install:** say "update creaite". With `gh` signed in, creaite fetches the zip itself (BL-238); without it, download the zip into `system/releases/` first.

## New

### Meetings

- **Draft the agenda and the invite text** (BL-223): **Draft agenda** in a meeting's Prep (and on the meeting's page before it starts) writes a POAD (Purpose, Objectives, Approach, Deliverables), or with **Lighter** a short friendly paragraph, and a timed agenda, ready to paste into the invite. creaite picks the points itself: decisions that need the meeting first, then overdue and urgent work, milestones due and what wasn't covered last time (at most six, timed from the meeting's length). Claude only words the text, and each sentence shows its sources. **Copy** keeps the formatting; **Save to Agendas** keeps it as a file in the project's Agendas folder, Internal unless you save it for attendees, and Coverage reads it. creaite never creates or changes an invite; for a confidential meeting Claude sees only the title and times.
- **Follow-up and chase emails** (BL-224): the Draft email has three kinds. The **follow-up** after a meeting (from its page: **Draft the follow-up email**) has the attendees, a short thanks, the minutes' own summary, the decisions and the actions grouped by owner, the person you choose first. **Chase overdue items** (on a person's Draft email, or **Chase** in an overdue item's right-click menu) steps up its tone with how often you have chased; press **I sent it** after sending and creaite notes the items and the date, so the next one knows and their page says "chased 03-10-2026, once". **Polish** rewords the opening paragraph in a warm, neutral or firm tone, beside yours. The same safeguards hold in every kind, and nothing is ever sent.

### Today and decisions

- **Decisions needed** (BL-210): a band at the top of Today lists each pending decision that has a decision maker and a needed-by date: yours first, marked **Yours**, with **Decide**, then the others, the latest first. Each row says the project, who it waits on, how many days it has waited, when it is needed, and what slips if it stays open (the items and milestones it blocks, from the links you have recorded; nothing is guessed). **Chase** drafts an email to the decision maker with that decision ticked: the chase once it is late, the update request before; nothing is sent. The same rows are on the project page's Decisions needed card and in a steering meeting's Prep.
- **Choose which priorities the Week ahead lists** (BL-235): **Settings › Work and views › Today** has a tick for Urgent, High, Medium and Low. Urgent is ticked by default; tick none to list every priority. Meetings and milestones always show. The change is saved like any other setting, with a backup and a log line, and the Week ahead changes at once.

### Project status

- **One set of facts, many forms** (BL-227): Generate has a **Form**: Current state as before, or a **Steering note**, a **Three-line update**, an **Email paragraph** or a **Teams message**, written from the same ranked facts, with each sentence's sources and a length check, to copy. Two new audiences, **Steering committee** and **Sponsor**, use internal wording with nothing confidential, restricted or marked Client-safe: no. Only the Current state is ever saved.

### Review and capture

- **Looks like an item you already have** (BL-221): when the minutes or the sweep propose something that repeats an action, risk or decision in the tracker, Review says so: "Looks like ABC-A-009 (same project, open) · Very likely" (or **Possible**; never a percentage). Open it to **Compare** the two side by side, **Merge as an update** (any new date, latest update or response goes to the existing item, for you to approve), **Keep as new** or **Reject as duplicate**. In Claude Code: `merge 4`, `keep-new 4`.
- **Probably done** (BL-218): when a mail you sent, a reply, a Teams message or a meeting shows an open item is finished, the sweep or the minutes propose closing it, with the words that show it ("Looks done: Mail 09-10-2026 15:02 by Alan Carter: “attached the SOW”"). **Maybe done** says why it's less sure; **Not done** re-dates an item that has moved. Only from the owner or you (or naming the owner), only in the item's own project, and never closed until you approve it on its own: Approve routine leaves it.
- **Sharpen on capture** (BL-220): as you type a new action (in its form, ⌘K's "add an action" or Quick Capture), chips suggest the owner, due date, workstream, milestone and priority from your words; a click sets one, and nothing is set without it. A vague title ("look into data") offers **Sharpen**: a clear, verb-first title beside yours, to use or discard. Review marks a vague proposed action and offers Sharpen there too.
- **Activity › Quality** (BL-233): what you approve as proposed, approve after an edit and reject, by source (minutes, sweep mail, sweep Teams, dashboard) and type, the reasons you gave, the fields you change most, and how each kind of AI draft is used. Every figure is a count out of its total; nothing is rated until a group has 10. It counts extraction and drafts, never a person, and stays internal.
- Settings › General › **AI drafts** has six new switches: Draft the agenda and invite text, Write the status in other forms, Polish the email wording, Spot repeats of existing items, Spot items that are probably done and Sharpen a vague action.

### ask creaite

- **ask creaite reopens at once** (BL-262): close and reopen the chat, or reload the page, and the conversation is there as it was; nothing is drawn again or typed out, and a request still running carries on with only the new text streaming in.
- **Send while ask creaite is working** (BL-257): a message sent during a request is queued under the chat (Edit, Remove) and runs as soon as the request finishes, in order. A question, a card or an error waits for your answer first (or press **Run now**); Stop clears the queue. Reloading keeps it.
- **A change on one card** (BL-258): asked to change the tracker, ask creaite shows one card with each item's exact before and after; **Approve** writes it at once (with Undo), **Reject** discards it. Nothing else waiting in Review is touched. If the same item already has a proposal waiting, or another job is busy, the card offers **Stage for review** instead.
- **More office quips, slower, and coloured dots** (BL-259, BL-261): 150 quips, each shown for 5 to 10 seconds with no repeats until all have been seen, beside three working dots in your theme's colours.

### The dashboard

- **Changes show within a second** (BL-253): an open dashboard is told the moment a sweep, a minutes run, an apply or a save lands, and refreshes only what changed (a new inbox file refreshes just the file lists). If that connection is unavailable, the page checks every 30 seconds as before.
- **The Stage wordmark** (BL-273): "creaite" at the top of the rail is now the Stage wordmark, IBM Plex Mono with a dashed draft behind it and a saffron bar, in every theme, light, dark and high contrast. The page draws it itself; nothing is downloaded. Retro Arcade and Thermo Scan keep their own lettering.
- **A quieter Today header** (BL-270): **Close the day** is a round door icon at the bottom right of the header. Its tooltip says when you last closed the day, or that it's time to, and a small dot appears after your nudge time; once closed it is in the accent colour. "Calendar as read on …" is now a small footnote at the bottom right of the page.
- **⌘↵ saves in every editor** (BL-274): in a milestone, the project panel, Movement's rebaseline form and an item, ⌘↵ (Ctrl+Enter) does what Save does and closes. A change still waiting for its reason takes you to that field. Each Save button shows ⌘↵.

## Changes

### ask creaite

- **ask creaite asks before it changes the record** (BL-267, a security fix): a plain chat could stage approved items, approve, apply or undo with no card, because the guard checked a command's form, not whether you had confirmed the request (it applied twice unconfirmed on 12-10-2026). Every command of every creaite script is now classed as read, rebuild, stage or record, and in a chat you haven't confirmed the guard refuses every record command, and any command it doesn't know. ask creaite then says what it would do and asks you to confirm. The health check counts these refusals, by command name only.
- **ask creaite finds items by their words** (BL-252): it looks an item up with `tracker.py read … --search "words"` (case-insensitive, with `--project`, `--status open|closed` and `--id`) instead of piping a whole table into `grep`, and when a read-only command is refused for its shape it runs it again in the allowed form, without asking you. An unknown `tracker.py read` option now fails with the valid ones listed.
- **Refused commands, counted** (BL-252): **Settings › System › Diagnostics** and the health check show how many commands ask creaite's guard refused in the last seven days, by shape (a pipe, a redirect, `cd … &&` and so on). Counts only, never the command. A rise means ask creaite's instruction to run each script on its own isn't holding.
- **"refresh calendar" works in ask creaite** (BL-268): it listed the meetings in the chat, but the guard refused the step that saves them, so the dashboard's calendar wasn't updated. A creaite script named by this vault's full path is now read as the usual short form and checked as before (any other folder is still refused), and the sweep's calendar step saves at once.

### Files and safety

- **Word and prep-page writes go through the safety net** (BL-271, a security fix): `docx_tools.py fill`, `build-adhoc` and `clean` could write a file anywhere the guard didn't refuse, with no backup, log or undo. They now write only inside `system/staging/drafts/` and say how to change a project's file instead. A prep page built by cx-prep is saved through `filechange.py`: a new page is logged and can be undone; creaite's own unchanged page is replaced with a backup; after reading outside content it goes on the approval card. The dashboard's Prep page button is unchanged.
- **Text from outside is escaped by default** (BL-228 D): Review, Intake, Files, Activity, Today and ask creaite now build their pages with a helper that escapes every value it is given, so a mail subject, a file name, a transcript line or a reply can only ever show as text. Nothing on screen changes, and Copy still copies the original text.
- **Backups no longer overwrite each other** (BL-266): two saves in the same second used to share one backup name, so the version before the first save was lost. Settings and project-memory backups from the dashboard, and the settings, permission, database, prep-page and update backups made by the scripts, now take a free name (`-2`, `-3` and so on) when the name is taken.

### Minutes

- **Minutes always come with their Word draft** (BL-260): processing a transcript no longer stages minutes without the draft Word file; it stops and names the step to run (a quick note still needs none). A minutes row in Review that has no draft says "No Word draft was made for these minutes: Reject them and process the meeting again" instead of an Edit that opened nothing, and Open source still opens the transcript. The health check lists any staged minutes with no draft.
- **Minutes with no Word draft can't be approved** (BL-260): Approve on such a row in Review is unavailable and says why; `review.py approve` and bulk approval skip it with a message; and an apply refuses it ("No draft minutes to file"), so the transcript isn't filed and the meeting isn't marked processed. The row stays in Review; Reject still works.

### The dashboard

- **Easier to click** (BL-216): every button, link and tick box is at least 24 by 24 pixels to click, or has room around it, without anything moving: Work's ▾ editors, the Display menu's move buttons, **Reject with a reason**, the filter ✕, link buttons and project names. A tick box in Review, Intake or Activity also answers a click just beside it.
- **A narrow window, and high contrast** (BL-217): down to 320 pixels wide nothing scrolls the page sideways. Review's rows stack with their buttons on their own line, the rail turns to icons under 480 pixels, and the project page's footer no longer runs off the edge. In high contrast (forced colours) buttons keep their edges, the selected tab, chip or switch shows in the highlight colour, and ⌘K's field shows focus.
- **Activity's search and Quick Capture's title fit a 320-pixel window whatever the font** (BL-217): they shrink to the room they have, so the view no longer scrolls sideways with Linux's wider fonts.

### Running creaite

- **A new icon: Stage** (BL-272): a dashed draft behind and the saffron record in front, carrying the c, on graphite, replaces the teal c with the spark in the browser tab and the installed app's window and Dock icon. The installed app's title bar is graphite. The dashboard's colours are unchanged. Make the launcher again to get the new Dock icon (see "Before you update").
- **Errors that have stopped no longer warn** (BL-263): the health check and **Settings › System › Diagnostics** show each repeated error with its first and last time. Only one seen in the last 24 hours (`logging.repeat_window_hours`) is a warning; older ones are listed as stopped, "none since". A long message is shortened at a word, never in the middle of a path, and a browser leaving mid-reply is logged as information, not an error.
- **A page left open across a restart stops asking** (BL-269): once it shows "This page is out of date", it sends the live server nothing more until you press Reload, and the debug log has one line for it instead of a warning every 10 seconds.

## For developers (not in the app)

- **The guard files split under pins** (BL-265): `test_guard_pinned.py` records 807 decisions of `reach.py`, `permissions.py` and `filechange.py` (every path and operation, the hook in ask and confirmed modes, refused rules, approvals, undo end to end) in a vault with a fake home folder. Then `reach.classify` and `bash_problem`, `permissions.check`, `settings_problem`, `validate_edit`, `preview_list`, `finish_file_edit` and `cmd_undo`, and `filechange.perform`, `cmd_undo` and `main` are split into steps. Every pinned decision is byte for byte as before, and BL-141's tests are unchanged.
- **The writer and the builders split, finished** (BL-229): each pinned first, then split, with output byte for byte the same: `minutes.cmd_stage`, `build_dashboard.review_queue`, `flow_data` and `hub_record`, `prep_page.context`, `live_server`'s `Run.read`, `docx_tools.fill_xml`, `report.weekly_data`, `weekly_stage`, `client_data` and `adhoc_rows`, and `tracker.check_values`, `run_file_ops` and `rewrite_project`. Functions at complexity 25 or more are under the target of 60 (57 in the baseline, from 81).
- **The public surface documented** (BL-230): docstrings on every script's public functions, `cmd_*` commands and live-server routes, and every route listed in `live_server.py`'s header. Missing docstrings in `system/scripts` fell from 538 to 13 (nested closures in the live server and two functions at their pinned length), held by `check.py`'s ratchet. Docstrings only: no code changed.
- **Screen snapshots** (BL-254): `dev/tests/test_snapshots.py` opens the sample vault at a fixed date, time, time zone and locale and compares the HTML of 21 screens (Today, Work and an open item, Review, Intake, Files, Activity, Projects, a project, Milestones, People, a person, Help, each Settings tab, ⌘K, ask creaite) with goldens in `dev/tests/snapshots/`, failing with a diff. `run_tests.py --update-snapshots [screen …]` records them again, deliberately, with the reason in the commit.
- **Tests run on GitHub automatically** (BL-234): on pull requests and pushes to `main`, report-only (nothing blocks a merge); changes to documents only are skipped and a newer run cancels an older one. Branch and pull-request runs use Ubuntu (Python 3.9 and 3.14); the macOS job runs only on pushes to `main`.
- **Escaping by default** (BL-228 D): step 1 adds `dev/tests/test_hostile_text.py`, which puts script-carrying text in every kind of item, file name, source and chat reply, opens every view and checks nothing runs or is injected (no hole found on main), and the page's `html` tagged-template helper, which escapes every value, `'` included. Step 2 converts Files, Intake, Activity, Review and its proposal peek, Today and ask creaite to it (with `putHTML` and `addHTML` beside it to write the result): `check.py`'s HTML sinks fall from 341 to 240 and `innerHTML` from 247 to 162 in `dev/check-baseline.json`, and every screen snapshot is unchanged. The remaining sinks are for v1.8.0.
- **Two clashing function names, found before release and fixed** (fix-main-170): the live event stream (BL-253) and the decisions-needed rows (BL-210) reused names already in the page, and the later declaration silently replaced the earlier. Clicking a sentence's evidence (BL-207) opened the event stream instead of the evidence panel, and Review's **Probably done** row (BL-218) lost the words that show it. Both are renamed, and `check.py` has a new hard check, JSDUP, that fails when a function is declared twice in the page.
- **The change stream** (BL-253): `GET /api/events` is token-checked, carries part names and numbers only, and is in `QUIET_ROUTES`; `test_live_updates.py` covers it.
- **Smaller:** `run_tests.py`'s flake log names the tests that failed under load and keeps their last 40 lines; test vaults no longer copy the checkout's own `system/config/*.jsonl` logs, and read the committed settings rather than a development copy.

## Check once by hand

1. **After updating:** make the launcher again (`system/scripts/run launch.py make-launcher`) and open creaite from it: the Dock icon, the window and the browser tab show Stage, and the title bar is graphite. `system/scripts/run doctor.py` runs 28 checks; "Staged minutes have their Word draft" passes or lists minutes to reject.
2. **The record guard:** in ask creaite, say "apply": it asks you to confirm instead of applying, with a confirm card. Press **Confirm**: the apply runs and the change shows on the dashboard. The health check counts the refusal by command name.
3. **A change on one card:** "Mark ABC-A-003 done" in ask creaite: one card, Status Open → Closed; **Approve**: the reply names ABC-A-003 and the item shows Closed; Undo puts it back. Again, and **Reject**: nothing in Review.
4. **Reopen and queue:** ask something slow, close the panel and open it again, then reload the page: the conversation is there at once, nothing typed out or faded in again, and the run carries on. While it works, send two more messages: both show as Queued; remove one; the other runs when the first finishes. Ask something that makes ask creaite ask you back, with a message queued: the queue waits until you answer (or press **Run now**).
5. **Quips and dots:** read the 100 new quips (`QUIPS` in `dashboard.html`, after the BL-259 comment) and look at the dots in two or three themes, light and dark.
6. **Find by words, and refresh calendar:** ask ask creaite about "the SOW action": it finds it with no refusal and no question. Say "refresh calendar": Today's meetings update, and the chat doesn't say the calendar wasn't saved. If they don't, **Settings › System › Diagnostics** shows the kind of command that was refused (`refused Bash (…)`): send that line.
7. **Draft agenda:** Today › a meeting's **Prep** › **Draft agenda**: decisions first, times add up to the meeting; **Copy** and paste into a new Outlook invite (not sent); **Lighter**; **Save to Agendas** and find the file.
8. **Follow-up email:** a meeting page with applied actions › **Draft the follow-up email**: To, summary, **Lead with**; copy into Outlook.
9. **Chase and Polish:** a person with overdue items › **Draft email** › **Chase overdue items** › **I sent it**; reopen: "A second reminder", and their page says "chased …, once". Right-click an overdue item › **Chase**. **Polish** (Firm) on any email: only the opening changes.
10. **Forms:** a project › Generate › **Form**: each of the four, then **Steering committee**; Copy; the Current state is unchanged.
11. **Decisions needed:** with a pending decision asked of you and one asked of someone else, both with a needed-by date, the band is above Needs attention, yours first with **Yours** and **Decide**. **Decide** opens it with Made staged; Apply records it and it leaves the band. Link a decision to an action it blocks: its row says "If it stays open:" with the action and its milestone. **Chase** on a late one opens Chase overdue items with only that decision; on one not yet late, the Update request. The project page's card and a SteerCo meeting's **Prep** show the same rows; an ordinary meeting's Prep is as before.
12. **Repeats:** run a sweep (or stage a near repeat of an open action): Review says **Looks like …**; open it, **Compare**, then **Merge as an update**; approve and apply: one item, with the new date.
13. **Probably done:** send yourself a mail closing an open action and sweep: Review leads the row with **Looks done** and the mail's words; **Approve routine** leaves it; approve it on its own.
14. **Chips and Sharpen:** Work › **+**, type "Sara to send the data extract by Friday, urgent" and click a chip; type "look into data", press **Sharpen**, then **Use this**. ⌘K "add an action for ABC: I will send the plan by Friday" shows chips; so does a line in Quick Capture.
15. **Quality:** Activity › **Quality**: counts with their totals; a small group says "Not rated yet".
16. **AI drafts switches:** Settings › General › AI drafts: turn each of the six new switches off in turn; its button greys and says why.
17. **The Week ahead:** Settings › Work and views › Today: tick High; the Week ahead lists Urgent and High actions and RAID items; untick all: every priority.
18. **Live updates:** with the dashboard open, apply a change from Claude Code: it shows within a second or two without pressing anything. Drop a file into `inbox/`: Intake lists it.
19. **A stale page:** leave a page open and restart the live server: "This page is out of date" with Reload, and the debug log has one line for it, not one every 10 seconds.
20. **Minutes without a draft:** if the health check listed any, Review says "No Word draft was made…", Approve is unavailable with the reason, and Reject works.
21. **Repeated errors:** Settings › System › Diagnostics: an old repeated error is listed as stopped, "none since", and isn't a warning.
22. **A prep page from cx-prep:** say "prep for <a meeting>" in Claude Code: the page is in the Agendas folder, and `system/scripts/run filechange.py list` lists it, to undo if you want. Then, in an ask creaite chat that has read a transcript, ask for a prep page from cx-prep: it waits on the one approval card; **Approve** writes it to the Agendas folder, and Activity › What ask creaite changed lists it with an undo.

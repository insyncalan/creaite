## New section, Features › "Project settings" (A and B, if #228 lands)
## Project settings
Where: the cog at the foot of the project page
- **A page of its own.** Search the settings at the top left; the categories are listed below it: **General** (name, client, code, status, folder), **People and partners** (who is on the project, and its partner organisations), **Logos** (the firm's, the client's and one for each partner organisation), **Milestones**, **Reporting** (client report on or off, how often, the reminder, the weekly report day) and **Archive** (archive or close the project; nothing is deleted).
- **Each change saves on its own,** with Undo. A setting here and the same setting elsewhere (the client report, the logos) are one setting.
- **Workstreams** are still managed in Settings › People and projects.

## ask creaite (A and B, if #231 lands): add where the panel is described
- **When it finishes with the panel closed,** a small mark shows on the ask creaite button for 30 seconds: a green tick and **Done**, a grey square and **Stopped**, or a red mark and **Failed** (which stays until you open the panel). Opening the panel clears it at once; it never shows while the panel is open. It does not stop or restart a run, and Esc still never stops one.

## VARIANT B ONLY: replace the Review and Intake sections with one "Intake and Review" section
## Intake and Review
- **One page, one entry, one badge.** The badge counts the meetings ready to process plus the proposals to approve.
- **Three stages.** **Arrived**: a meeting or a file creaite has seen but not turned into proposals (a transcript is ready, still to come, or being drafted). **Drafted**: processed, proposals waiting for you. **Approved**: every proposal decided this week. Mail and Teams proposals are a separate group below, not a stage.
- **Choose your view** with the **List | Board | Two panes** switch: List (counts and one list), Board (a column for each stage, a card for each meeting) or Two panes (meetings on the left, the one you pick on the right). creaite remembers your choice for you, in this browser only.
- **A meeting's row** shows its name, time, project, state and how long it has waited. **Process** runs in the background with progress on the row and ask creaite stays free. **Later** sets it aside until its transcript arrives. **Not needed** (the ✕) asks for a reason and can be undone. **Process all ready** does every ready meeting.
- **Click a meeting's row** to open what came from it; click again to close it. Its items hang under it on a coloured rail, and each group heading starts with the meeting's name.
- **Needs your decision** comes first: draft minutes, reports, new decisions, new risks, health changes, closing updates and anything else approved one by one. **Routine** follows: each has a project and no conflict, and none is a change you approve one by one. **Approve routine (n)** approves only the routine group.
- **Every proposal** shows a kind label (Minutes, Decision, Risk, Issue, Dependency, Action, Update), what it is in larger type, the sentence it came from and **Open source**, which opens the whole file.
- **Mail and Teams proposals** are split the same way and say what changes (an update shows, for example, status Open → Closed). Click one to open it in the side panel: edit its title and fields, see **What changes**, then **Reject**, **Save** or **Save and approve**, with arrows to the next one. Nothing reaches the tracker until you approve.
- **Upload files** stays on this page: drop transcripts or minutes anywhere and they become rows with the same actions.

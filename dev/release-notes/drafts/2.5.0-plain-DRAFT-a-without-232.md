# What's new in creaite 2.5.0 (plain words)

You can edit both reports side by side, add a logo by clicking its place in the report header, open a project's settings on a page of its own, and ask creaite tells you when it has finished.

## What changed for you

- **Edit both reports at once in Side by side.** The internal and the client report each keep their own edits, autosave and save line (for example "Saved · version 2 · Client: Saved · version 1"). ⌘S saves the pane you are in. The panes share one zoom and scroll together, and Esc closes only the open menu of the pane you are in. Nothing from the internal report reaches the client report except through "Match internal" or "Add to the client report". An approved internal report no longer locks the client page.
- **Add or change a logo by clicking its place in the report header,** in both reports. A logo place with no file shows a dashed "Add … logo" tile; a filled one is a button to change it; nothing shows for a logo you have turned off, in Show as exported or in any export. Changing the firm's logo asks first, because every project uses it.
- **A tidier report hub.** The label row (name, status, Match internal, Refresh, Edit again) sits inside each grey pane and scrolls with it, and the week list's dots show each week's health from the project's history.
- **Project settings on a page of its own.** A cog at the foot of the project page (beside Reports and Browse files) opens it: General, People and partners, Logos (one row for each partner organisation), Milestones, Reporting and Archive. Each change saves on its own, with Undo. (If #228 lands.)
- **ask creaite says when it has finished.** If the panel is closed when a run ends, a small "Done" mark shows on the ask creaite button for 30 seconds (a grey "Stopped", or a red "Failed" that stays until you open the panel). It clears at once when you open the panel. (If #231 lands.)
- **Safer settings and tighter client exports.** The client-report setting is read and written only on its own line; saving a project's settings checks it was not changed in another window; messages no longer show file paths; with a project's client report turned off, creaite refuses to build, save, export or approve a client report for it. An unclosed stylesheet address, and picture metadata, can no longer carry hidden material into a client file, and "Match internal" checks what the client may see before it copies a section. (The last three if they land.)

# What's new in creaite 2.5.0 (plain words)

Intake and Review become one page where you see the work move from arriving to approved, you can edit both reports side by side, and ask creaite tells you when it has finished.

## What changed for you

- **One page for Intake and Review.** One entry in the side bar and one badge. Meetings and files move through three stages: Arrived (a transcript is ready, or still to come), Drafted (creaite has made proposals for you to check) and Approved. Mail and Teams proposals have their own group below.
- **Choose how you see it.** List (the stages as counts and one list), Board (a column for each stage) or Two panes (your meetings on the left, the one you pick on the right). creaite remembers your choice for you.
- **A meeting opens in place.** Click its row to see everything that came from it, on a coloured rail so it is clear which items belong to which meeting. Click again to close it.
- **Routine and not routine are split.** "Needs your decision" (minutes, new decisions, new risks, closing updates and anything else you approve one by one) comes first; "Routine" has one "Approve routine" button that never covers the first group.
- **Every proposal says what it is and why.** A kind label, what changes in larger type, the sentence it came from, and Open source.
- **Review mail and Teams proposals in a side panel.** Click one to edit its title and fields, see what changes, and Save and approve, with arrows to the next one.
- **Later and Not needed.** Later sets a meeting aside until its transcript arrives; Not needed asks for a reason and can be undone.
- **Edit both reports at once in Side by side.** The internal and the client report each keep their own edits, autosave and save line; ⌘S saves the pane you are in; they share one zoom and scroll together. Nothing from the internal report reaches the client report except through "Match internal" or "Add to the client report".
- **Add a logo by clicking its place in the report header,** and the report hub follow-ups: the narrower menu, neutral week dots, tidier panes, and safer settings saves.
- **ask creaite says when it has finished.** If the panel is closed when a run ends, a small "Done" mark shows on the ask creaite button for 30 seconds (a grey "Stopped", or a red "Failed" that stays until you open the panel).

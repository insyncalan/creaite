# What's new in creaite (next release after 2.4.0, plain words)

Side by side now lets you edit both reports at once.

## What changed for you

- **Edit both reports in Side by side.** The internal and the client report each keep their own edits, their own autosave and their own save line (for example "Saved \u00b7 version 2 \u00b7 Client: Saved \u00b7 version 1"). \u2318S saves the pane you are in. The panes scroll together and share one zoom. Esc closes only the open menu of the pane you are in. Nothing from the internal report reaches the client report except through "Match internal" or "Add to the client report".
- **An approved internal report no longer locks the client page.**
- **After "Match internal", the client report now autosaves** (it used to wait for your next edit).

## Good to know

- Only one click-to-edit summary box is open at a time: clicking into the other pane closes the first.
- The section menu is one popup, so it can't be open at the same time as an AI menu.

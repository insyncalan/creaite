# creaite 1.5.2 (Beta)

A one-line fix, released quickly.

## Fixed

- **ask creaite no longer trips the guard just by looking inside a file** (BL-202). Asked something like "how often do I check for VIPs?", it answered correctly, then tried to check a settings file with a shell `grep`, which the guard refused and showed you as a warning. Its instructions now say to look inside files with its own Read, Grep and Glob tools, never the shell. Nothing about what it may do has changed: the guard and the allow list are exactly as before.

## Check once by hand

- Ask ask creaite "how often do I check for VIPs?" (or any question about a setting). It answers without a refusal or a warning.
- After updating, restart the live server: ask creaite's instructions are read when the server starts.

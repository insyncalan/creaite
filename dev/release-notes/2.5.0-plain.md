# What's new in creaite 2.5.0 (plain words)

Project settings get a page of their own, both reports can be edited side by side, and the report logos handle more than one partner.

## What changed for you

- **Project settings on one page.** A gear on the project page and in the Projects list opens it (Settings › People and projects stays as a list that links in). It has General (name, client, status), People and partners, Milestones, Reporting, Logos and Archive. Workstreams stay where they were.
- **Partners and their logos.** List a project's partner organisations once; each gets its own logo row, and every logo you tick prints in the header of that project's reports. Add or change a logo from the page, the cog panel or by clicking its place in the report header; the firm's logo is shared by all projects and asks before it is replaced or removed. A "Logos not used" list lets you remove a logo left behind when a partner is renamed.
- **Both reports editable side by side.** In the Reporting hub's Side by side view you can edit the internal and the client report at the same time. Each is saved on its own, and the menus, pickers and shortcuts belong to the pane you are in.
- **Match internal checks first.** Before it copies internal text into the client report, it checks the wording the way export does; a section with internal wording is held back and named, the rest is copied. Internal-only text sections are never copied by "every section".
- **Ask creaite shows when it has finished.** With the panel closed, a small mark appears on the ask creaite button when a run ends (a tick for done, a different mark for stopped or failed). It goes after 30 seconds, or when you open the panel.
- **Safer settings and exports.** Settings that change a project's memory file refuse a file that changed in another window, keep the rest of a line, and never show full file paths in messages. Client exports drop more kinds of hidden content (unclosed styles, damaged pictures, GIF and WebP pictures).
- **Week list colours.** The weeks in the Reporting hub's week menu show their real health colours.

## Good to know

- With a project's client report switched off, the server now refuses to create, save, export or approve a client report for it; existing client reports can still be opened.
- Partner names are 1 to 60 characters (letters, digits, spaces and . , & ' - ( )); other names on the Partners line are ignored.

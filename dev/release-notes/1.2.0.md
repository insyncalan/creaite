# creaite 1.2.0 (Beta)

The second update. Three things matter most: **transcripts load themselves** (Calendar Import shows which meetings have one and brings the transcript and the meeting chat into the inbox), **Work has a board**, and **projects are called by their name** everywhere, with the short code only inside item IDs like `ABC-A-003`. Underneath, ask creaite can now read all of Microsoft 365, and the project and file handling is far wider than in 1.1.0.

**Still a Beta:** built and tested on sample data. The transcript loading and the Microsoft 365 reading have never been run on your real vault yet (see "Check once by hand").

## What's new

- **Transcripts, from the calendar** (Intake › Calendar Import):
  - the daily sweep looks up each meeting in the window and saves what it found, so the list is ready when you open it: **Transcript available**, **Not transcribed**, **No transcript** or **No access**. New meetings and ones still *Not transcribed* are looked up again each day; **Check again** on a row does one now;
  - meetings with an unprocessed transcript are highlighted (a tint, an accent bar and a **Transcript** badge); a **With transcripts** chip shows only those; **Select with transcripts** ticks them;
  - **Load into inbox** saves the transcript as `YYYY-MM-DD Title.vtt` (cues in order, a header with the title, date, organiser and attendees) and the meeting chat as `YYYY-MM-DD Title - chat.txt`; **Load and process** then goes straight on to draft minutes. creaite writes the files itself from what Microsoft 365 returned (Claude never retypes a transcript), and each carries the meeting's calendar ID, so a meeting is never processed twice;
  - attending doesn't matter: if you can read the transcript, you can load it.
- **A Work board:** a **List | Board** switch for Actions and RAID. The same items as cards in columns, with the same filters, search, saved views and sort. Group by **Due** (Overdue, This week, Next week, Later, No date), **Status**, **Owner** or **Milestone**. Drag a card, or use **Move to…**: Due asks for a date (never a past one), Status sets the status, Owner reassigns, Milestone attaches. Each move is one change with **Undo**.
- **Projects by name:** lists, headings, reminders, printed lines, ask creaite's answers, Word reports and drafts, and Draft emails use the project's full name. Report files are now named `YYYY-MM-DD Weekly report - <Project name>.docx` (and Client report); earlier files keep their names. Codes remain in item IDs, in Settings › Projects, in what you type, and at the start of a Draft email subject so your Outlook rules still match. The weekly report's title no longer carries the code.
- **Clear day** (Calendar Import): set a day's meetings, or one meeting, aside so they stay out after Refresh and the next sweep. Cleared is not processed. Undo, a **Cleared (n)** list with Restore, and a question first if a cleared meeting has an unprocessed transcript.
- **ask creaite reads everything:** every Microsoft 365 reading tool (calendar, mail and Teams search, chats and channels, SharePoint and OneDrive search, people, `read_resource`), every vault and project file, and every creaite script's read commands, with no prompts. **Writing is still confirmed in the panel first, and sending, deleting or changing anything in Microsoft 365 stays off.**
- **ask creaite and your files:** it reads every project document, and creates or changes one file at a time when you ask (shown first, the earlier version saved, undoable). Paste, drop or attach up to four images for discussion. Permission files change only through a request you confirm for that one change.
- **Mail, calendar and alerts:** a sweep **ignore list** (Settings › Sweep) starting with ToDo invites, calendar entries beginning "Info:" or "FYI:", HR mail and anything marked private; **VIPs** (a column in the people register, alerts first in Today and Review, a VIP filter in Work); a **Prep page** for a meeting, built from the invite, the open items and the latest reporting, saved in the project's Agendas folder.
- **The dashboard:**
  - **Files** lists the real report folders (Weekly, Ad hoc, Steerco), sets documents aside, and refreshes when you look; **Files search** is a centred bar with an ask creaite button;
  - **Current state** has two resizable panes, saved as one message;
  - the project page lists **open work with no due date** (to push for a date), has a **Flow** card (opened and closed, overdue and ageing over time) and a **Dependencies** card, with early warnings when a dependency's date threatens a milestone;
  - per-project **weekly report reminders** ("Example Programme weekly report for week ending 09-10-2026 is due"), each cleared when its report is filed.
  - **Today** reads more like a brief: dates are written out ("Thursday 15th October"); **Needs attention** adds a line for open work with **no owner** or **no due date**, each count opening Work filtered to those items; **Week ahead** marks **Urgent** and **High** work, flags meetings worth **preparing** for (and why), lists Urgent work that has no date, adds a **Further ahead** fortnight of key milestones and deadlines, and leaves out calendar entries beginning "ToDo", "Info:" or "FYI:";
  - **Calendar Import** greys out meetings that haven't ended yet (later today, or in progress): they can't be picked until they're over, since there's no transcript before then;
  - **/** always opens ask creaite, on every page (Work's search is clicked instead).
  - **Three new themes:** **Notion**, **Linear** and **Cursor**, beside creaite standard, Minimals, Brightwater and creaite teal (Settings › Theme, or ⌘K › "Theme:"), each in light and dark.
- **Meeting times in your time zone:** the sweep converts Microsoft 365's times to your laptop's own time zone itself (Claude no longer does it by hand), and files each meeting under its local date.
- **Decisions that are still to be made:** a decision can be **Pending** before it is made. Raise one in Work › Decisions (**Decision needed**), or let a meeting or a mail raise it. It has who is asked, when it is needed by, and no date until it is made. **Record the decision** opens the form filled in (made, today, the same record and number); **Withdraw** closes it without one. Decisions can also carry their reasoning, the options, a revisit date (or a trigger in words) and the decision they replace. Pending ones show on Today ("2 decisions waiting on others, 1 for you"), the project page, the person's page, the Draft email and the weekly report; one past its date is an early warning, and a revisit date that has come is a reminder.
- **An executive Current state:** Generate no longer retells the work lists. creaite works out a short ranked sheet of facts (the position and whether it changed, what moved, the top risks, the oldest overdue items, what is needed from whom and by when including decisions, figures from your minutes and reports) and writes a short executive status from it: the headline first, the few points that matter, the risks that count, the asks with who and by when. The dialog gains **Audience** (Internal or Client-safe) and **Length** (Short, Standard or Fuller). Small notes under the draft say what to look at and never stop you saving; **Facts it was written from** shows the sheet. **Make it executive** rewrites your own text in the same voice as a draft you use or discard, and notes any date or number it lost or added. The weekly and client reports are written from the same guide and facts. The voice is set out in `system/templates/executive-style.md`.
- **A steering-committee one-pager:** say "steerco one-pager" for a project (or press **Draft the one-pager** in a steering meeting's Prep). creaite drafts one Word page from the tracker: status and trend, what changed since the last one, milestones, top risks, decisions needed, blockers, flow and the next period. There is an **internal** version (sensitive items marked) and a **client-safe** one (only client-visible items, in their client wording). You review it as usual; on approval it is filed in the project's Steerco folder as `YYYY-MM-DD <Project name> Steerco one-pager.docx`, never replacing a file that is there. It never sends anything.
- **Groundwork for later:** the database now has tables for **promises**, **links** between items and **labels** (with **Sensitive** built in), and the people register has a **Labels** column. Nothing uses them yet apart from the one-pager, but a later update won't need another migration.
- **Change a project's code or name:** say "change project ABC's code to XYZ" (or run `propose.py recode ABC XYZ --name "…"`). creaite shows what changes, waits for your yes and applies it on its own: every item ID keeps its number under the new code, history, links, promises, labels and rejected items follow, the old code is retired, and "undo last apply" puts it all back. It refuses while other proposals still mention the old code. You rename the engagement folder; creaite never does.
- **A Decisions board:** Work › Decisions has the List | Board switch too, grouped by Status, Decision maker or Needed by. Drag a pending decision to **Made** and the Record-the-decision form opens filled in (nothing is saved until you save it); to **Withdrawn** and it asks first; to another person or a date column to change who is asked or when it is needed. A decision that is made or withdrawn doesn't move, and nothing goes back to pending.
- **Decisions in meeting prep, and from mail:** a meeting's Prep (the brief and the Prep page) lists the decisions still needed and the ones whose revisit date has come. A sweep that finds a message making or withdrawing a pending decision proposes an update to that decision (the same number), not a second decision, and no longer puts a date on a pending one.
- **"ask creaite"** is written in lower case everywhere, as the product's name.
- **Fresher dashboard, better chat:** a sweep rebuilds the dashboard itself, and an open dashboard notices new items within about 30 seconds (a quiet “3 new items in Review”; if you're editing, a chip waits instead of reloading). ask creaite's replies are formatted (lists, tables, code with Copy, links; only web and email links work), and every chat bubble shows its time, with day dividers.
- **creaite as an app:** a launcher opens creaite in its own Chrome window and stops the server when you close it.
- **A debug log:** one plain-text file a day in `system/logs/`, the last seven days kept. Every script run, the live server and the dashboard's own errors are logged, and so are the working scripts' main steps (apply and undo, the sweep, minutes, review, reports, Excel): IDs, counts and paths, never the text of mail, minutes, notes or chats. **Settings › Diagnostics** lists the latest errors and has **Copy diagnostics**; `doctor.py` and the session-start line mention repeated errors; ask creaite can read the log.
- **Backups in their own folder:** the OneDrive copy of the daily backup now goes to `_Portfolio/99 System/creaite backups` (made the first time). `99 System` is creaite's own folder: never listed, searched or read, including by ask creaite.
- **Steadier checks:** the release check runs on Linux, and the tests that flickered under load wait for what they need.

## Before you update

- **The first update migrates the database.** The decisions table is rebuilt so a decision can be Pending (a backup is made first; every existing decision becomes Made and keeps its date), and four new tables are added. Running it again changes nothing. The people register gets its **Labels** column the next time it is saved.
- **Say "update creaite"** in your vault. It shows what changes, backs up first, adds the new settings and never touches your data, your `CLAUDE.md` or your projects. It also **removes two permission rules creaite shipped before** (`Write(.claude/settings.json)` and `Write(.claude/settings.local.json)`, which warned on every run; `Edit(…)` covers the same ground) and adds the Microsoft 365 reading rules. Your own rules are never removed. Then **restart the live server** and refresh the page.
- **The sweep needs one more step to learn about transcripts.** After the update the Calendar Import transcript column says "Not checked yet" until the next sweep (or say "refresh calendar") has looked each meeting up.
- **The backup folder moves.** If you never changed it, the update moves the backup copy's folder to `_Portfolio/99 System/creaite backups` and shows that before you confirm; a folder you chose yourself is left alone. The copies already in `_Portfolio/creaite backups` stay where they are: once the new folder has a copy, `doctor` tells you the old one can be removed.
- **Report file names changed** as above. Nothing is renamed; the new ones are used from the next report.
- **Anything you said or scripted that relies on `ABC weekly report …` wording** in reminders now reads with the project name; the command to say still uses the code.

## Known limits

- **Beta,** as above.
- **Not built yet:** swimlanes and hand-ordering on the board, dependencies beyond the milestone link (the link table exists, with no way to attach yet), the sensitivity label's warnings and exclusions (BL-94: the labels exist and the client-safe one-pager honours them, nothing else does), portfolio-level reports, archiving, promises and waiting-on (the table exists and can be filled from Excel or an edit, with no lists or capture yet), and meeting prep listing decisions needed.
- **One machine only:** there is no lock for two laptops sharing the same OneDrive projects folder.
- **The live server is started by hand** (or by the launcher).

## Check once by hand

- **Load one real transcript:** in Calendar Import, tick a meeting marked *Transcript available* and press **Load into inbox**. Open the `.vtt` and the chat file in `inbox/`. If a status looks wrong, say what you saw.
- **ask creaite and Microsoft 365:** ask it "what's on my calendar tomorrow?" and "who was in Tuesday's steering?". It should answer without asking for permission, and refuse to send or change anything.
- **Raise and record a decision:** in Work › Decisions press **Decision needed**, then open it and press **Record the decision**; check it keeps its number and that Today and the project page stop listing it.
- **Read an executive Current state:** press Generate on two or three real projects (Internal and Client-safe, Standard length) and read the drafts and the notes under them: is the headline right, is the one thing that matters the right thing, are the asks real? Then press **Make it executive** on something you wrote yourself and check it kept your facts. If the wording is off, say what you saw; the voice is one file (`executive-style.md`).
- **Open a steering one-pager in Word:** draft one for a real project (internal and client-safe) and check it fits on one page and that the client-safe one holds nothing internal. The lists are capped (8 changes, 7 milestones, 5 risks, 6 decisions, 6 blockers, 8 actions) to keep it to a page, but it has not been rendered in Word.
- **A Draft email and a client report** look right with the project's name (and the Draft email's subject still starts with the code).

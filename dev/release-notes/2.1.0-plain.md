# What's new in creaite 2.1.0 (plain words)

Reports, minutes and notes now live inside creaite, and the editor, Today and a handful of annoyances got better. Written for you, not for engineers; the technical notes are separate.

## Reports and minutes, in creaite

- **The weekly report, in creaite.** A proper A4 page you edit in place, with twelve sections you can move, hide and rename. It saves in creaite's own format, and you export it to HTML, Word or PDF. Nothing is ever sent.
- **The client report.** The same week, seen as the client sees it: only client-visible items, in the client's words, never anything marked Sensitive, and a check before every export.
- **Meeting minutes, in creaite.** A processed transcript becomes draft minutes on a page, with the transcript beside them and a time tag on every line. The actions, decisions and risks are rows in the page, approved unless you remove them. Type a deadline in words ("next Friday") and it works out the date. Approving is one step with Undo.
- **A Reports page and a reporting space on each project,** with a card on the project page and a Friday reminder.
- **The meeting page is gone.** Its jobs moved: the meeting's Prep, its minutes and Review. Old meeting links still land somewhere sensible.
- **✦ on a report section** redrafts it, and you accept or keep yours. On the client report it can only use what the client may see.

## Notes

- **A tidier Notes header:** a "+ New note" button like Work's, "+ Tags", and a date you can type in words ("last monday").
- **Delete a note.** It moves to the project's archive, you confirm it, and one approval card makes the move. You can restore it from Files › Archived.
- **A better editor:** checklist boxes line up with their text, the "/" menu opens where you typed, three heading levels, "#" adds an item with its type and title, and one toolbar that fits on one line.

## Today, Review and the small things

- **Today** keeps its content at 80% width, centred. The long "Urgent work…" line is gone, Week ahead can be hidden like Resolved, and Top risks and issues show two lines of the resolution action.
- **Generate current state** is wider, with the settings in one quiet strip, a roomy Steer it box and Draw on folded away.
- **VIP threads** show as cards: what kind of message, what it asks, when it is due, who owns it, and which item it matches.
- **Review's multi-select bar** follows you as you scroll.
- **Smaller fixes:** ⌘↵ saves any dialog; the Latest update box grows as you type; Esc no longer leaves a panel highlighted; the health form opens over the page; "Show changed only" is gone from Settings; two more switches stay visible in forced colours.

## Safer and clearer

- **Runs that read outside content are locked down further.** They cannot write files or use built-in tools, and every run names the tools it may use.
- **Client-safe checks tightened.** A report redraft can only use client-visible items, and a stray hand-staged change can no longer remove or copy a note.
- **Clearer messages:** a refused Apply says what to do; a Health fix that only raised an approval card says it is waiting on the card; the live server says why it stopped; the first page after a restart is quicker and Settings stops re-checking.

## Not in this release

The margin sparkle and hover AI bar, links in editors, Notes autosave and Notes focus mode are in 2.2.0.

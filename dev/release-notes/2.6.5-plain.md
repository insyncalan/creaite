# What's new in creaite 2.6.5 (plain words)

2.6.5 contains all of 2.6.4. The headline: Intake leads with the work to decide in all three views (the meetings still waiting for a transcript fold away), a clear Intake says what to do next, People has a Display menu like Work's, saving from an item's panel is confirmed calmly, the ask creaite box has a plain border when you click into it, and a set of design fixes (bigger click targets, no one-word last lines, tidier wording). One thing goes: Work's List no longer has "Show: Empty groups". A few older wordings and paths are still on screen in places; they are listed under Good to know.

## What changed for you

### Intake
- **Intake leads with the work in all three views.** In the List, the Board and Two panes, the meetings with something to decide come first: Drafted and Approved, then what came from mail and Teams, then Arrived. The long tail of meetings still waiting for a transcript starts folded under "Waiting for the transcript", with its count and a Show button (Hide folds it again). It opens by itself when you ask for it: the Arrived box, Show: Waiting for the transcript, or a search. With a Group by chosen, nothing is folded. The Board puts the open meeting's suggestions above its columns. (#340)
- **Mail and Teams shows five suggestions, then "Show all".** The rest are one press away ("Show all 12", then "Show the first 5"). Those that need your decision come first, and Approve routine counts and approves only the routine rows you can see. (#340)
- **A fold never leaves a tick behind it.** Folding the waiting tail clears the ticks, so the bar above the list cannot act on a meeting you cannot see. Esc in the search box and Group by clear the ticks too. (#340)
- **A clear Intake names the next job.** When nothing is ready to process and no suggestion is waiting, Intake says "Nothing is ready to process or approve." and then one next job with one button: "Next: refresh the calendar" (Refresh calendar) when the calendar has not been read today, otherwise "Next: check mail and Teams" (Check now) when today's check is due, otherwise "Next: open tomorrow's meetings" (Open tomorrow's meetings) when the saved calendar has tomorrow. With none of those, it says only the first sentence. Refresh calendar and Check now are greyed out with the reason while ask creaite is busy or creaite isn't running; Open tomorrow's meetings only moves you to Today's tomorrow, so it is never greyed out for busy. (#356)
- **Later and Not needed appear only when they hold something.** No more "Later 0 Show" and "Not needed 0 Show". (#356, #343)

### Today
- **The Week ahead offers tomorrow.** When nothing is due or booked and you are looking at today, it says "Nothing is due or booked until Monday. Next: look at tomorrow." with a Show tomorrow button, but only when the saved calendar has meetings for tomorrow itself (never the next saved day). (#356)
- **Step 1 of the first-run checklist is shorter.** "Connect it in Claude itself; this page can't. The first mail and Teams check confirms it." (#343)

### People
- **A Display menu, like Work's.** Press D or the sliders button beside the filter. Group by None, Organisation, Project, Role, Overdue or Details, with one Sub-group. Order by Name, Email, Organisation, Role, Actions, RAID, Decisions or Overdue (the default, most first), and the ⇅ button reverses it; clicking a heading is the same choice. Properties are tick boxes with up and down arrows to choose the columns and their order (Name always stays first). Reset to the default puts it all back; Done or Esc closes it and returns you to the button. (#360)
- **Groups.** Each group has a heading with its name, "n people" and "n overdue", and a chevron to fold it. A person on two projects is listed under both when you group by Project, and "Showing x of y" still counts people, not rows. (#360)
- **It is remembered in this browser only.** The grouping, the columns, the order and the folded groups are not written to the people register or to Settings. Another browser or laptop starts from the default. (#360)
- **Organisation, Role and Projects keep whole words.** At 900 px and at 1400 px no word is broken in the middle of these columns, and the table still fits with Overdue on screen. (#343, #360)
- **Help.** The key table says D opens the Display menu on Work and People, and People's section has a line about it. (#360)

### Work
- **Work's List loses "Show: Empty groups".** This is a choice you gave up: in the List, a Group by now shows only the groups that have something in them, and there is no tick box to bring the empty ones back. The Board keeps "Show: Empty groups". You accepted this on 24-10-2026. (#343)
- **The Overdue group says overdue once.** Its heading no longer repeats the overdue count. (#343)
- **The Display menu is drawn from shared pieces.** Work's menu looks and works as it did; People's is built from the same pieces. (#360)

### Saving
- **A calm confirmation after you save.** When you press Save on the bar that says "N changes waiting to be saved", the bar is replaced by "Saved N changes" in the soft "ok" tint with one Undo, and nothing moves. It stays for 10 seconds, longer while you hover over it. The line "A backup was taken first" is now the hover text rather than part of the message. If you make another change while it shows, the waiting bar comes back and the message becomes "The earlier save can still be undone in Activity". If only part of the changes saved, you get the plain message with its Undo and no "ok" tint. (#357)
- **The ask creaite box has a plain border when you click into it.** The border that turned slowly is gone; the box gets one accent-coloured border while it has focus. The dots on the ask button and the typing dots are unchanged. (#359)

### Design fixes
- **Click targets.** The help (?) button, the evidence links, the row ID links in Work and the Open source links in Review are at least 24 px, and nothing is under 12 px on the screens that were changed. (#343)
- **Wording and repetition.** Paragraphs are set so they rarely end on a single word on its own line. Review's line under a suggestion joins what it shows (where it came from, the match, Open source) with one dot between each and leaves no stray or doubled dots. (#343)
- **Cockpit.** At 900 px the health numbers share one row, so "Early warnings" is no longer alone on a second line. In Top risks and issues, the priority and the type are separated by a dot, the title is in bolder type, and the owner follows after a dash: "You" for your own, or "No owner". (#343)
- **Files.** A file's type is said in words: Word, Transcript, Excel (and PDF, PowerPoint and so on). The Type filter and its chip use the same words and still match the same files. (#343)
- **Display headings.** The headings in Work's Display menu are now second-level headings, so the page no longer skips a heading level. You see no change. (#343)

## Good to know

- A dashboard tab left open from before the update gets a "reload the page" message instead of deciding anything. Reload it.
- The calm "Saved N changes" is for the Save bar only. Approving in Review still shows its plain message with Undo.
- Work's List has no "Show: Empty groups" any more, as said above.
- Not in 2.6.5 (minutes): the other six #333 fixes wait, among them the count in the toast after Approve minutes (it can say more work items registered than were saved when a ticked row is held back in Review) and the long dates ("23 October 2026") in the minutes headers, which need your decision.
- If Approve minutes stops after you confirmed where to file the minutes (for example a change to the Track column is turned away), the note "Filed as …" can stay on screen although nothing was filed. Close it and try again.
- With the weekly AI budget switched on and set to ask first, a message waiting in the queue can start after you answer a card without your pressing Run anyway. The budget is off unless you switch it on.
- Still on screen in a few places: a file path or a command (the hover on the portfolio folder in Files, the folder shown on a project's Settings page, the detail lines of some Health checks).
- Help still says past days on Today show each meeting's follow-up ("Transcript ready", "Minutes made", "Check", "Process"). That is out of date since 2.6.4; use Intake for those jobs.
- Still wrong in one place: after you edit an item, the top says "Saved for Review" while the bar below says nothing is saved until you press Save.
- On People, an email address can still break across two lines when the window is narrow; the other columns keep whole words.
- The figure check does not look at the year when the transcript gives a date as a day and month in words: "20-11-2027" passes against "the 20th of November". A month word that is a verb ("may") can also pass as the month.
- In a rare crash recovery (a stopped run that started its work in the same minute as an earlier suggestion), the earlier minutes can drop out of Review with nothing on the page showing it.
- Older words you type still work. Help's command table still lists them.
- Not in 2.6.5: fetching transcripts as they appear, the shared-database work (paused).

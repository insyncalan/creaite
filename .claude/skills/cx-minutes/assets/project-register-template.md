Notes:
- **Code:** 3 to 7 capital letters or digits, starting with a letter (e.g. `ABC`, `ORX2C`); unique and never reused, including retired codes. Used in email subjects (`[ABC]`), meeting titles, channel names and project folder names (`projects/<CODE> <Name>`).
- **Key stakeholders:** list only people who are mainly on this project. People who span projects go under Shared.
- **Email domains:** list client or partner domains only. Never list your own company's domain.

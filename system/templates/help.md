# Start here

*creaite {version} · first day*

**Claude drafts. You approve. creaite keeps the record.**

creaite reads your meetings, mail and Teams, proposes the actions, decisions, risks and issues it finds, and keeps them in one project record once you approve. Everything stays in one folder on your laptop.

## The loop

Every piece of work follows the same five steps. Only step 3 needs you, and nothing reaches your project record without it.

1. **Material arrives** (In):
   - Meetings in your calendar, on Intake (Process)
   - Transcripts and minutes you add to Intake (Add files, or drop them on the page)
   - Notes from a call or your own thinking (a note, [[⌘⇧N]]), turned into items with Find updates
   - The daily mail and Teams check
2. **Claude drafts** (Propose): Draft minutes, plus suggested new items and updates to open ones, each citing its source. It matches every item to a project and owner.
3. **Review** (You) {you: The only gate}: On Intake, approve, edit or reject each suggestion. Routine items can be approved together (**Approve routine**); minutes, new decisions and new risks are approved one at a time.
4. **Save** (Record): Save writes what you approved to your project record and files the minutes in the project folder. Every save can be undone.
5. **See and report** (Out): The dashboard updates at once. Weekly and client reports are drafted from your project record as creaite pages, and you approve them before they're filed.

The next meeting starts the loop again. When a meeting discusses an item that's still open, Claude proposes an update to it, never a duplicate.

## Your first hour

*Once*

About an hour, most of it telling creaite about your projects and the people on them.

1. **Install**: Download **creaite-{version}.zip** from the release, not "Source code". Run `python3 ~/Downloads/creaite-{version}.zip` and choose a folder outside OneDrive and iCloud.
2. **Say "setup"**: Open the folder in Claude Code, connect Microsoft 365, and say ``setup``. It asks before each step: git, your name and role, projects, people, a memory file for each project, and the **creaite** launcher (say yes).
3. **Open creaite**: double-click the **creaite** app in your Applications folder (or `creaite.command` in your creaite folder). It starts creaite and opens creaite in its own Chrome window. If you have no launcher yet: in Terminal, `system/scripts/run live_server.py`, then open the address it prints in Chrome or Edge, not Safari.
4. **First check**: Let setup run the first check over the last 14 days, or press **Run the first check** on Today. Then open Intake and approve what's right. Until set-up is done, Today shows a short checklist of these steps, each with its button. **Settings › Health › Set-up** runs the health check and rebuilds the views from the page, and says how to do the two things it can't (connect Microsoft 365, make the launcher), with **Copy** and **Check again**.

## Every day

*Every day*

**The rhythm**

- **First session after 07:00**: **It catches up.** creaite backs up your project record, runs the mail and Teams check if it's due, and lists reminders and what's waiting.
- **After a meeting**: **Bring it in.** On Intake, press **Process** on the meeting (or **Add files** for a transcript or minutes you already have), then check the suggestions it makes.
- **Whenever it suits**: **Review and save.** Clear Review in minutes. Then check Today for what's overdue, due and changed.
- **Friday from 12:00**: **Report.** A reminder to run the ``weekly report``. Client reports come on each project's own schedule.

## Where things are

*The dashboard*

Every edit you make on the dashboard is written as a suggestion and saved at once, with Undo.

- **Today**: A morning brief: your meetings, what needs attention, your commitments, what was resolved, the week ahead
- **Intake**: one page for what has arrived and what waits for your say-so (a note is [[⌘⇧N]], from any page)
- **Projects**: Current state, health checked against evidence, milestones, early warnings, and each project's Notes
- **Reports**: Every project's week at once: its internal report and each client's report, with a count of what needs you
- **Work**: Every action, risk, issue and decision, with filters and saved views
- **People**: Each person's open work, Draft email, and handover
- **Files**: Minutes, transcripts, documents and reports, with search that answers with sources
- **Settings** (the gear): Daily check time, reminders, health rules, registers, themes

Keys: [[⌘K]] search or jump anywhere · [[⌘J]] ask creaite, the assistant · [[J]]/[[K]], [[A]], [[R]] move, approve and reject suggestions on Intake

## Four rules

*Never bends*

- **Local only**: All project data stays in your creaite folder on your laptop. Never cloud folders, never shared.
- **You approve every write**: Claude proposes; nothing reaches your project record until you approve and save. Scheduled runs only propose.
- **A source on every row**: Every item names the meeting, email or chat it came from, so you can always check it.
- **Client-safe by default**: Client reports show only what you've marked client-visible. Confidential material stays out.

# How do I…

*creaite {version} · how-to*

**How do I…**

The common jobs, step by step. Each card says where to go on the dashboard, and what to say instead in Claude Code or ask creaite ([[⌘J]]).

### Every day

## Start the day

Where: the creaite app · Claude Code · Today

1. Open creaite: double-click the **creaite** launcher (it starts creaite). Without it: `system/scripts/run live_server.py` in your creaite folder in Terminal, then open the address it prints in Chrome or Edge.
2. Open your creaite folder in Claude Code. The first session after 07:00 backs up your project record, runs the mail and Teams check, and lists reminders and what's waiting.
3. Read **Today** from the top: **Decisions needed** (when any wait on someone), **Needs attention**, **Your commitments** (with **You owe** and **Waiting on** when there are any, and at its foot one quiet line saying what waits in Intake), **Messages** (when there are any), **Week ahead** and **Resolved**.

Or say: `sweep` to run the check again at any time.

## Close the day

Where: Today · ⌘K

1. At the end of the day, press the door icon at the bottom right of Today's header (its tooltip says **Close the day**), or type "close the day" in [[⌘K]]. A short card opens with four steps, the same every evening, and takes about three minutes. Move with **Back** and **Next** or the ← and → keys; Esc is **Not yet** and keeps your place for later.
2. **Catch up.** If the last check is more than 30 minutes old, a catch-up check of your Inbox, Sent Items and Teams starts as the card opens. It shows what it is reading, with **Stop** and **Skip**, and runs on while you read the next steps. When it ends it lists what it found. It only proposes items for Intake: nothing is saved and nothing changes in Microsoft 365. Swept recently, it says "Caught up at 17:41" with a quiet **Check again**. Settings › Essentials › Your day can make it ask first or turn it off, and sets the 30 minutes.
3. **Today.** What moved today and what you promised. A promise still waiting on Intake has **Add it to your commitments**, which approves it there.
4. **Loose ends.** Everything still open: the check's new items first, then other suggestions in Intake, files and transcripts waiting there, and your actions and RAID items due today or overdue. Give each a place: **Review now** (after you close, Intake opens with them highlighted, or Work), **First thing tomorrow** (the default) or **Leave**. A long list shows the first eight and "and n more", and one row sets them all at once. Nothing is approved or rejected here.
5. **Tomorrow.** The next working day's meetings with their prep pages. **Make prep page** queues one to be made after you close, one at a time. On a Friday it adds the weekly report.
6. Press **Close the day**. It ends on "Day closed at 18:04. Everything has a place." with the counts. Closing while the catch-up check is still running closes the day when it finishes, and anything it found that you didn't place goes to First thing tomorrow. Only the time and references to your choices are noted (a small file kept by creaite); nothing is written to your project record and nothing is sent. Steps with nothing in them are passed over on a quiet day.
7. The next morning, **First thing** heads Today's brief with what you put there that is still open, and the session-start line in Claude Code lists it too. It is cleared when you close that day.
8. To be reminded, set **Evening, offer Close the day from** in Settings › Essentials › Your day (17:30 by default; empty means no reminder). After that time a small dot appears on the door icon (never a pop-up), and its tooltip says so. Once the day is closed the icon is in the accent colour.

Or say: `what did I promise today?` `what needs prep for tomorrow's meetings?`

## Review and approve suggestions

Where: Intake

1. Open **Intake**. A meeting with suggestions opens in place, with its suggestions under it in two groups, **Needs your decision** and **Routine**. Mail and Teams suggestions have their own group below the meetings, and any other suggestion sits under **Other suggestions**.
2. Each suggestion is one row: what it is (Action, Risk, Decision, Update, Minutes), what changes, whether it is routine or why it needs you, and its source. Hover the line for the owner, priority and due date, which the open suggestion also shows. A quote shows what it came from. **Search** at the top of the page finds a meeting or a mail suggestion by its title, project or source.
3. Press **Approve** or **✕** on the row, or click the row to open it over the page like a work item: your edits stay in it until you choose **Save**, **Save and approve**, **Reject** or **Cancel** at its foot ([[⌘↵]] saves). **Previous** and **Next** step through the suggestions on the page. With a row focused, [[↑]] and [[↓]] move, [[Enter]] opens it, [[A]] approves and [[R]] rejects with a reason; [[Esc]] closes the open suggestion first, then the meeting.
4. **Approve routine** (in the Routine group's heading) approves every routine suggestion in that group in one go, with one **Undo**. Minutes, new decisions, new risks and items that close something are approved one by one, and the first group is never touched.
5. A suggestion that needs a project says so; choose it with the **Project** chip in the open suggestion. creaite may offer to remember what decided it, so similar items match on their own next time.
6. Draft minutes are approved with **Approve** on their row (it needs creaite). Minutes are drafted as a page in creaite's own format: **Open draft** opens the page in your browser, and its decisions, risks, issues and actions are drawn from the items under it on Intake. Minutes drafted in Word open in Word to edit first, and **Approve** checks and cleans the Word file. Once filed, minutes are never saved over.
7. **Wrong project?** Open the suggestion and choose another in its **Project** chip, beside Owner and Due. Its old project's milestone and workstream are cleared, and it waits for your decision. Updates to items already in your project record stay where they are.
8. **Looks like an item you already have:** a suggestion that repeats an action, risk or decision in your project record says **Looks like ABC-A-009 (same project, open) · Very likely** (or **Possible**, never a percentage; another project's name, or **closed**, when that's where it is). Open it for **Compare** (the two side by side, with the new source), **Merge as an update to ABC-A-009** (any new due date, latest update or response goes to the existing item, as an update you approve; with nothing new it is rejected as its duplicate), **Keep as new** (the mark goes) or **Reject as duplicate**. Merge is offered only for an open item in the same project.
9. **Update an existing item instead:** when a new action, risk, issue or decision is really news about something you already track (and nothing marked it, or it marked the wrong one), open it and press **Update an existing item instead**. A list of that project's open items of the same kind opens, the best matches first (the one it looks like, then the same thread, people and words, in its time window), each with how likely it is in words, its owner and due date. Choose one ([[↑]]/[[↓]] and [[Enter]], [[Esc]] to close): the suggestion becomes an update to that item, carrying its date and latest update (or response), and waits for your approval as usual; with nothing new to carry it is rejected as a duplicate of it. **Undo** in the message puts it back as it was suggested. It needs creaite.
10. **Reject with a reason:** one menu from a row's ✕, [[R]] or the open suggestion's **Reject**: **Reject this because**, then **Not an action**, **Duplicate**, **Chatter**, **Already done**, **Wrong project**, **Doesn't need tracking** and **For another team** on [[1]] to [[7]], **Use each guess** ([[G]]) and **No reason** ([[0]]); [[Esc]] closes it. **Use each guess** rejects the row with creaite's own guess for it. **Duplicate** records the item it repeats, or asks which (the look-alikes, an ID typed, or none). Minutes, reports and file changes are rejected one at a time, without a reason.
11. **Every reject can be changed:** the message has **Undo**, which puts the row back on Intake. **Activity › Quality › What you rejected** lists recent rejects with their reason: change it, undo the reject, or **Approve instead**, which undoes it and approves it in one step. Minutes, new decisions, new risks and anything else approved on its own go back to Intake and open for their own approval. The reasons are counted in Quality and read by the minutes and the check as guidance.
12. **Looks done:** when a mail you sent, a reply, a Teams message or a meeting shows an open item is finished, the check or the minutes propose closing it. The row leads with **Looks done** (or **Maybe done**, with the reason) and the evidence ("Mail 09-10-2026 15:02 by Alan Carter: “attached the SOW”"); **Open source** opens the message. **Approve** closes it; **Reject** with a reason says why, and the reading for items reads it next time. Closing is always approved on its own, never by **Approve routine**. **Not done** re-dates an item that has moved.

Or say: `review` `approve minutes for the ABC steering` `9 is DEF` `minutes 3 is DEF` `reject 4 as a duplicate of ABC-A-009` `reject 3, 5 as chatter` `reject 2-6 with their guesses` `approve that reject instead` `merge 4` `keep-new 4` `4 is an update to ABC-A-009` `apply`

## Find anything

Where: ⌘K · Files › Search everything

1. Press [[⌘K]] and type: a project, an item ID, a person, a view, a milestone or a file name.
2. To search inside meetings, reports and documents, use **Search everything** at the top of Files. Codes, names, types and dates such as "last month" become filters.
3. Type a question ("what did we agree about the pilot?") and press Enter. Claude answers from your files, with a source on every point.
4. [[⌘K]] makes changes too: see [Change an item from ⌘K](#change-an-item-from-k).

Or say: `how is ABC going?`

## Change an item from ⌘K

Where: ⌘K, anywhere

1. Press [[⌘K]] and type the change in words: "move ABC-A-002 to fri", "mark the UAT room action done", "Omar owns the SOW action", "reopen ABC-A-004", "set ABC-005 priority to high" or "add action for ABC: send the SOW, Sara, Friday". An item can be named by its ID or by words from its title.
2. The change is listed first, marked **Change**, and the preview under the list shows each field before and after, and what else it touches: items tied to a milestone, a milestone it would pass, a weekend date, an owner creaite doesn't know. For "add an action", **chips** suggest an owner, due date, workstream, milestone or priority read from the words; a click adds that one field before you press Enter, and a vague title says so.
3. If two items or people match, it asks which one; pick it and the palette stays open.
4. Press Enter to **save** it: nothing reaches your project record until you save, and the bar offers **Undo last**. [[⌘↵]] saves it and opens Intake. Esc changes nothing.
5. A request it can't read as a change is offered to ask creaite ("Give it to ask creaite"). Nothing in ⌘K deletes.

Or say: `move ABC-A-002 to fri` `Omar owns the SOW action` (ask creaite shows the change and waits for `apply`)

### Meetings

## Turn a meeting into minutes

Where: Intake

1. **From the calendar:** a meeting whose transcript is ready says **Process** (the check looks transcripts up for you; **Check again** looks one up now). Press it, or **Process all ready** to go through every ready meeting and file in turn. creaite loads the transcript and its meeting chat and drafts the minutes in the background; the row shows the progress.
2. **From a file:** press **Add files** (the upload icon) or drop the transcript or minutes anywhere on the page (Teams transcripts, Word, text, Markdown or PDF files). A transcript says **Make minutes**; minutes someone else wrote say **Find work items**, which turns them into suggestions without making new minutes. If creaite can't tell which a file is from its name, it asks (**Transcript** or **Minutes**).
3. Wait for the row to move to **Drafted**. You get draft minutes (a page in creaite's own format, filed as `YYYY-MM-DD <Meeting title>.html` when you approve them), new items, and updates to open items the meeting discussed.
4. Approve them in [Review and approve suggestions](#review-and-approve-suggestions). Nothing is filed until you do.

5. **Was the agenda discussed?** If the meeting had an agenda in the project's Agendas folder, or a prep page, the minutes record each point as **Covered**, **Touched on** or **Not discussed**. Intake shows it on the minutes' row, and **Carry to the next agenda** in the minutes puts a missed point on the next prep page of the same meeting, under "Not covered last time" ([Follow a meeting](#follow-a-meeting-from-invite-to-follow-up)).

Or say: `process new minutes` `process Tuesday's ABC steering`

## Capture a note

Where: any page · [[⌘⇧N]] · ⌘K "New note" · Today › a meeting's **Note**

1. Press [[⌘⇧N]] on any page (or **N** when you aren't typing in a box: in an ordinary Chrome tab ⌘⇧N opens an incognito window). The note opens at the right; the page stays where it was.
2. Type. creaite guesses the project from the project register (a code, the project's name, an alias, a stakeholder, a client domain or two keywords) and says why ("guessed from “Omar Ali”"). Change it in **Project** and it stays your choice. If nothing names a project, the note is **Unfiled** (the portfolio's notes folder).
3. It is kept as you type ("Saved 10:42"). Esc or × closes it and keeps what you typed: [[⌘⇧N]] carries on, and unfinished notes are listed under **Recent notes**.
4. Press **Done** ([[⌘↵]]) to save it in the project's `08 Notes` folder, named by its title (or its first line). Or press **Done and find updates** to save it and open it on the project's Notes tab with Find updates, which turns its lines into project record changes.
5. **Confidential** (off unless you turn it on) marks the note confidential: it is kept out of prep pages and client reports, and Claude reads its text only if you say yes.
6. To make the note wider, drag the thin bar on its left edge (up to the width of the page) or focus it and press the left and right arrow keys. Press **Reset width** at the top of the note, double-click the bar, or press Home, to put it back. creaite remembers the width on this computer.
6. On Today, **Note** under a meeting starts a Working session note with the meeting's title, date and who was invited.

Or say: `note: Sara confirmed the pilot extends to 60 agents`

## Keep notes on a project

Where: Projects › a project › Notes · Projects › Portfolio notes

1. Open the project and choose the **Notes** tab, beside Overview. Press **New note**, give it a title and choose a template: **Blank**, **Stakeholder**, **Lessons learned** or **Working session** (with its date and who it was with). **Make the note** saves it in the project's `08 Notes` folder, named by its title.
2. Click and type: there is no Edit, Save or Cancel. The formatting bar shows while the cursor is in the note, on one line: **Heading** (Text, which also takes a heading off, Heading 1 to 3, and Clear formatting), bold, italic, strikethrough, code, **List** (bulleted, numbered, checklist, indent, outdent), a quote, **Link**, **Insert** (a link to another note, a person with `@`, a work item with `#`, a table, a picture), undo and redo. Point at Heading, List or Insert to open its menu (it closes a moment after you move away), or press Enter or the Down arrow on it; the arrows move, Enter chooses and Esc closes it. Tab and Shift+Tab indent and outdent in a list. To remove a link, press Link with the cursor in it and choose **Remove link**, or click the link and choose **Remove** (the words stay). The new-note drawer and Current state keep their own, shorter bars. Press Tab once to reach the bar, then the arrow keys, Home and End to move along it and Esc to go back to the text; a button that is lit is in force where the cursor is. ⌘⇧K (Ctrl+Shift+K off a Mac) adds or edits a web or email link (⌘K stays the search and jump box everywhere). The note is saved about two seconds after you stop typing, and at once when you leave it.
3. Link as you type: two square brackets link another note, `@` a person, `#` a work item (a chip with its live state, written "ID · Action: title"), and `/` at the start of a line or after a space opens a menu under the cursor: Text, Heading 1, 2 or 3, lists, a checklist, a quote, code, a table, a picture, a link to a work item, person or note, or a template. Type to narrow it, ↑ ↓ and Enter to insert, Esc to close. The same menu is in Current state (headings, lists, quote, picture, work item), and so is `#` (that project's items); the item fields have none. Click a link or chip to open it; ⌥-click to put the cursor in it. **Web and email links:** select the words and press the bar's link button, choose **Link** in the `/` menu, press ⌘⇧K, or paste an address over the words (pasting an address on its own makes it a link, with Undo on the message). A small box asks for the words and the address: Enter saves, Esc cancels, Tab stays inside it. Only http, https and email (mailto) addresses are linked; anything else is stopped and nothing is inserted, and a bare `example.com` is given the https prefix. Click a link to see its address with **Open** (a new tab, so you never leave the note by accident), **Edit** and **Remove** (which keeps the words). A link is saved as `[words](address)`. Click a work-item chip to open it; ⌥-click to put the cursor in it.
4. Change the title and the file is renamed to match when the note saves; links to it in your other notes follow. **Activity › What ask creaite changed**, which lists your note saves too, can undo a save or a rename.
5. Select some text and press **AI** (or Alt + /) to rewrite it, shorten it, turn it into a checklist and more ([Let creaite draft the wording](#let-creaite-draft-the-wording)). The ⋯ menu pins the note, marks it **Confidential** or shows the file.
6. **Delete note…**, last in the ⋯ menu, moves the note to the project's archive folder (`99 Archive`), never away for good. A box asks you to confirm, and that is all: pressing **Delete note** there moves it at once, with no approval card (your click is the approval). The note closes, leaves the list and search, and the message that follows has **Undo**, which brings it back. ## Chase one item, or ask for help with your own

## Working with lists
- **Select and open:** a click highlights a row, and only it. Click its **ID**, double-click the row, or press **Enter** to open the item over the list. **⌘-click** adds a row to the highlight or removes it; **Shift-click** highlights a range; a plain click replaces the highlight. **Esc** clears the highlight and closes the details together. With two or more rows highlighted, the first **Esc** clears the highlight and the details stay; the next closes them. With the details open, a single click on another row shows that item in them. The same works on the Board, a person's page and in Review.
- **Keys:** **J** and **K**, or **↑** and **↓**, move the cursor (the details follow), **Shift+J** or **Shift+K** extends the highlight, **Space** adds or removes the cursor row, **Enter** or **→** opens it, and **←** closes it again (with nothing open, **←** opens the row's menu). On Work, with the peek open, a click on another row shows that row in it.
- **One key per property (Work):** **O** opens the owner's list on the cursor row. With the item open, **S** status, **O** owner, **P** priority and **T** due date open that chip; with several highlighted they change all of them. With no item open, **S**, **P** and **T** go to Settings, Projects and Today, as on every page, so use the row's menu (**←**) or click the value. **C** closes the cursor row, with Undo, when no item is open (with one open, **C** is Draft email). **V** turns **Client-visible** on or off (also the eye at the top of an open item, and the right-click menu): on means the item can appear in client reports, and it needs client messaging first. The keys do nothing while a menu, list, calendar or dialog is open, and a held-down key counts once.
- **Rows (Work):** a row shows its status and priority in their own columns (Display shows or hides them); there are no marks before the title, and a screen reader still hears both; a client-visible item has a **Client** tag. The two icons beside List and Board choose **Compact** rows (the default: one line, the ID beside the title) or **Comfortable** ones (the ID on a second line); only the height changes, never a column's width. Where you haven't chosen a grouping, Actions are grouped by when they are due (Overdue, This week, Next week, Later, No date, then Closed, folded until you open it), RAID by priority and Decisions by status; ordering by a column shows the list in that order, and **Group by: None** in Display keeps it ungrouped.
- **Wide tables** show a fade at the right edge and **more columns →**: press it, or scroll sideways. The first column stays in place.
- **High contrast:** buttons keep their edges, the selected tab, chip or switch shows in the highlight colour, and the focus ring shows.
- **Change a value:** click it, or its **▾** (shown when you point at the row; **Tab** to it and press **Enter** from the keyboard). Pick from the list or calendar: status, owner (the people register, with TBC), due date, priority, type, workstream. A RAID item's priority edits its **Impact** and **Likelihood**. Choosing saves it, with **Undo** in the message; **Esc** or clicking away leaves it. Titles, responses, free text and the project are changed in the item (click its ID). A decision's status changes only while pending; **Made** opens it with Made saved and today's date.
- **Right-click** a row or card for its common actions (Open, Close or Reopen, Status, Owner, Due, Priority, Workstream, Milestone, Copy ID, Copy link, Draft email). With several highlighted, it acts on all of them. See [Add or change an item](#add-or-change-an-item).
- **Change several at once:** with two or more highlighted, a bar shows "3 selected", **Close**, **Reopen**, and lists for status, owner, due date, priority, workstream and milestone. A field that doesn't fit every item is greyed out, with the reason on pointing. Each item is checked on its own; the ones that can't take the change are named and left out. It is one change with one **Undo**. More than 25 asks first. Review's bulk approval never covers minutes, reports, health changes, new decisions or new risks.
- **An open item** slides over the page at half the window (beside ask creaite when its chat is open), already editable. Its key values show as chips (actions: status, priority, owner, due date; RAID: status, priority, owner, due date, impact and likelihood; decisions: status, decision maker, needed by or date made), then the latest update, the response or the reasoning, **Details**, and its history (**All**, **Activity**, **Updates**, **Source**). Click a value to choose another; dates take DD-MM-YYYY or words such as fri or +3d. Each change is saved on its own, marked "saved" until saved. Closing sets the closed date (reopening clears it); a client-visible item needs its client messaging first; a title can't be empty. Clicking another row, clicking away, Esc or the arrow closes it. The corners icon makes it full width.
- **Filters combine:** Status, Due, Owner (Me first), Project and Priority are on the toolbar. The funnel (**Add a filter**, or **Shift + F**) holds the rest: workstream, RAID type, response, source, client-visible, **Stale** (no change for 7 days, or the stale setting), **Last updated**, **Raised**, **Milestone**, and due, raised or closed between two dates. Each field has **is** and **is not**; an excluded filter's chip says "not". Each menu shows how many items each choice would give.
- Each filter is a chip with an ×, then **Clear all** and "Showing 12 of 87 · 14 closed hidden". With no match, the list names the filters and offers **Remove**, **Include closed items** and **Clear all filters**. Actions, RAID and Decisions each keep their own filters. Workstream has a "No workstream" choice.
- **Decisions** have a Status (Pending, Made, Withdrawn) and a Needed by date. **Pending** lists what is still to decide, soonest first; **Overdue** lists those past their date. Made decisions keep their date, reasoning, options, revisit and replaces fields.
- **Views** (All open, Mine, Due soon, **Chase**…) are presets. Changing one shows a dot on its chip: **Update view** saves it, **Save as new view** keeps both. A saved view's **▾** renames, moves or deletes it. Views that don't fit, and Overdue on Actions, are under **More views**. **Chase** is open items owned by someone else that have gone quiet (stale). Settings › Work and reports › Opening views sets the view each tab opens with.
- **Display** (the sliders icon, or **D**) holds **Group by**, **Sub-group**, **Order by** (⇅ reverses), **Properties** (columns, or a card's fields; a card shows four) and **Show** (empty groups; how much of the Closed column shows). **Reset to this tab's default** undoes it. Saved views keep the layout.
- **Groups:** Group by Project, Due, Status, Owner (Me first), Milestone, Priority, Workstream or Type gives a header with its count and overdue count. Click it, or Enter on its ▾, to collapse it (remembered per tab and grouping). A Sub-group adds a second level. A Shift range never reaches a collapsed group; **Select all N matching** says how many are hidden that way.
- **List or Board:** the two icons show the same items as cards in columns, on every tab. The Board's columns are its grouping; a Sub-group adds swimlanes. Columns count their cards, collapse to a strip, and can be hidden when empty. Closed shows the last 14 days, with **Show all**. Cards sort by Order by; there is no hand-ordering.
- **All items** (the fourth tab) lists actions, risks, issues and decisions together. It is the List only and read-only: click a row to highlight it; its ID, a double-click or Enter opens the item to edit. Columns are the item, its **Type** (Action, Decision, Risk, Issue, Assumption or Dependency), owner, due, status and, in Display, priority, project, workstream, source and last updated. A decision's due date is its Needed by. **Status** has three choices: Open (open, delayed and pending), Closed (closed and made), Cancelled (cancelled and withdrawn). Filters and grouping match the other tabs. Views: All open (where it opens), Mine, Due soon, Chase, Overdue and Closed. There is no bulk bar here.
- **Every count opens its list.** A number that counts items anywhere in creaite (the Projects table's Evidence, a project page's Progress over time, the People list, a person's page, Today, a meeting's prep, the People page) is a link, underlined with dots. Work opens filtered to exactly those items, with the filters as chips. "Open" counts Open actions only; a date figure (overdue, due soon, no due date) also counts Delayed actions. **Back** returns. The Projects table's No date and Slip open the project page at the card that lists them.
- Click a column heading to sort, again to reverse, and a third time for the default. Filters, sort and view are kept in the address, so Back, a reload or a bookmark keeps them.
- The owner picker lists the project's people first. Type a name, alias or email. An unknown name brings likely matches first ("Is 'Sara' Sara Khan?", with "remember it as an alias"); otherwise it asks for details (the full name is required). **Add workstream…** at the end of a project's list adds one.
- **Projects are shown by their full name** everywhere. The short code appears only at the start of an item's ID (ABC-A-003), in a project's settings page, and in what you type (commands, filters and search take the code). A Draft email subject starts with it, so Outlook rules still match.
- Turn on **Client-visible** in an item's details to let it into client reports (off by default).
- **Properties** in Display chooses the columns, Milestone and Last updated included. Drag a heading to move a column (or use ↑ ↓ in Properties); drag an edge to resize it, double-click to reset.

## How saving works
- Every save, new item, health change, current-state edit and Approve or Reject is written as a suggestion in creaite's staging area, already approved. Nothing reaches your project record until it's saved.
- **A change shows at once.** Edit a value in the list, move a card, change an open item or use ⌘K and the screen changes straight away; the row shows a small hollow dot while it is being saved, which goes when it is saved (or turns into the saved dot when it waits for **Save**). Several quick changes to one item are saved in the order you made them. If a change can't be saved (the item changed elsewhere, or a rule stopped it), it is put back on screen and the message says why, with **Retry**. **Undo** works while it is still saving, too.
- With creaite running, saves are saved straight away. Without it they wait, and the top bar shows how many are held. When the server is down a red alert shows the command to start it and **Check again**; Settings › Health › creaite and backups says when mail and Teams were last checked and when the data was built. Say **save** in Claude Code to save them.
- Undo in the message removes a change that hasn't been saved yet. For an saved change, use **undo last save**.
- Settings are saved straight to your settings, with a backup and a log line each time.
- **Theme and mode:** Settings › Essentials › Look and help › Style picks the colour theme (creaite, the default, then Brightwater, Notion, Linear, Cursor, Claude, Apple, Retro Arcade and Thermo Scan; a theme you had saved that has been retired opens as creaite); Theme picks Follow my Mac, Light or Dark, and the moon button flips light and dark. `Cmd+K` › "Theme:" switches theme too.
- **Motion and reduced motion:** creaite uses a little motion to show what changed (a button presses, the line under a tab slides to the one you picked, a filter chip grows in or closes up, a highlighted row's tick draws in, a saved setting shows a tick, a copy button says **Copied**, the colours fade when you change the theme, menus and the details ease in, a row new since your last visit has a small dot that fades once you've seen it, and a thin line under the top bar shows ask creaite is working). Nothing moves on its own while you're idle, and nothing makes you wait. It follows your computer's reduced-motion setting; **Settings › Essentials › Look and help › Motion** chooses **Follow system** (the default), **Reduce** (no animation) or **Full** (keep creaite's own animation even if the computer asks for less; the spinner still follows the computer). Under Reduce everything still happens, at once.

The weekly and client reports use the same guide and facts.

Notes are your own working thinking (a stakeholder, a position, lessons, a conversation): internal, never in a client report, and never read for items by the check or on their own.

- **Links as you type:** two square brackets link another note (one that doesn't exist yet shows red; click it to make it), `@` mentions a person from the people register, `#` links a work item (it shows as a chip with its live state, worded "ID · Action: title"; click it to open the item), and `/` on an empty line inserts a sub-heading, a checklist, a table or a template's headings. ⌥-click a link to put the cursor in it instead.
- **Pictures:** **Image** on the toolbar adds a PNG or JPEG (up to 2 MB) after the paragraph the cursor is in. It is saved at once in the notes folder's `_images` folder, and its caption is its description. × removes it from the note; the file stays.
- **A Working session's date and who it was with** can be changed in the note's header at any time. Type the date as DD-MM-YYYY or in words (today, yesterday, tomorrow, last monday, next friday, in 3 days, 3 days ago): the pop-up says what it understood ("Reads as Monday 12-10-2026 · Enter to use") before anything is accepted, or pick a day on its calendar. Text it cannot read says so and keeps the old date. Separate the names with commas; it saves with the note.
- **Tags:** each note's tags sit on their own line under the date, after the fixed **Internal** (or **Confidential**) chip. **+ Tags** adds one (commas add several), click a tag's text to rename it (Enter keeps it, Esc puts it back, empty removes it), × removes it. A tag is never added twice, and is cut at 40 characters. Until a note has tags a few are suggested.
- **The right column:** **Mentioned in** (the notes that link to this one), **Linked work** (each item it mentions, with its state) and **People**. An item's panel says when notes mention it ("2 notes mention this"), with a link to each.
- **The ⋯ menu:** Pin or unpin, mark it **Confidential** (kept out of Prep pages and of what search gathers for Claude), and show the file.
- **Focus on a note:** the expand icon at the left of the title (or [[⌘⇧F]]) lifts the open note over the page, with the page dimmed and out of reach behind it; Tab stays inside the note, and the cursor goes into it. It is the same note, so it keeps saving as you type. To leave, press **Esc**, click the dimmed page, press the icon again or press ⌘⇧F again: they all do the same thing, saving first and then closing, with no question when it saved. If it couldn't be saved (the file changed outside creaite, or creaite isn't reachable) the note stays open and asks: **Keep editing**, **Copy my text then close** or **Save again**; nothing you typed is dropped. If a menu, a picker or the AI bar is open, Esc closes that first and the next Esc leaves. You come back to the control you started from. Find updates and the ⋯ menu are hidden while the note is lifted.
- **One editor:** a note uses the same editor as Current state, an item's text fields and Prep's notes (Tiptap), with the same keys, Undo (⌘Z, ⌘⇧Z) and paste cleaning. A note can hold everything the toolbar offers: sub-headings, bold, italic, strikethrough, code, lists, checklists, quotes, tables, pictures and links.

Spelling and grammar are fixed in every suggestion except Ask…. The answer is a suggestion with Accept, Discard, Try again and Show changes; ⌘Z undoes an Accept. **Make this an action, a risk, a decision or an update** runs Find updates on the selection's one line only, and a selection over two lines runs nothing and says so.
- **Turning lines into work:** notes are passive. Nothing reads them for your project record until you press **✦ Find updates** ([Find updates in a note](#find-updates-in-a-note)); the check and the minutes never do.

Reading a note is not outside content, so it never brings the approval card on its own; a note's pictures, transcripts, documents and the inbox still count.
- **Plain files:** each note is a Markdown file with a small header (title, dates, tags, pinned, confidential), readable in Finder and OneDrive without creaite. Search (⌘K) finds notes in their own **Notes** group and opens them here; **Files** lists the notes folder of each project.

## Early warnings
- A lightning flag marks trouble before anyone reports it; hover for the reason. It shows on the item in Work, Today and the project page, in the detail panel, in the project's **Early warnings** card and evidence row, in the portfolio's evidence, beside the health editor's evidence, and in the weekly report's facts.
- **The signals:** an action re-dated 3 or more times; a risk raised in 3 or more meetings or checks with no response; a milestone whose forecast has moved later 2 weeks running (and still is); an action open more than twice as long as the project's usual (at least 14 days; the portfolio's usual if the project has fewer than 5 closed actions); a pending decision past the date it is needed by; one person owning more than half of a project's open items (at least 6), shown on the project page, their page and in the health evidence (it counts owners only; it never appears in a client report); and a dependency attached to a milestone that is due after the milestone's forecast, or is overdue while the milestone is within 14 days. The sentence reads like "ABC-M-002 is forecast for 20-11-2026, but dependency ABC-005 isn't due until 30-11-2026".
- **Weekend due date** (on unless you switch it off): an open action or RAID item due, or a pending decision needed, on a weekend day. The item's panel offers to move it to the Friday before or the Monday after.
- They're worked out from what creaite already holds each time the dashboard is built, and never stored as items. **Settings › Advanced › Review and warnings › Early warnings** switches each off or changes its threshold.
- Warnings are evidence, not health: they never change a project's health on their own.

## Updates from meetings
- When creaite processes a meeting matched to a project, that project's **open items are expected** in it, whether or not you opened its prep brief. For the ones the transcript (or a quick note) actually discusses, it proposes an **update** instead of a new item: a status, a new due date, a response to a risk or issue, or a latest update. Items not discussed are left alone.
- In Review the updates sit under the meeting's minutes beside its new items, labelled **Update**. Each shows the item's ID and text, every changed field as before → after, and what was said. An update that **closes** an item is approved on its own: “Approve routine” and “Approve all from these minutes” skip it.
- **Probably done:** a meeting passage (and, from the check, a mail you sent, a reply or a Teams message) that shows an open item finished (an action, a RAID item, or a promise kept: the person who made it counts as its owner) becomes a closing update marked **Looks done** or **Maybe done**, with the words that show it. It must come from the item's owner or you, or name the owner; the item must be in its own project and raised before. Teams counts a little less than mail. Settings › AI drafts › **Spot items that are probably done** turns it off.
- It never changes an owner, title or project (use Review or ask creaite), and never proposes an open item again as a new one. Raising an open item again is still counted, so repeated risks show up in the early warnings. If you change an item before approving, the update is held as a conflict instead of overwriting your change.

## Find updates in a note
- **✦ Find updates** on a note reads the lines that are new or changed since you last ran it and opens a panel **beside the note**, in place of its right-hand column, until you close it (Close, or Esc). Each line that gave a change gets a numbered marker, and its rows carry the same number; pointing at either highlights the other.
- Rows are grouped: **New** (an action, risk, issue or decision), **Updates to existing items** (a due date, done, owner, latest update), **Milestones** and **Project information** (a new person for the people register, a line for the project memory, a project detail). Lines with nothing in them are left alone and counted ("1 line left as a note"): not every line needs to become an item.
- **Ticking a row is your approval.** Routine rows (a new action, a due date, done, an owner) start ticked. New risks, new decisions, milestone moves, health changes and memory lines start unticked: tick them one at a time. A hedged line ("probably", "might", "to confirm") never moves a milestone or changes health: that row is shown, marked **Tentative**, and can't be ticked; an action to confirm it is suggested beside it, linked to the milestone so it shows as an early warning there until it's done.
- Click a row to edit it first (title, owner, due date in DD-MM-YYYY, priority, the change itself). Every row is checked against your project record's rules; a row that fails says why and stays unticked. Each row also carries the usual checks: **Looks like** an existing item, **Probably done** with the words that show it, and **Vague title**.
- **Save n changes** writes the ticked rows to your project record as one change, and **Undo** in the message at the foot of the page reverses all of it. **Save for later** puts the ticked rows on Intake instead. Unticked rows are never kept. Without creaite, Save saves them to Intake and says so.
- Each item's source is the note and its line (`…/08 Notes/<title>.md#p4`): its source link opens the note at that line. What a line became is kept beside the note, never in it (a small file kept by creaite: the line's number, a fingerprint and the item IDs, never your words), so running Find updates again looks only at new or changed lines. A line already sent shows a ✓ in the note's left margin; editing the line takes its tick away. Undo makes the lines new again.
- Claude sees only the note's new lines, the project's open item IDs and titles, its milestones and the people register's names, with no tools: it can't read files or mail and can't change anything. A note marked **Confidential** asks once before its lines go to Claude, and its new items are labelled Sensitive. Find updates only runs when you press it: never on its own, never from the check.

## Minutes view
Where: Intake › Open draft on minutes · address `#minutes=`

- **Open draft** on a meeting's minutes in Intake opens them as a page under Intake (it needs creaite). The decisions, risks, issues and actions in them are the items under them in Intake: a change to a row changes the item. Filed minutes are never saved over; a change is saved as a new version.
- **One version.** Minutes have one version: what you see is what is filed and exported.
- **The bar** is one row of icons (two on a narrow window): **Zoom** (the minutes open fitted to the width, but never past 100%: on a wide screen the page shows at its real size, centred on the grey; − and +, or pinch, or Ctrl and the wheel, still go past it, 50% to 250% of the fitted size; the percentage is the real size, and clicking it fits the page again, still at most 100%), **Show as exported**, **Transcript** (pressed while it shows), **Export** and **Re-read for items** (opens ask creaite with the request written, for you to send; it doesn't re-draft the minutes by itself), then **Approve minutes**. Scroll the page and the header slims to one bar with the meeting's title.

Press a pressed button again to clear it. Actions and issues start ticked; decisions, risks and every item approved one by one (a closure, a decision recorded) start with neither. Neither on such an update leaves it waiting on Intake. Nothing changes in your project record until you approve the minutes.

- **Coverage:** an agenda point the meeting didn't cover can be carried on, made a separate session or marked no longer needed, with Undo, and optionally a line under "Not covered in this meeting".
- **Approve minutes** is one click: the minutes are filed and every ticked line becomes a work item, in one save; the message says "Minutes approved · 3 work items registered" with **Undo** (Undo puts the minutes back exactly as they were before). Only when a ticked item has a real problem (an owner who needs details, a possible duplicate) does an **Approve the minutes** box list just those, with **Go back** and **Approve anyway**. With nothing ticked the minutes are filed and no work items are made.

If a ticked item is worth a look, the minutes open with the Approve the minutes box. **Approve n routine** follows the Track column for that project's items too: crossed lines leave the minutes, lines with neither stay untracked, the ticked routine items are approved, and its **Undo** puts all of it back; the minutes themselves, and anything approved one by one, still wait for their own Approve. A row you crossed or left with neither is never approved anywhere else either: Approve routine, the bar's Approve and the group tick leave it out ("crossed in the minutes' Track column"), and so does `review.py approve` in Claude Code or ask creaite.

What your selected text already held stays yours. Minutes with no project in your project record can't be checked, so creaite stops before anything is sent to Claude.

## AI drafts
- **A draft is a suggestion, never a save.** It lands in a field you then edit, a suggestion on Intake you approve on its own, or a box with Use and Discard. creaite chooses the facts from your project record and your creaite folder; Claude only words them, one run at a time, with progress and **Stop**.
- **Sources on every draft:** each sentence carries the sources it rests on; one with none is marked **no source** before you see it. Confidential or restricted sources are skipped, and client wording is written from the item's own text only.
- **Where:** an action's latest update (one item, or **Draft updates** for several), client messaging (one item, or **Draft all** on the project page), a decision's reasoning and options, a RAID item's response with impact and likelihood, the reason for a health change and for a forecast move, a clearer title for a vague action (**Sharpen**), a meeting's agenda and invite text (**Draft agenda**), a Draft email's opening paragraph (**Polish**) and the status in another form (Generate's **Form**). Movement's slip range also has a one-line explanation worked out from its own numbers.
- **AI on selected text:** select text in any editor and choose **AI** (or Alt + /): a suggestion for just that text, with Accept, Discard, Try again and Show changes, and the plain-code check under it. Claude runs with no tools at all and sees only the text and the project's name, people and open item IDs (`/api/edit/ai`). Counted under **edit** in a counts file (offered, used, rejected); never the text. Make it executive on a selection is this quick path; on the whole Current state it keeps the full flow with facts and sources.
- **Spotted at reading for items, by plain code:** a suggestion that repeats an existing item (**Looks like**, merged as an update on Intake) and an open item a message or meeting shows finished (**Looks done**). Claude, running the minutes or the check, judges only the close matches. Both are suggestions you decide; neither changes anything by itself. Chips for a typed action are plain code too.
- **Counted, never read:** a small file kept by creaite keeps, for each kind, how many drafts were offered, used as written, used after an edit and rejected. Counts only. Settings › AI drafts shows them and turns each kind on or off.

## Approving suggestions
- Suggestions wait on Intake until you decide. Click a suggestion's row (or its pencil) to open it over the page in the same layout as a work item (title, key values, text, Details and Source), with **Reject**, **Cancel**, **Save** and **Save and approve** at its foot: edits stay in the suggestion until you choose. The "needs a project" note, the repeat note and the **Remember** tick are there. A suggestion that updates an item already in your project record opens that whole item, with each changed value marked "was → now" and a line saying what changes if you approve; its status, due date and latest update can be edited there.
- **New items arrive on their own.** A check rebuilds the dashboard when it finishes, and an open dashboard (served by creaite) is told within about a second, and updates only what changed (a new file in the inbox refreshes just the file lists): Intake and Today update, with a quiet “3 new items in Review”. If you're in the middle of editing (a form or editor is open, a dialog is up or you're typing), nothing is reloaded: a chip in the top bar says **New items arrived: refresh**, and the page refreshes when you finish or click it. If that connection drops, the page checks every health-check interval (30 seconds by default) instead until it's back. A page left open while creaite was restarted says **This page is out of date** with **Reload**, and asks the server nothing more until you reload it. A dashboard opened as a file is a snapshot and doesn't update.
- Approve routine never covers draft minutes, reports, health changes, new decisions, a decision being recorded or withdrawn, new risks, closing updates, conflicts or items without a project. A promise marked Done or Dropped is the one closing update it covers, unless it only **Looks done**. It acts on one group at a time and is one save with one **Undo**.
- **Stale:** a meeting row shows how long it has waited and says **Stale** once it has waited longer than the stale setting (Settings › Advanced › Review and warnings › Stale after, 7 days unless you change it).
- **Reject with a reason:** a row's ✕, **R** (or Shift+R) on a focused row or the open suggestion's **Reject** opens one menu: the seven reasons on 1 to 7 (Not an action, Duplicate, Chatter, Already done, Wrong project, Doesn't need tracking, For another team), **Use each guess** (G: creaite's own guess for the row) and **No reason** (0). Duplicate records the item it repeats, or asks which. The reason is kept with the rejection, shown in Activity and Quality, and read by the minutes and the check as guidance; only the exact item is never suggested again.
- **After a reject:** the message has **Undo**, which puts the row back on Intake. Activity › Quality › **What you rejected** changes a reason, undoes a reject or **Approve instead** approves it in one step (what is approved only on its own goes back to Intake and opens).
- **Looks like:** a suggestion that repeats an item in your project record says **Looks like ABC-A-009 (same project, open) · Very likely** or **Possible** (open items of its project first, then recently closed ones, then another project's open items with the same owner). Open the suggestion for **Compare**, **Merge as an update** (same project, open item only), **Keep as new** or **Reject as duplicate**. Settings › Health and warnings sets how close a match must be for the hint on suggestions made before this; Settings › AI drafts turns the marks off.
- **Update an existing item instead:** in the open view of any suggested new action, RAID item or decision with a project. It lists the project's open items of the same kind, best matches first (strength in words, never a percentage), and turns the suggestion into an update to the one you choose, as Merge does; **Undo** in the message puts it back (it undoes a Merge too). Nothing reaches your project record until the update is approved and saved.
- **Looks done:** a closing update the check or the minutes suggested from a sent mail, a reply, a Teams message or a meeting leads with **Looks done** or **Maybe done** and the evidence. It is a closing update, so it is approved on its own; so is a promise that looks kept.
- When nothing waits, Intake says so.
- Items read for items from draft minutes sit under their meeting. Rejecting the minutes leaves their items for review, with an option to reject them too.
- **Change the project** of a suggested item with its **Project** chip in the open suggestion. Updates to items already in your project record never move. Nothing is filed or written until you approve and save.
- **With an item open, a single click on another suggestion** shows it in the same place; one with unsaved changes stays until you save or cancel it.
- Items without a project need one before approval. When you choose one, the panel may offer to **remember** the signal that decided it (a sender, domain, channel, alias or person), so similar items match on their own next time; it's added to the register when saved, and undo removes it.
- Each item shows what you need to decide: owner, due date, priority, response, workstream and milestone. Its Raised date comes from the meeting or message date.
- Draft minutes and reports are approved with **Approve** on their row, which checks the draft against the items, accepts tracked changes and removes comments first (the same check as **approve minutes for …** in Claude Code). A **conflict** (an edit to something that changed after it was suggested) shows your project record's current value and this one side by side, with **Keep current** and **Use this one**; both save at once, with Undo. Both need creaite; without it, say **keep 5** or **override 5** in Claude Code.

Only one run goes at a time: while one is busy, **Process** waits.

Either way the row shows progress ("Drafting in the background", with the step it is on) and **Stop**, then moves to **Drafted**; ask creaite's panel stays closed, and ask creaite waits until the run ends (one run goes at a time). If creaite needs you to finish a file (its project isn't clear, say), the row says **Needs you** with the question, and **ask creaite about this** puts it in ask creaite's box for you to answer. **Process all ready (n)** does them one after another, and **Stop** on the running row ends the rest too; it leaves out private and confidential meetings and files whose type isn't known, and says how many it left out. Naming one by pressing its own **Process** still works.

Its summary says "calendar from today". Mail and Teams still go back to the last check, but never further than **Check looks back at most** (Settings › Check and Intake › Check; 3 days unless you change it): after a long gap the summary says how many older days were not read, and "check the last 7 days" reads them anyway. - **Transcripts:** the daily check looks up each meeting in the window (new ones, and any still *Not transcribed*, since a transcript can appear hours after the meeting) and saves what it found, so a row shows it at once. A meeting with a transcript is **ready**; the others wait. Private meetings are listed as Busy, with their times only.
- **Add files:** press the upload icon or drop transcripts or minutes anywhere on the page (Teams transcripts, Word, text, Markdown or PDF files, up to 25 MB each). They are saved to the inbox folder; a file with the same name is kept alongside, never overwritten. creaite reads a file's kind from its name (a Teams transcript file is always a transcript); when it can't tell, the row asks. A switch on the row changes the kind before you process it.
- A minutes file from someone else is copied to the project's minutes folder when you approve its suggestions; the copy in the inbox is then removed.
- Three views, **List**, **Board** and **Two panes**, and a **Search** box show the same rows; **Add files**, **Process all ready**, **N** (Not needed) and **H** (Later) work in all of them. Notes from a call with no transcript are a note ([[⌘⇧N]], from any page).

## Milestones: Plan and Movement
- **No project chosen:** opening Milestones without a project shows **Choose a project**, with your projects as links; press one to open its Milestones page. With no projects it says **No projects yet** and links to the **Projects** page.
- **The page** (the pencil on a project's Milestones card, ⌘K's **Add milestone**, a milestone in search, an answer or Activity) has its own address (`#milestones=ABC`), so Back, a reload and a link keep it, its view and the open milestone. The dates suggest one: Overdue when the agreed date has passed unachieved, Slipped when the forecast is later than agreed, On track otherwise. At risk is only ever yours. It appears only with 8 such closed milestones ("No range yet: 3 of 8 closed milestones with history") and is marked **Thin evidence** while fewer than five projects have them. Internal only.
- **Decide what to do:** **Hold** (a one-line reason, recorded as a decision), **Rebaseline** (a new agreed date, who agreed it, and why; it records a decision), **Add recovery action** and **Escalate to steering** (a decision needed). Each is saved for Intake; nothing changes until you approve it there.
- **The milestone editor** opens beside the page; each change saves on its own with Undo. Every date is a calendar pick (arrows, Page Up and Page Down, Enter, Esc). A click outside closes it, except on another milestone (it switches), or while a calendar or the people list is open (the first click closes only that), or while a change waits for its reason (it shakes and says so). **Progress** is set by hand. **Cancel this milestone** keeps its history and hides it everywhere.

## Activity
- Every change, newest first: when, what changed and by whom. Undo reverses a change not yet saved, or the latest save (its row has **Undo**).
- **Quality** (the second tab): what you approved as suggested, approved after an edit and rejected over the last four weeks, by source (minutes, check mail, check Teams, dashboard) and by type, the reasons given, the fields your edits change most, and how each kind of AI draft was used. - **What kind of change:** each line starts with the item's icon (action, risk, issue, assumption, dependency, decision, milestone, project, weekly update, file); point at it for its name.
- **Search and filters:** the list shows the newest 300 changes; the box at the top (press **/** while Activity is open) looks through every change read (the newest 2,000) and narrows the list as you type, by the item's ID or words, the project's name, who made the change, the change itself ("owner", "Closed") or the date; it shows "12 of 340" and **Clear**. ## Word documents and permissions in ask creaite
- **One-off Word documents:** ask for one ("make a Word document of open actions for ABC"). It reads your project record, says what it will build, and waits for your confirmation. The draft is branded and goes in your drafts folder (internal; nothing is filed or sent).
- **What it can read and change** (Alan's decision, 10-10-2026): anything in your creaite folder, your projects folder and your home folder, except secrets (`~/.ssh`, `~/.aws`, keychains, password stores, browser profiles and saved logins) and the operating system. It changes files itself when you ask (a new file, your notes, drafts, project files you named). Each change is backed up first and can be undone in **Activity › What ask creaite changed** (last seven days, with **Undo**), or with `python3 system/scripts/filechange.py undo`.
- **The approval card:** some changes wait for you, shown on **one card** with why and the change itself. These are: a change to ask creaite's own permissions or settings; anything outside your creaite folder and the projects folder; a delete or a move; another person's file (a transcript, sent minutes or a report, a client's document); more than ten files at once; and anything after it has read mail, Teams, a transcript, a document or a web page (an instruction hidden in a message could be aimed at it). Reading one of your notes doesn't count; a note's pictures do. There are two tiers: **Sensitive** (a triangle on yellow: settings files such as your settings or `schedules.md`, and the kinds above) and **High-risk** (a shield on red: a permission file such as creaite's permission settings, or any edit that adds, removes or changes a permission rule). Under each High-risk change is its way back: **Undo** in Activity, or the undo command that **Copy** copies. **Approve** makes all of them, exactly as shown; **Decline** makes none. The card waits 30 minutes and comes back after a reload. ask creaite can ask for a change but can't approve it: the approval is held by creaite and fits that one change only.
- **Never, whatever is asked:** secrets, the operating system, the files that run commands at log-in, and the database, the engine and creaite's rules (those change only through their own commands: save, and an update). A permission change that would let it run anything, reach those, skip permissions or remove a safety entry is stopped even if you approve it.
- **Permission files** (creaite's permission settings, your local permission settings, your settings and the other configuration files) can be changed through the card: approve it and the change is made directly (no Terminal), backed up, logged and undoable. A new rule comes as an item on the same card. A rule already allowed is shown as information, with what was actually stopped (often a `cd … &&`, a pipe or a redirect) and **Try again**. Only a rule that is out of bounds tells you to edit creaite's permission settings in Terminal.
- **Project files:** reading is not the same as finding items in a file. Never read: `99 Archive`, `_Templates`, `99 System`, or an online-only OneDrive file. Creating never overwrites (a taken name gets a number). Project documents are internal.
- **Images:** paste (⌘V / Ctrl+V), drop or attach (the paperclip) up to four PNG or JPEG images. They are for discussion only (nothing is read for items), and are deleted with their chat.
- **Formatting:** replies, the release notes in Settings › Health › creaite version, confirmations and a Markdown file's preview in Intake are formatted: headings, bold and italic, lists, tables (scrolling sideways in the bubble), quotes and code blocks (each with **Copy**). Links open in a new tab; only web and email addresses become links. An item ID such as ABC-A-003 opens that item, and a file name from Files opens the file. Your own messages stay as typed.
- **Times:** each bubble shows its time: 14:32 for today, or 08-10 14:32 for an earlier day; point at it for the full date. A reply shows when it finished. A line marks a chat that runs over midnight (Today, Yesterday or the date). Chats saved before this show no times.
- **The running and finished badge:** while ask creaite works on anything (a chat message, **Process**, a transcript check or load, a check, a report, **Generate**, the Prep page, a search answer), a badge at the bottom right of any page shows a pulsing spark, **ask creaite is working…**, what it is doing and how long. When the run ends it becomes a tick and **ask creaite has finished**, with the time taken, until you press its **×**, open the chat or start the next run. A stopped run says **ask creaite was stopped**; a failed one, **ask creaite stopped with a problem**. The badge is hidden while the chat is open. If the tab is hidden when a run ends, its title gets a ✓ until you look.
- **A run creaite started itself** (a Prep page, **Generate** or **Make it executive**, an AI draft such as **Draft agenda**, **Shape this meeting** or **Polish**, a transcript check or load, Close the day's catch-up check) shows three things: the ask, as one plain line ("Prep page for ABC weekly, Thursday 15th October 10:00"); the activity, with **✓ n steps** in plain words ("Reading your project record"); and the outcome, one line with a link ("Saved the prep page in … › Agendas · **Open**"), or what went wrong and what to do, or that it was stopped. The raw instructions and reply are never shown. The day's runs are kept in History as one chat, **Runs creaite started**. For a problem, **Settings › Health › Diagnostics** has the run's record (counts and IDs only).
- **Over the page:** ask creaite opens on top of the page at any width. The page keeps its layout and doesn't move when the chat opens, closes or is resized (drag its left edge). With an item's details open too, the details keep their place beside the chat. Under 1100 pixels, Work's filter bar folds unused filters into the funnel.
- **Your place:** while a reply arrives, the chat follows it only if you are at the bottom. Scroll up and it stays put; a **↓ New activity** button (**↓ Latest · still working** while it runs) appears. Sending, opening a chat from History and starting a new chat go to the bottom.
- **Copy, recall, edit, try again:** every reply has **Copy**. In an empty box, **↑** brings back your last message (↑ again goes further back, ↓ forward). **Edit** on your last message puts it back to change and send. A stopped or failed run offers **Try again**.
- **When something is stopped:** a command creaite doesn't allow is reported in one plain line: what it tried, why that isn't allowed, and what it does instead. **Show the command** opens the exact command. A ⚠ marks only a refusal the request didn't get past.
- **Checked against your project record:** before a reply is shown, every item ID in it is looked up. A title that isn't the item's is flagged in place ("(⚠ doesn't match your project record: <the real title>)"); a wrong owner is corrected to your project record's, saying what the reply had said; an ID not in your project record is marked "(⚠ not in your project record)". A quiet line counts what was checked. This is plain code, not the assistant checking itself, and it changes only the words shown, never your project record.
- **A plain "I can't":** some things can never be done, whatever you confirm: send, forward or reply to email, post in Teams, create, move or answer meeting invites, change files in SharePoint or OneDrive online, or start or stop creaite, the app or the login agent. Ask for one and it says so first, with a button to where it can be done (**Draft email to <name>**, **Open People**, **Open Today**, **Open Files** or **Open Settings**). It never offers a **Go ahead** that would be stopped.
- **Send while it works (queued):** press Enter while ask creaite is still working and the message is **queued**, marked **Queued**, and runs in the same chat when the current request finishes, one at a time in the order sent. **Edit** puts it back in the box; **Remove** takes it out. If a request ends with a question or a card, the queue waits for your answer (or **Run now**). **Stop** clears the queue too. creaite keeps the queue, so closing the panel or reloading doesn't lose it.
- **Changing an item's fields:** ask for changes to existing actions, risks, issues or decisions ("mark ABC-A-003 done", "Sara owns the SOW action") and a card shows each field's before and after, checked against your project record first. An unknown ID, a field the item doesn't have or a value it can't take is named, and the card then offers only **Cancel**. **Go ahead** saves it as a dashboard edit does, with the same checks and rules (closing sets the closed date; a made decision can't go back to pending), for all the items at once, saved at once with **Save edits automatically** on, with **Undo**. **Cancel** writes nothing. Up to ten fields at a time.
- **Any other change on one card:** for a change the item fields don't cover ("add a risk for ABC…", a project, memory, the register, client-visible), **one card** shows every item's exact before and after, as creaite's own preview reads it. **Approve** writes it at once (only that change; anything else on Intake is left alone) and says which items changed, with **Undo**. **Reject** discards it. If another change to the same item waits on Intake, or another job is writing, the card offers **Save for review** instead. It waits 30 minutes and comes back after a reload. File changes come on their own approval card (above).
- **Typing check is your go-ahead:** a message that is only a check (`sweep`, `sweep mail for ABC`, `sweep the last 7 days`, `sweep the <subject> thread`, `refresh calendar`; the plain words work too: `check my mail`, `check my mail and Teams`, `check mail for ABC`, `check my mail the last 7 days`) runs at once, marked **Checking (saves suggestions only)**, with no Go ahead. Its suggestions wait on Intake. That run can't save, approve, reject or undo anything. Anything more in the same message ("check and save") is an ordinary request, so ask creaite asks first, and so is a word after "for" that doesn't look like a project code ("check my mail for urgent"): type the code in capitals.
- **Go ahead on a change saves it once:** if ask creaite asks you to confirm a project record change instead of showing a card, **Go ahead** is your approval. With **Save edits automatically** on, it is written as soon as the run finishes and the chat says what changed ("Saved: FAL-A-010 · Owner: Alan Carter → Ivo (save 106)"), with **Undo**. With it off, the change waits for **Save**.
- **Reopening:** closing and reopening ask creaite, or reloading, shows the conversation at once. A running request carries on, and only what is new streams in. While it works, a quip ("Circling back…") shows beside three dots; each stays 5 to 10 seconds, and none repeats until all 150 have been shown.
- **Your draft is kept:** an unsent message (and the names of its images) stays in this browser for that chat, and is back after a reload. It goes when you send it, and never leaves the laptop.

## Commands for Claude
As you type in ask creaite, suggestions appear from your skills and commands, with project codes, open item IDs and review numbers where a command needs one; type `/` to see everything. Nothing is chosen until you choose it: **Enter sends what you typed** (type `apply`, press Enter, and `apply` is sent). Press ↓ or ↑ to choose a suggestion, then Enter puts it in the box to finish and send; Tab puts the first one in; a click picks one; Esc closes the list. Drag the panel's left edge to make it wider or narrower (double-click to reset); it remembers the width. The clock icon lists your 10 most recent chats (older ones are deleted); + starts a new one. A request keeps running if you close the panel or reload the page (the ask creaite button shows a dot, and a note tells you when it's done); only Stop ends it. While a request runs, Send becomes **Stop** (or press Esc): a question stops at once, and a confirmed change stops once its current step has finished, so nothing is left half-done.

## Updating creaite
- Say **update creaite** in Claude Code: with the GitHub command-line tool (`gh`) installed and signed in, it fetches the newest `creaite-X.Y.Z.zip` (pre-releases included) into the updates folder itself and checks it against its checksums (it installs nothing until you say yes). Without `gh`, download the zip from the GitHub release (the attached file, not "Source code (zip)") into the updates folder yourself.
- Settings › Health › **creaite version** shows the installed version. **Review** shows what the update changes, with its release notes formatted (**Copy the notes** copies them as written); **Install** backs up, installs and checks it, then asks you to restart creaite. Or say **update creaite** in Claude Code.
- Your `CLAUDE.md`, your project record, projects and settings values are never changed. **Undo update** goes back to the previous version.

## Open creaite as an app
- **Double-click the launcher.** It starts creaite if it isn't running and opens creaite in its own Chrome window (no tabs). In Chrome you can also use ⋮ › Cast, save and share › **Install page as app**, which gives it a Dock icon.
- **Make the launcher:** ask in Claude Code (`setup`); it offers a small **creaite** app in your Applications folder, or a `creaite.command` file in your creaite folder, and asks first. From Terminal: `system/scripts/run launch.py make-launcher` (Settings › Health › Set-up has it to copy, and **Check again** confirms it's made). The page can't make it itself: it goes outside your creaite folder.
- **One launcher per creaite folder:** your creaite folder's launcher is **creaite**; the development copy's is **creaite (dev)**, with DEV across its icon, so both can sit in the Dock and run side by side, each opening its own creaite folder. If **creaite** in Applications opens a different folder, making it says so and leaves it alone until you ask to replace it (`make-launcher --replace`), and the health check warns about it.
- **It stops itself.** A server the launcher started stops a minute after the last creaite window closes, and never in the middle of a job. A server you started in Terminal is left running. Settings › Health › creaite and backups has **Stop creaite** for a manual stop.
- If the server is stopped when you open the app, it shows “creaite isn't running” with a **Check again** button.
- The app belongs to the port in Settings. If you change the port, install the app again and reconnect your creaite folder. A development copy and a live creaite folder use different ports, so both can run at once.

## creaite and backups
The dashboard runs from creaite on this Mac. Start it in Terminal with:

[start command]

- Leave the Terminal window open while you work; close it (or press Ctrl+C) to stop the server. Or open creaite with the launcher (see Open creaite as an app), which starts and stops it for you.
- If the server stops, a red alert appears at the top; saves are held and saved when it's back.
- The database is backed up at the first session of each day and before every save, in the backups folder.
- Each day creaite also makes a closed **backup zip** of what rebuilds your creaite folder: the day's database backup, your settings (creaite's settings folder and your local permission settings), your `CLAUDE.md`, Review (suggestions and drafts, never ask creaite's chats or their images, nor who you follow), your creaite folder's git history and the flow history, with a manifest. If your projects live in OneDrive the zip is copied there (`_Portfolio/99 System/creaite backups`, made the first time; written whole so OneDrive never syncs half a file). That zip is the only thing besides your project files that leaves the laptop. If OneDrive can't be reached nothing stops: the copy is made the next day it can be, and Health warns after two days. To rebuild a creaite folder, use **Settings › Health › Restore from a backup** (choose a backup, **Preview** shows what it will replace, then **Restore**), or say **restore creaite from backup** in Claude Code. Either keeps the current database and every file it replaces first, and never touches a project file.
- **Old backups tidy themselves away** once a day: every daily backup for 30 days, then the Monday's for a year, then the first of each month for good; save and undo copies 14 days; dashboard snapshots 30 days, then one a month; the installed release and the two before it; the last three update backups; file-change backups 90 days. Only creaite's own files, never the newest of each, and it can't be undone. The numbers are in Settings › Advanced › Archiving and backups. Once a week creaite checks it could restore: the newest backup and zip open, match their manifest and their history reads. Settings › Health shows the last copy, check and tidy-up. `99 System` is creaite's own folder: it is never listed, searched or read. The live database never leaves the laptop. Doctor shows the last copy's date. If an earlier version put copies in `_Portfolio/creaite backups`, they stay there; doctor tells you when they can be removed.

## Troubleshooting
- Red alert at the top (and a hollow red dot under Settings): start creaite (see creaite and backups). Saves are held until it's back.
- **Reconnect creaite folder** in the top bar: you've opened the dashboard as a file. Open it from creaite address instead, or click it and allow access again.
- Something changed by mistake: Undo in the message, or **undo last save**.
- Something failed: **Settings › Health › Diagnostics** lists the latest errors and has **Copy diagnostics** (a report with no mail, minutes, notes or names). The log itself is the day's log file, one file a day for the last seven days.
- Anything else: say `health check`.
